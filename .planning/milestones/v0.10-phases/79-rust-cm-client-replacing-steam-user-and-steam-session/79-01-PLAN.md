## D-01 decision (Claude's discretion, from spike 085 final results)

**Lease + idle close (RESEARCH Option A), with reconnect inside a lease and no periodic probe.** The signed-in session is leased on demand. Concurrent acquires share one logon, and the session closes 60 s (`AUTH_SESSION_IDLE_GRACE_MS`) after the last release.

While a lease is held, liveness is **probe-on-failure plus a sleep detector**, with no 30 s periodic probe:
- Any job that fails or times out triggers a probe: a PICS access-token job for app 480, with a 10 s timeout.
- If the probe fails, the Connection is rebuilt (discover + connect + refresh-token logon) with 5→10→20→40→60 s backoff, at most 4 attempts and 30 s per attempt. The failed command is then retried once.
- A 5 s monotonic tick compares wall and monotonic clocks. A gap over 5 s marks the session suspect. If `leaseCount > 0`, the session is probed at once and rebuilt if dead.
- The whole command (attempt + probe + reconnect + retry) runs in a spawned task under one `COMMAND_DEADLINE` of 120 s. The control loop never awaits a CM call. This bounds the unexplained 92 s Run A stall.
- `ClientLoggedOff` eresult 34 is terminal for the session: it is dropped, the pending command fails with `replaced`, and nothing reconnects (D-02; 085's ping-pong finding).

Reasons:
- Run C held one session for 2 h 59 m with 358/358 checks ok, no GAP and no kicks. A steady session does not die on its own, and a 10 s probe produced no false positives.
- Every failure 085 observed was externally triggered (a network drop or sleep). Those are caught by a failed call or by the gap detector.
- A periodic probe during a multi-hour depot lease would add one CM job every 30 s and detect nothing earlier that matters. A request-code call that meets a dead socket costs one bounded timeout and a reconnect of about 1.4 s, which retry-once absorbs.
- On macOS, D-14 removes Refresh's signed-in logon whenever Mac Steam is running, so a long-held session buys little.
