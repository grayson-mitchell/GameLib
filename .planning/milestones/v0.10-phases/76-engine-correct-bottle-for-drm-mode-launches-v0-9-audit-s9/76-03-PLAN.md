<objective>
Close the engine-layer half of the 76-VERIFICATION gap (CR-01). A pinned (Wine-Staging) bottle carries
no engine marker, CrossOver's preflight demo-status probe adds a `Timestamp` to its cxbottle.conf, and
inferMarker then reads it as CrossOver, so the DRM switch silently no-ops and persists a wrong
crossover marker. This plan, in src/backend/engine/engineSwitch.ts only:

1. Hardens inferMarker: DXVK-macOS present in the bottle beats a Timestamp (evidence below).
2. Distrusts a `crossover` marker contradicted by DXVK-macOS in the bottle (heals bottles already
   mislabelled by the 76-01 build and by pre-76 TCP launches, including the live UAT bottle).
3. Exports `reconcileBottleEngineMarker` (fix (b): infer-and-persist before any CrossOver probe),
   `recordPinnedBottleEngine` (fix (a): marker at pinned creation) and `recordCrossoverBottleEngine`
   (marker at CrossOver creation, so only legacy bottles ever reach inference), all wired by 76-04
   (D-07a in 76-CONTEXT.md).
4. Adds the VERIFICATION-required test (c) against the REAL engineSwitch on a temp bottle dir.

Evidence for the hardening (decided at planning time from the code):
- DXVK-macOS (`current_dxvk-macOS`) is installed only by non-CrossOver paths: createFallbackBottle
  (bottlePrimitives.ts ~L317), the switch's installNextDxvk (engineSwitch.ts ~L640), and launcher.ts
  ~L1122 autoInstallDxvk, which is gated on `gameSettings.wineVersion.type === 'wine'` (never crossover).
- Every switch to CrossOver removes it and fails as dxvk-failed if the file survives (engineSwitch.ts
  ~L794), and gameLaunch.ts CR-03 refuses to start CrossOver over DXVK-macOS DLLs. So a bottle whose
  true engine is CrossOver never carries the file; the file is positive evidence of a non-CrossOver
  engine, whereas Timestamp is written by any CrossOver tool touching the bottle, including the probe.

Inference for a marker-less bottle with neither Timestamp nor DXVK-macOS stays 'pinned' (decided at
planning time, checker warning 2). Evidence, read-only: CrossOver's own template
`/Applications/CrossOver.app/Contents/SharedSupport/CrossOver/share/crossover/bottle_templates/win10_64/CXBT_win10_64.pm`
sets `Timestamp` in `create()` (~L196, `$s->set("Timestamp", $cxconfig->get("CrossOver", "BuildTimestamp", ""))`),
and provisionBridgeBottle creates with `--template win10_64`; a survey of the 30+ bottles on the dev Mac found a
Timestamp in every cxbottle.conf. So a Timestamp-less conf can only come from GameLib's writeCxbottleConf, i.e. a
bottle the built-in engine initialised (a DXVK-less one: a pin without DXVK or a DXVK step that left no file).
Preferring 'crossover' there would risk running CrossOver beside a live Staging wineserver; preferring 'pinned'
costs at most a wineserver kill and a marker write (restoreNeeded is false without DXVK). With 76-04 writing a
crossover marker at CrossOver creation, new CrossOver bottles never reach inference at all.

Purpose: DEBT-01 (ROADMAP goal: a SteamStub title never launches in DRM mode over a Staging-initialised bottle).
Output: engineSwitch.ts changes plus tests. TDD: RED then GREEN.
</objective>
