The bridge-notice debug session (`.planning/debug/resolved/bridge-notice-display-unconfirm.md`) added it, and live-proved it raising a Cmd+H-hidden window (display=confirmed elapsedMs=81). That session also added the `dialogDisplayed` ack, which this plan reuses.
