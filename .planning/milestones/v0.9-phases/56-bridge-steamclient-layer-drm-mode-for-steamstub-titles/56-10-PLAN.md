<objective>
Package GameLib's Steamworks-licensed `lsteamclient` component, built in wave 1 by 56-02 and
56-03, as an on-demand, sha-pinned GitHub release asset (D-18). Carry the licence separation with
it in a component `NOTICE` and a `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` section (D-02).

Four parts:
1. `src/common/steamclientLayerCatalogue.ts`: pure data. It holds the pinned release coordinates,
   the archive sha256 and size, and the sha256, size and licence of each file. It is modelled on
   `src/common/knownFixOverlayCatalogue.ts`.
2. `meta/buildSteamClientLayerArchive.ts`: an operator-run, deterministic archive builder modelled
   on `meta/buildOverlayArchive.ts`. It reads the shared staging dir
   `build/steamclient-layer/steamclient-layer-cx<build>/` plus the committed NOTICE, gates every
   file, and prints `pinned: match` only when the archive equals the catalogue.
3. `drm/steamclientLayerInstall.ts`: the DRM-mode `component` step, modelled on
   `src/backend/knownFixOverlayInstall.ts`. It downloads on first use, verifies against the pins,
   extracts in Node, runs `codesign --remove-signature` on the `.so` under a fake HOME, installs
   atomically into `<appFolder>/steam-bridge/drm/component/<component-id>/`, and returns a typed
   `DrmStepResult`.
4. `native/steam-bridge/steamclient-layer/NOTICE` and the `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` section. This
   plan also adds the `build:drm-native` and `build:steamclient-layer-archive` scripts.

Purpose: research F6 showed that a notarized `.app` cannot carry an unsigned x86_64 Mach-O, and Wine
builtins fail to load once codesigned. D-18 resolves that by moving the component out of the
bundle. D-02 requires the Steamworks licence boundary to be visible in packaging. 56-11 calls the
installer as the third setup step. 56-08 reads `lsteamclient.dll`, `lsteamclient.so` and
`detour-cx<build>.bin` from the component dir, 56-09 reads `lsteamclient.dll` and `steam.exe` from
it, and 56-15 publishes the archive this plan builds.

Output: three TypeScript modules, two jest files, a NOTICE, a THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES section, two
package scripts, and a locally built, gitignored archive whose pins are committed. Nothing is
published here; 56-15 uploads the asset after a human-verify checkpoint.
</objective>
