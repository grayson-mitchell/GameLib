<objective>
G25 cause (a): the G22 early refusal is gated on `isOnline()`, which read `online` at Play in
both UAT 34 runs although DNS for Steam CM and github.com was failing. The operator turns Wi-Fi off
immediately before Play (a real offline-at-Play pattern). In `online_monitor.ts`, after the boot
ping round the status only leaves `online` through the renderer's `connectivity-changed` listener
(fed by the webview's DOM `offline` event in `src/frontend/helpers/onlineMonitor.ts`) or
`set-connectivity-online`; while the status is `online` the backend never re-probes. So the
status is only as fresh as WKWebView's `offline` event, which did not arrive before Play.

Per the gap's direction ("do not rely on the event-driven status alone"), this plan adds a fresh,
bounded check that a caller runs at the moment of an action, and makes a fresh offline finding
push the monitor back into its own re-probe loop. Plan 59-42 wires it into Play.

Signal choice (Claude's discretion): (1) the monitor already says not-online -> offline;
(2) no network interface with a routable address (synchronous `os.networkInterfaces()`; Wi-Fi off
with no Ethernet drops en0's address at once) -> offline; (3) otherwise one `dns.promises.lookup`
of `github.com` (the host the pinned-engine download needs) capped at 1500 ms: failure -> offline,
success -> online, cap elapsed -> unknown, reported as not offline. A timeout is deliberately not
treated as offline so a slow resolver can never refuse a launch.

Output: `connectivityNow.ts` with tests, `recheckConnectivity` in the monitor, diagnosis in the
SUMMARY.
</objective>
