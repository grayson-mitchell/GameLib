<objective>
G26 (UAT 38, major): two launches of Planet Crafter ran concurrently during pre-flight (19:31:35 and
19:31:44). Launch B switched the bottle while launch A's demo-status probe was still running in it;
the DXVK removal failed (`reg delete d3d10core/d3d11`, `wineboot -u` code 1), leaving the marker
`dxvk:"failed"` and the bottle half-switched. `launchBridgeGame()` (games.ts:4835) has no per-appId
in-flight guard, and round 4 widened the pre-flight window.

Default decision taken at planning (the operator did not answer; overridable): a second Play of
the same appId JOINS the in-flight launch (awaits and returns the same promise) instead of being
rejected or silently ignored, and shows a short "already starting" notice through `notify()`, the
same non-modal path the D-06 fallback toast uses. A modal dialog is avoided on purpose: a second
modal queued behind the first is exactly the G28 surface. To override: make the joiner return
`false` without a notice (silent ignore), or drop the notify call; the guard itself stays.

Output: guard + notice in games.ts, catalogue key, tests.
</objective>
