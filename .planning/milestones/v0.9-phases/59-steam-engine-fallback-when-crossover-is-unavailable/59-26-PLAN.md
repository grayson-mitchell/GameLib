## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-59-94 | Tampering | `.gamelib-engine.json` failed-restore rewrite | mitigate | written only through `writeMarker` (tmp + rename) from `prev` as already validated by `readMarker`; only `dxvk` changes, to the literal `'failed'`; no path from the restore result is written |
| T-59-95 | Tampering | source-pinned backup recopy | mitigate | the copy source is the pin's DXVK dir (`pinnedEnginePaths`) or a marker dir already constrained by `isInside(pinnedEngineRoot(), dir)` (T-59-60); the destination is the bottle's own `drive_c/windows` only |
| T-59-96 | Denial of service | launch blocked after a failed restore | accept | blocking is the safe direction (D-03); the dialog says why and the switch is retried on every later launch; the user can still choose an override |
| T-59-97 | Denial of service | stopping an unrelated wineserver | mitigate | the restore no longer spawns a marker wineserver that does not exist, and `killPrevious` keeps its engine-root allowlist |
