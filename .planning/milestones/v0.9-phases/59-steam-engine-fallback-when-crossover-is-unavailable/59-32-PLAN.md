<objective>
Diagnose, then fix, H1: `DXSETUP.exe /silent` (DirectX Jun2010) exits 247 on the pinned
Wine-Staging, so the entry never settles and is retried. It completes on CrossOver bottles
(38400, 648800). Do not presume the fix: Task 1 establishes what 247 means under Staging and
whether the DirectX components actually landed; Task 2 implements the branch the evidence
selects.

Context worth knowing before diagnosing: the step already has a WR-04 back-off (after 3
consecutive failures at one manifest GID the entry is skipped for 72 h, then retried once), so
"retried every launch" is really "three wine spawns, then a CM round trip per launch and a
retry every 72 h, never settled". 247 is -9 as an unsigned byte; in dsetup.h -9 is
`DSETUPERR_INTERNAL`, but treat that as a hypothesis for Task 1 to confirm or refute, not as
the answer.

Output: the diagnosis in 59-32-SUMMARY.md, the fix, tests.
</objective>
