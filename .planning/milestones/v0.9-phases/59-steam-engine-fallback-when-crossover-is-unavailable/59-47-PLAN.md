<objective>
G27 (UAT 38, minor): with CrossOver healthy and the marker's DXVK copy present, the offline restore
waited 12454 ms on the demo-status probe before the switch.

The planning-time default from the orchestrator was "skip the demo-status probe when the fresh
check says offline AND the marker is non-crossover with a present DXVK copy". That default
conflicts with locked decision D-09 and the roadmap goal ("demo-status check before every
CrossOver launch"): with the probe skipped the launch goes to CrossOver unprobed. Per context
fidelity the planner does not override a locked decision, so this plan diagnoses first and puts
the choice to the operator. Planning-time evidence that the premise needs checking: in UAT 38,
launch B's probe ran offline seconds after A's and took 163 ms; the online probe after the same
pinned marker at 19:53 took 5421 ms; UAT 34 run 2's offline probe took 9644 ms. That points at a
cold first CrossOver run on a bottle last run by the pinned engine, with offline adding to it,
not at "offline" alone.

Output: diagnosis, operator decision, and either an override entry or the gated skip with tests.
</objective>
