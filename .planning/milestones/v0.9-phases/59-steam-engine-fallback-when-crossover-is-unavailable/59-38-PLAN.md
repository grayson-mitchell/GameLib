the pinned engine moved aside, the launch-blocked dialog arrived about 10 s late
(elapsedMs=9671): `decideLaunchEngine` runs the CrossOver `--demo-status` probe first, and only
afterwards does the restore discover it needs the absent pinned engine and cannot download it
offline.

Task 1 diagnoses before changing anything: where the time goes, and whether, from that state,
every branch of the decision needs the pinned engine (healthy CrossOver -> restore needs the pin
per 59-26; unhealthy CrossOver -> fallback needs the pin). If it does, the outcome is decided
before the probe can matter, so refusing early does not weaken D-09 ("pre-flight before every
CrossOver launch"): no CrossOver launch happens either way. Task 2 implements that early refusal
only if the diagnosis confirms it; otherwise it records why the wait is the probe D-09 requires
and changes no launch code.

`gamelib:steam.engine.downloadFailed` text with reason `offline` ("GameLib couldn't download its
built-in engine (offline). Check your connection and try again."). It is true in both branches,
whereas the restore-failed text presumes CrossOver is healthy, which the early path has not
checked. No new string.
