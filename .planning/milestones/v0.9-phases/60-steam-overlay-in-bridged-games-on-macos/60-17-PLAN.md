<objective>
Second half of the G1 instrument (runs in parallel with 60-15). Extend the 60-11 probe dylib with
(1) the open/close signal observers 60-12 lacked and a check on whether the probe can see renderer
calls at all, and (2) the user-approved release-on-open experiment, opt-in behind
GAMELIB_OVERLAY_RELEASE_EXPERIMENT (values `cb:release`, `cb:release+block`, `key:release`; 60-15
validates and passes them).

60-12 facts this builds on (all three runs, both engines): in gameplay winemac.so issues, once,
`CGWarpMouseCursorPosition`, `CGAssociateMouseAndMouseCursorPosition arg=1`, `NSCursor-hide` and
`setMouseConfinementRect 1x1`, and holds them until gameplay capture ends (rect 0x0 + unhide). The
overlay opening never releases them. No `caller=gameoverlayrenderer.dylib` line was ever seen. The
renderer carries two `__interpose` sections (15 pairs) and imports CGAssociateMouseAndMouseCursorPosition,
CGDisplayHideCursor, CGDisplayShowCursor, CGSForceShowCursor, CGWarpMouseCursorPosition, HideCursor,
ShowCursor and NSCursor.

Note on the 60-12 log: `CGAssociateMouseAndMouseCursorPosition arg=1` was logged at capture start
alongside the 1x1 confine. In CoreGraphics `true` (1) means mouse and cursor are associated
(normal), so the capture is the 1x1 confinement plus the hidden cursor, not a dissociation; the gap
brief's "dissociated" reading should not be carried forward. The experiment does not depend on
either reading: on open it calls associate(1) itself, and on close it replays whatever winemac last
asked for.

Purpose: 60-18 runs this live to find which signal really marks the overlay opening in gameplay and
whether releasing the capture makes the overlay usable. That verdict decides the fix (60-19/60-20).
Output: the extended probe dylib, the build argv change if new frameworks are needed, and smoke tests.
</objective>
