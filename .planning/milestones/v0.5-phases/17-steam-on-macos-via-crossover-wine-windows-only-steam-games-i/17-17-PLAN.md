- WR-01 — WARNING: `SteamGame.install()` starts the bottle ACF poller unconditionally even when `tellBottledSteamToInstall` returns an error, producing ~60s of false "installing" state.
- WR-02 — WARNING: `SteamBottleConfig.loggedIn` is declared/surfaced (via `steamBottleStatus`) but never written anywhere in the backend, so it always reports `false` — a dead always-false signal.

Purpose: make the guided setup incapable of touching the shared bottle (defense at the authoritative backend chokepoint AND the reachable UI), and remove two dishonest bottle surfaces (false "installing", always-false `loggedIn`).
