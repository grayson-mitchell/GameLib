<objective>
On macOS and Linux, Play on a Steam game currently goes Play → (brief Launching) → Play → Playing.
The click latch shows Launching only until the `launch` IPC resolves. rungameid makes that
almost immediate, so the button drops back to Play (the flicker), and Playing arrives about 2 s
later when the watch's ps scan finds the game. After this plan the button holds Launching from
the click until the watch finds the game, then shows Playing/Stop. If the game never starts
within the watch's 90 s window, the watch sends `done` and the button returns to Play.

This extends quick 261111-am0's win32 Launching hold to posix inside the same watch. It adds one
ordering guarantee: the launch IPC waits, with a bound, for the watch's `launching` decision.
Without that wait, the async Rust `pollerReports` read lets the IPC resolve first and the Play
flash survives.

Purpose: one launcher that shows the truth about what it launched, from the click onward, on every OS.
Output: the extended watch, its updated and new tests, the awaited watch start in both rungameid
callers, and a pending post-fix live check.
</objective>

<decisions>
**From the orchestrator brief.**
- **B-01** Hold Launching on posix by reusing the am0 win32 contract as closely as the posix loop allows.
- **B-02** Send no `launching` when `pollerReports(appId)` is already true, and never after cancel, `playing`, `done` or the run's end.
- **B-03** Once `launching` has been sent, every posix exit ends with exactly one terminal frame. No card may be left on Launching.
- **B-04** Keep the changes in `steamLaunchWatch.ts` where possible. Update the posix tests whose expected `sent` arrays change without weakening what they prove. Add the listed cases.

**Planner decisions.**
- **P-01 Root cause, confirmed.** In `steamLaunchWatch.ts` (lines 189-204), `launching` is decided inside an `if (win)` block only. On posix the frontend latch is released in a `finally` when the IPC resolves. That happens in `GamePage/index.tsx:757-769` and `GameCard/index.tsx:784-798`, and `gameLaunch.ts:202-204` returns right after `openExternal`. `runPosix` sends `playing` only from its 2 s scan (lines 250-256). Its start-window exit (265-268), the no-folder exit in `run()` (365-368) and the `.catch` send nothing. The `.finally` fallback (line 383) is gated on `win`.
- **P-02 Posix `launching` comes from the existing decision, not a second mechanism.** Drop the `if (win)` gate around `decideLaunching` (lines 189-204). Posix takes no poller claim, so `releasePoller` is undefined and `held` is absent. The decision then runs at once: in the same turn when `pollerReports` is synchronous (the tests), otherwise once its promise resolves. The guards `reports || cancelled || doneSent || playingSent || finished` stay as they are and cover B-02. `entry.shown = true` on the send stays as well, so it now applies on posix too, which is what P-07 needs.
- **P-03 The launch IPC must wait for the decision. This is the one change outside the watch.**
  - In production `pollerReports` is always async: `rustPollerReports` in `steamLaunchedDeps.ts:104-117` is a `requestRustInvoke` round trip.
  - `gameLaunch.ts` returns `true` in the same turn that `watchSteamLaunch` starts the watch, and `dispatchSteamLaunch` then replies to the IPC. So the IPC result frame goes out before the `launching` frame, and the latch drops onto Play for at least one render. That is the user's "flicker".
  - Fix: `startSteamLaunchWatch` returns `launchingDecided: Promise<void>`. It settles when the decision has sent `launching` or skipped it, when the watch is cancelled or finishes, or after `decideWaitMs` (option, default 1000), whichever comes first. It never rejects. `watchSteamLaunch` returns it, and both rungameid callers `await` it before they return.
  - The status frame is then written to the sidecar's stdout before the IPC result frame. That is the same ordering assumption am0 made with its same-turn send.
  - This also restores am0's guarantee on win32. Phase 80's async claim (80-REVIEW C-WR-06) deferred the win32 decision past the IPC result.
  - `gameLaunch.ts` is now 889 lines, no longer 995 as it was at am0 time, so a one-word change fits.
- **P-04 Posix detection no longer reads `entry.shown`.** Once the `launching` send sets `shown`, `runPosix` must not use it as "game detected". Its four reads of `entry.shown` become reads of the existing `playingSent`: the scan-failure bail (237), the first-detection branch (250), the gone count (259) and the start-window check (265). The detection branch sets both `playingSent = true` and `entry.shown = true` where it sends `playing`. The posix `done` sends at lines 241 and 261 go through `sendDone()`, so `doneSent` is accurate for the fallback.
- **P-05 The terminal fallback becomes platform-neutral.** Remove `win &&` from the `.finally` condition at line 383. Inside the `whenPollerReports` callback:
  - When the poller does not report the appId, send `done` as today.
  - When it does report it and the platform is not win32, log `appId <id>: the RunningAppID poller reports it running; ending Launching as Playing` and send `playing`.
  - win32 keeps sending nothing in that branch, because release(false) has just made the poller republish `playing`.
  - Why posix needs the `playing`: posix holds no claim. The Rust poller emits `playing` only on a RunningAppID transition for an unclaimed appId (`src-tauri/crates/gamelib-steampoll/src/running.rs:352-369`). On Linux that emit can predate the watch's `launching`. On macOS the poller is registry-only and never reports (`running.rs:189-192`), so this branch is Linux-only in practice.
  - This one fallback covers `runPosix`'s `if (reports) return` hand-back. There is no extra send inside the loop.
- **P-06 Relaunch during Launching re-sends nothing. This departs from the brief, on evidence.**
  - `noteRelaunch` (lines 169-172) sets `reassert` from `entry.shown`. After P-02, `shown` is true during posix Launching, so the posix loop (227-230) would send a false `playing` on its next tick. Gate `reassert` on `playingSent` instead.
  - That gate is behaviour-identical on win32. There `reassert` was set during Launching but never consumed, and it was cleared where the search sent `playing` (am0 P-05).
  - The brief also asked for a fresh `launching` on relaunch-before-shown, because "the latch release would otherwise drop to Play again". That premise does not hold:
    - `libraryState.ts:327-334` keeps the current status, and a duplicate status is a no-op.
    - `launching` is held until `done`/`error`.
    - The frontend `launch` helper writes no status.
    - `MainButton.tsx:44-58` and `GameCard/constants.ts:40-57` disable Play while launching, so a relaunch during Launching arrives only by deep link or tray.
  - The status is therefore still `launching` when any latch drops. Not re-sending keeps posix identical to am0's win32 A3 truth ("no second `launching`"). P7 pins it.
- **P-07 Stop during posix Launching needs no `games.ts` change.** `SteamGame.stop()` (`games.ts:683-702`) sends one `done` when `cancelSteamLaunchWatch` reports `shown`, and P-02 sets `shown` on the `launching` send. A cancelled watch sends no fallback (`!cancelled`). G3 already pins the `games.ts` side.
- **P-08 `whenPollerReports` treats a synchronous throw from `pollerReports` as `false`.** The posix decision now runs inside `startSteamLaunchWatch` after `deps.registry.set`. A throw there would escape after the entry was registered, leaving it stranded, and every later Play would return `already-watching` forever. Production's async function cannot throw synchronously, but the type allows a boolean-returning implementation. A throw from the loop's own read (line 225) already lands in `run()`'s `.catch`, then the fallback (`done`).
- **P-09 Known trade-off, mirroring am0 L-01. Flag it to the user and do not engineer around it.**
  - A posix Play whose game never starts now shows a disabled Launching button until the 90 s start window ends, then Play. Before, it dropped back to Play at once.
  - A Steam cold start plus game start that takes longer than 90 s gets Launching → Play, and no Playing later on macOS, where the poller is silent. The watch already ended at 90 s today, so this part is unchanged.
- **P-10 Scope fences.**
  - Unchanged: every frontend file, `games.ts`, `runningPoll`/the Rust poller, `stopSteamLaunchedGame.ts`, `runningFromFolder.ts`.
  - The win32 wire is unchanged: W1-W7, A1-A8, A3b and the four C-WR tests pass with their bodies unedited. W8 is a darwin no-folder test: it keeps its pollerHold assertions and only its `sent` expectation changes.
  - No new interval or probe. The single new timer is the `decideWaitMs` bound. It comes from the injected clock with no `keepAlive`, so it is unref'd (sidecar exit contract, half 1), it is armed only while a decision is pending, and it is cleared the moment the decision lands. It is never armed at boot, so half 2 of the contract is unaffected.
</decisions>
