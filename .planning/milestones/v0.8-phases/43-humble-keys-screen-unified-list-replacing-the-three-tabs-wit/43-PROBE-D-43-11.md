## ADDENDUM 2026-09-21 — operator-confirmed post-link behaviour, and why this probe can never be re-run

Reported by the operator after acting on the 2026-09-18 finding above. These are observations of
Humble's own UI and email, not GameLib measurements.

1. **Linking GOG to Humble is a ONE-OFF**, confirmed by a linking-confirmation email.
2. **Linking auto-claims the pending backlog.** Humble's own pre-link message stated that on
   linking, **Racine would be claimed automatically**.
3. **After linking, a newly purchased GOG game offers "reveal your GOG key".**
4. **Humble offers NO unlink for GOG.** (It does offer unlink for Steam.)

### Consequence 1 — candidate A's denial is explained, and the question changes shape

Fact 3 is the important one. Post-link entitlements are **not** silently auto-granted forever;
they present a **reveal** step. That is the keyed flow, and it makes the unlinked-state denial
this probe measured almost certainly a missing-grantee error, exactly as the supersede block
above reasoned.

### Consequence 2 — the likely answer is that GameLib ALREADY supports this, with no new mechanism

"Reveal your GOG key" implies a key code exists. That points at `key_type: gog`, not
`gog_keyless` — and the repo already handles keyed GOG end to end:

- `classify.ts:174-181` retains `gog` as "the evidenced value for a real keyed GOG key" and
  documents `gog_keyless` as a *separately*-evidenced value for the direct-redeem shape "(no key
  code; Humble redeems straight to the linked GOG account)".
- `keyTypePresentation.ts:131` already ships
  `gog: (code) => https://www.gog.com/redeem/${code}` in `REDEEM_URL_BUILDERS`.

**Working hypothesis, not yet measured: `gog_keyless` is the UNLINKED-account shape.** Once
linked, GOG entitlements arrive keyed, travel the existing reveal-then-redeem path, and candidate
A is not needed — not because it fails, but because the entitlement never takes the keyless form.
Under that reading, 43-09's embedded-browser path is the correct affordance specifically for
**unlinked** users, which is a narrower and more defensible scope than the one it shipped with.

### Consequence 3 — this probe is probably unrepeatable, on ONE observation. Re-check before relying on it.

Fact 4 looks like a one-way door: with no GOG unlink on Humble the account cannot be returned to
the unlinked state, and it is the only account available.

**CAVEAT, raised by the operator and NOT yet resolved (2026-09-21): the absence may be a cooldown,
not a permanent condition.** Account-link UIs commonly withhold unlink for a period after linking.
The observation is **one look, on the same day as the link**. An earlier draft of this section
asserted permanent irreversibility outright and called it "structural" — that was an overclaim
from a single data point, made in the same breath as correcting a different overclaim.

**Re-check the Humble account settings page on or after 2026-09-22** and record what is there. If
unlink appears, the unlinked state is recoverable and a controlled re-test becomes possible again
(at the cost of re-linking afterwards). If it is still absent after several days, the one-way-door
reading firms up.

Until that re-check: treat unlinked-state behaviour as *probably* unobservable again on this
account, and do not build a plan that depends on re-entering it without confirming first.
