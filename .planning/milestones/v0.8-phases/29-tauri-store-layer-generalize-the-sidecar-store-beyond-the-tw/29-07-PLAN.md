        caches once, so making `steamBottleConfigStore` live would have armed a silent
        clobber. Fixed with the path-keyed shared cell. In-process only — the
        cross-process case remains D-07.

        store read async and cross-process, forcing a refactor of every synchronous
        module-scope `.get()` caller. Rust's role stays "the platform seam" (keyring,
        opener), not "the database". Do not re-litigate.
