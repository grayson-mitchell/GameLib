ASVS V5 control for this channel and it is **ported, not rewritten**.

path proven by test.

on failure; `steamBottleProvision` downloads a real CrossOver bottle. Both stay unit-proven and get a
declared row in `34.4-PORTED-CHANNELS.md` (plan 34.4-09). Phase 26's 5 deferred live-key UAT items
stay deferred and are NOT folded in.
