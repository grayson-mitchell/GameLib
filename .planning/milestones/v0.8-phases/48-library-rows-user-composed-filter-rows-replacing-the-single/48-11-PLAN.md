<objective>
Close the two theme-contrast gaps with fixes whose ratio is a property of the theme, not of the
artwork, and prove them at the desk over all 10 themes before the live re-measure.

**G-48-4a (chevron, major).** The 55% `color-mix` scrim measured 3:1 in 12 of 40 theme x card x edge
combinations, minimum 1.005 in gruvbox_dark (UAT item 4). A flat alpha over arbitrary art cannot
bound the ratio: bright art behind the scrim leaves a mid grey that any mid-luminance accent fails
against. The gap entry's own `missing` list offers "an opaque disc". With an opaque
`var(--body-background)` disc the glyph's only neighbour inside the circle is the theme's body
colour, and `--accent` on `--body-background` resolves to at least 3.91:1 in every theme (desk
census, this session; the same resolver reproduces UAT item 7's live divider numbers within 0.02).
**Deliberate deviation from 48-UI-SPEC** ("semi-transparent dark scrim"): that contract's purpose
was "stays legible over any artwork", which only an opaque disc can guarantee. Icon colour stays
`var(--accent)` as the UI-SPEC requires.

**G-48-7 (divider, minor).** `.FilterFocusRow__divider` paints `--text-secondary` on the panel's
`--navbar-background`: dracula 4.25 live (UAT item 7) and 4.27 desk (`#b1b1b1` on `#44475a`), and nord-light 1.52 live and desk (`#393b41` on
`#4c566a`, the only light theme, whose dark text token sits on a dark navbar). Fix: a nested
`body.dracula &, body.nord-light &` override painting the tier-2 rows' own colour chain
`var(--navbar-inactive, var(--navbar-accent))` (7.48, 7.38). Rejected alternatives, measured:
the row chain on all 10 themes would recolour 8 passing themes' dividers (to accent hues in
cyberSpaceOasis, marine and zombie) and erase the UI-SPEC's "lighter than the rows" hierarchy
where it already works; `--navbar-accent` alone still fails nord-light (2.74); `--text-default`
fails nord-light (2.11); a theme-level `--text-secondary` change would break nord-light's dark
body text. Precedent for a measured per-theme nested override: `NavTabs/index.scss` `body.nord-light &`
(quick `260823-w2f`) and the dracula-only tier-2 divider (34.11-09).

**Verification hook.** `48-UAT.md` item 10 is the re-measure gate for both entries; it stays the
live proof. Task 3 unblocks it (`blocked` -> `pending`) and logs the desk numbers it should
reproduce.

**Depends on 48-10**: both plans edit `FocusRowStrip/index.css` and `focusRowStripSource.test.ts`.

Purpose: the chevrons and the divider labels are legible in every offered theme, provably.
Output: one CSS and one SCSS edit, two censuses, one updated source gate, two closed todos, an
unblocked UAT item.
