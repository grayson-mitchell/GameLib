<objective>
Close G-48-4b and G-48-9, the two gaps this plan owns (a hovered edge card
paints over the chevron; the operator saw it with a real mouse on Windows), and fix the strip-end
clipping that G-48-8a lists as its third missing item and G-48-8b says the thicker hover ring will
make worse. **Gap ownership:** G-48-8a and G-48-8b are owned by 48-09 alone (its `gap_ids`), because 48-09 closes their substance (no controller-mode resize; the hover ring); this plan only pairs with them, so they are deliberately absent from this plan's `gap_ids` and one plan owns each gap.

**Stacking fix, and why not "a z-index above 3".** `.focusRowStrip__control` is z-index 1;
`.gameCard:hover` is z-index 2 with `scale(1.05)`, and the card is a flex item of the track, which
forms no stacking context, so they compete in one context and the card wins. Raising the control's
number alone is a race against an open-ended set: at rest a card has no stacking context of its
own, so its children's z-indexes (title 3, icons bar 4, status and update badges 5, all in
`GameCard/index.css`) also land in that shared context. `isolation: isolate` on `.focusRowTrack`
contains every card-internal z-index inside the track, and the track then paints at the z-index 0
layer, below both z-index 1 controls, whatever any card does. The gap entry offered this route
("or a stacking context").

**Clipping fix.** `.focusRowTrack .gameList` has `padding: 0`, so the first card sits flush with the
track's left edge and the last flush with its right edge at the end of travel; the debug session
measured the first card's scaled body and its ring cut off at x 12.1 against the track's 16. With
48-09's 3px ring at +2px on hover too, the ring reaches about 9.15px past the unscaled card edge
((78 + 5) x 1.05 - 78). This plan pads the list 12px inline and bleeds the viewport out 12px the
other way, so the cards do not move relative to the grid (`.focusRowStrip` keeps its 16px padding,
UI-SPEC Spacing). The list gets `width: max-content` because padding on a block-width flex list
whose items overflow it does not reliably extend the track's scroll width in both engines this app
ships on (WKWebView on macOS, WebView2 on Windows); a max-content list carries its own padding
inside its border box, which every engine counts. The vertical axis already has room: the list's
16px block margin against about 11.5px of ring reach (debug session, Eliminated).

**Scope.** Strip-only by nature (these are the strip's own container rules). The global card
changes are 48-09; the chevron's contrast is 48-11, which depends on this plan because both edit
`FocusRowStrip/index.css` and its source gate.

Purpose: the chevrons stay on top and clickable, and the strip never cuts a card's ring in half.
Output: edited `index.css` and `focusRowOverflow.ts`, with their two gates extended.
</objective>
