<objective>
Close UAT gap **G-48-11b** (major): from a fresh launch with 14 cards the focus-row strip pages past
its last card into blank, and the forward control never disables.

**The live signature, verbatim from 48-UAT.md item 11.** 14 sideloaded fixture cards, solid-grey
data-URI art, `focusRow` all/view, midnightMirage, 1280x800, reproduced on two launches. Forward
click 1 shows cards 5-9. Click 2 shows cards 10-13 and one EMPTY slot. Clicks 3 and 4 show a
completely blank strip, and the forward chevron stays enabled (full glyph colour) throughout.

**What the signature already proves, before any tool runs.** A blank viewport at `scrollLeft` s
with s still at or below `scrollWidth - clientWidth` means the track's scroll extent runs past the
last card. A stale `measure`, an inflated pitch or a wrong page delta can each explain the chevron
staying enabled or a page landing off-grid. None of them can make the browser scroll into empty
space, because the browser clamps `scrollLeft` to the real extent. So the investigation measures the
scroll extent first: `scrollWidth` against E = N x w + (N - 1) x 24 + 30.

**The cause is NOT known, and this plan does not guess it.** Task 1 reproduces the signature at the
desk in a real engine and isolates the cause with a counterfactual. Only then does Task 2 fix it,
red gate first. The orchestrator's triage calls this probably a 48-12 regression. The bisect window
is wider than that. Item 5 measured a correct end of travel on 2026-10-07. That was a 20-card strip
at 156px, with `scrollWidth` 3576 = 20 x 180 - 24 exactly. 48-10 (`78cd69511`, 2026-10-08) and
48-12 both landed after that measurement. 48-10 is the commit that added `width: max-content` and
`padding-inline` to `.focusRowTrack .gameList`. Candidate hypotheses are listed in Task 1. Each is a
lead to test, not a finding.

**Why the desk needs a real engine, not just jsdom.** The Frontend jest project is
`testEnvironment: 'node'`, with no CSS engine, and jsdom reports every rect as zero. A stubbed-DOM
arithmetic walk is still written, as one discriminator: it clears or convicts `pageScrollDelta` and
`canScrollForward` over the true geometry. But it cannot see a CSS cause. The real-engine recipe on
this machine is in
`/Users/graysonmitchell/.claude/projects/-Users-graysonmitchell-Projects-GameLib/memory/real-engine-dom-probe-at-the-desk.md`.
The headless Chromium shell stands in for Windows' WebView2. The live failure was measured in
macOS WKWebView, so a compiled WKWebView probe is the primary engine.

**Shared-code check (done at plan time).** `focusRowOverflow.ts` and every `.focusRowTrack`
selector have no consumer outside `FocusRowStrip/` (`git grep` over `src`, 2026-10-08). Several
