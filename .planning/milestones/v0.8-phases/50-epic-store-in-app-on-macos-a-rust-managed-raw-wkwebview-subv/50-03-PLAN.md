their English strings: the D-07 slow-load banner, the D-10 `'epic-challenged'`
`WebviewUnavailablePanel` reason with its Try again button, and the D-14 blocked-launcher-scheme

</objective>

<ui_spec_amendment_surfaced>
**UI-SPEC A3 (the D-14 notice's placement) conflicts with a measured compositor constraint, and
Task 1's checkpoint is where it gets ruled on — this plan does not decide it silently.**

UI-SPEC A3 assumes the D-14 notice follows `HumbleExpiryToast`'s fixed-position floating shape.
`Skill("spike-findings-gamelib")` records the measurement that makes that unshippable on this
screen: **overlay UI cannot render above the embed — it is a native subview, so it must be hidden
first.** `HumbleExpiryToast` itself proves the point, because it calls
`useSuppressStoreEmbedWhile(visible)` precisely so the embed gets out of its way. Doing that here
would make the Epic store vanish for the duration of a transient notice about a blocked link,
which is a worse outcome than the notice.

The recommended resolution, which Task 1's `checkpoint:decision` presents: the D-14 notice renders
as a row in the SAME notice stack under `StoreEmbedControls` as the D-07 banner — a region the
slot rect excludes by construction (Phase 40 D-24: the chrome renders above the slot and the
slot's measured rect never includes it). Copy, colour token and icon are unchanged from A3; only
the placement moves, which A3 itself rated medium-risk and UI-SPEC A5 explicitly left to the
planner. The two E5 backstop truths above are therefore authored against the bounded-width
notice-row geometry rather than a floating fixed-position element.
</ui_spec_amendment_surfaced>
