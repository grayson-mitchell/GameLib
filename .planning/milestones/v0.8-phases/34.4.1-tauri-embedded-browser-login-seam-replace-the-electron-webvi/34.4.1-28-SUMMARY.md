## Three vacuity failures found in this plan's own script

All three were caught by checking output against known ground truth, **none by reading the code** —
which is the transferable lesson.

1. **Bare-substring live matching classified every row `VERIFIED-LIVE`.** "cookie" appears in
   nearly every document in this phase. The instrument was vacuous on its first run.
2. **Tightening to `method(` then MISSED F-6's own real observation** — `34.4.1-LIVE-GATE.md`
   writes it as `` `delete_cookie` `` with no parens. Fixed by accepting either form, both being
   deliberate code references rather than prose.
3. **String-literal interiors counted as call sites**, so `logError('queryLocalFonts threw')`
   became a fake UNGUARDED hit. Hit detection now blanks string interiors while
   `strip_ts_comments` keeps its WR-08 string-preserving behaviour, which its own case pins.

Ground truth now reads correctly: `delete_cookie`/`cookies` VERIFIED-LIVE, `build()` UNVERIFIED-LIVE.

## Two things the plan and research had wrong

- **The research's S-11 diagnosis was incomplete.** The docblock was not merely id-less — once the
  id was added it was **not being read at all**. `extract_file_header_comment()` requires the whole
  comment inside a **3000-character** window and the addition pushed its closing `*/` to character
  3149. Moving the docblock above the imports gives it the full window at 2931. **The window was
  not widened**; loosening the instrument to fit prose would defeat its purpose. ~70 characters of
  headroom remain, recorded in the docblock itself.
- **The plan named two cross-reference files that do not exist** —
  `references/tauri-chromium-only-web-apis.md` and `references/navigator-clipboard-noops-under-tauri`.
  The skill's `references/` holds four other files. The script walks the whole tree instead. (Note:
  `queryLocalFontsSafe.ts`'s own docblock also cites the first of these non-existent paths — a
  pre-existing broken reference, left alone as out of scope.)
