# Phase 34.4.1 Plan 08: Run the Blocking Live Gate — Summary

**The Tauri Humble login mechanism works end-to-end on real hardware — and the gate that proved it also caught two blocking defects a fully-green 3279/3279 suite could not see, so the phase does not close.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~50 min wall clock (operator-driven; gate items run interactively)
- **Started:** 2026-07-30 ~17:36 local
- **Completed:** 2026-07-30 ~18:21 local
- **Tasks:** 3 of 3 (Task 1 completed 2026-07-29; Tasks 2–3 completed 2026-07-30)
- **Files modified:** 3

## Accomplishments

- **Ran all four gate items to completion.** Verdict: **FAIL, 2 of 4 clean.** Items 2 and 4 pass; item 1 passes its mechanism but violates its own evidence requirement; item 3 fails.
- **Proved the headline claim.** A Humble session was created **from scratch** under `pnpm tauri:dev` from an absent credential store, survived a genuine quit-and-relaunch at the persistence layer, and drove a **successful key reveal** through the in-webview POST. 34.4 D-09's standing statement is falsified and struck.
- **Confirmed D-07's central bet live.** The reveal returned a real key, not the 403 D-07 explicitly permitted — a genuine WKWebView network stack clears Cloudflare where axios could not, exactly as rounds 1–6 of `humble-reveal-key-fails` predicted.
- **Found two blocking defects with one shared root shape** — an Electron capability silently dropped at the Tauri seam, invisible to the automated suite because the suite asserts that ported code compiles and its units pass, never what the sidecar does at runtime.

The plaintext branch is the only reachable branch. Structural, not a denied Keychain prompt. Phase 28 migrated Steam to `TokenStore`; Humble was never migrated. |

## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` frontmatter. The two that most shaped the outcome:

- **Continue the gate after the first blocking defect.** Once item 1 was non-clean and 3(b) was pre-recorded unobservable, 4/4 was already unreachable. Running items 2–4 anyway produced item 4's PASS (which proves `humbleRevealKey` ships proven rather than declared-degraded) and item 2's PASS — both of which would have been lost by stopping early, and both of which reduce the gap cycle's scope.
- **Do not update `IPC-PORT-INVENTORY.md`.** That edit was the one change plan 07 deliberately left for this plan, gated on item 3 passing. Item 3 failed. Marking a declared-partial "closed" on the strength of unit tests while the live behavior is broken is precisely the failure this gate exists to prevent.
