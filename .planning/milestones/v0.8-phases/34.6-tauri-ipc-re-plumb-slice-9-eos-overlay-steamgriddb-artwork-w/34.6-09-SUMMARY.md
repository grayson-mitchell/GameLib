## Decisions Made

- **Amendment A-03 is binding and confirmed in code, not just by convention:** the SteamGridDB
  API-key channels are the one deliberate exception to this phase's default D-02 "port
  byte-equivalently" rule. `hasApiKey` becomes async (transport-transparent for an invoke
  channel).
- **`getGogDiscounts` required a logic extraction** (`discounts/fetchDiscounts.ts`) rather than a
  direct re-export, because the sidecar registration module must curated-import the underlying
  logic (D-04), never a feature's own `addHandler`-registering `index.ts`.
- **The sidecar's `steamgridSecretStore.ts` migration is a distinct, already-correct codepath,
  not a third instance of the branch this plan eliminates** — it migrates plaintext to the
  KEYRING (sidecar-only concern, landed by plan 34.6-02), while `secretStore.ts`'s surviving
  branch migrates plaintext to the Electron `encrypted-config` format. Both are legitimate and
  distinct; only `ipc_handler.ts`'s duplicate of the latter was in scope for deletion.
- **`WIRING_GUARD_MODULES`** was introduced as a narrower module list for Describe 2's
  "imported but never called exactly twice" assertion, because `handlers.ts`'s own docstring
  prose mentions `registerEnrichmentFlows` a third time — a false positive for that specific
  wiring guard, unrelated to actual wiring correctness (which is covered by other tests).

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - blocking issue] `getGogDiscounts` reaches an Electron API the plan assumed it didn't**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** The plan's draft assumed `getGogDiscounts` had no Electron dependency and could be
  imported directly from `discounts/index.ts`. In fact its body calls `app.getVersion()` from
  `electron`, and `discounts/index.ts` self-registers via `addHandler` (which itself imports real
  `electron`) — importing it directly into the sidecar's curated registration module would violate
  D-04's curated-import discipline.

  `getGogDiscounts(locale, hideOwned, wishlistOnly)` function. `discounts/index.ts` was rewritten
  as a thin `addHandler` wrapper importing from `./fetchDiscounts`, and
  `enrichmentFlowRegistration.ts` imports from the same new module.

  `src/backend/discounts/index.ts`, `src/backend/sidecar/enrichmentFlowRegistration.ts`

  `electronReachLedger.test.ts` correctly attributes the new electron-reach edge to
