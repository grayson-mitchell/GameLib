<objective>
Land the FIRST half of A-03: the SteamGridDB API-key storage seam and its Rust keyring slot,
BEFORE any SteamGridDB channel is registered on the sidecar (plan 34.6-09).

A-03 is an explicit, operator-approved exception to D-02's port-byte-equivalently-then-harden
rule, taken for this channel only. D-02's rule is UNCHANGED for its own three channels
(`importGame`, `moveInstall`, `runWineCommandForGame`), which are handled by plans 34.6-06/07/11.

Why it must land first: `src/backend/steamgrid/secureKey.ts:25` imports `safeStorage` from
`electron`. Under the sidecar that resolves to `src/backend/sidecar/electronStub.ts`'s deliberately
dead stub, `encryptionAvailable()` catches to `false`, and `encryptApiKey()` takes its plaintext
branch — so `GlobalConfig.setSetting('steamGridDbApiKey', ...)` writes the key in the clear.
D-06's live gate enters a REAL SteamGridDB API key through the settings field, so the gate step
itself would be the moment of exposure. `secureKey.ts:17-24` already states this TRIGGER CONDITION
in the repo and instructs that the keyring slot land "BEFORE porting the channel -- not after".

Purpose: close the information-disclosure hazard at its source before the channel that triggers it
exists.
Output: a Rust allowlist arm with unit tests, a slot constant, and a swappable
`SteamGridDbSecretStore` seam whose Electron implementation is behaviourally identical to today.
</objective>
