<the_step_8_problem>

**This plan cannot produce `PASS n/n`, and no amount of careful execution changes that.**

Step 8 scored FAIL: Epic logout reports clearing 8 cookies while clearing zero of the 7 in-scope
`epicgames.com` cookies, all of which survive with full values including a 219-byte
`EPIC_SESSION_AP`. Under D-12 — one document, one verdict, no partial-pass mode — a single FAIL
forces the document's verdict to FAIL.

The gap cycle deliberately excluded Step 8. The reasoning is sound and is recorded in run 1's own
closing statement §2: it is a shell/wry-layer defect on a channel **34.6 never ported**, so fixing
it inside an IPC re-plumb phase would be scope creep, and it is already filed and owned as
`2026-08-24-epic-logout-reports-clearing-cookies-it-does-not-clear.md`.

But Step 8 was not an accident of the contract either. It is one of **four inherited verification
legs deliberately re-homed from the parked Phase 34.7** — it was put in this gate on purpose.

So exactly one of three things must happen, and **it is the operator's call, not this plan's**:

  **(a) Fix it.** Widen the gap cycle to cover the wry cookie-clear defect, then re-drive Step 8.
       Honest, and closes the phase green. Costs an unbudgeted shell-layer investigation.
  **(b) Re-scope it, explicitly.** A seventh SUPERSEDES amendment declaring Step 8 out of scope for
       34.6's VERDICT — scoring a channel this phase never ported — and carrying it forward as a
       named, owned gate item on the phase that does own that surface. **This is the dangerous
       option**: amending a contract so a failing item stops counting is exactly the move a
       rigorous project must not make casually. It is defensible ONLY because run 1 independently
       reached the same scoping conclusion on the evidence, BEFORE any verdict pressure existed.
  **(c) Close the phase FAIL.** Record `FAIL 9/10, 1 FAIL (Step 8)` and let 34.6 close with a known,
       named, owned failure rather than manufacturing a green.

**Task 3 must BLOCK on this decision and must not pick for the operator.**
