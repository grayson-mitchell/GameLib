## The A-04 correction was bigger than A-04 said

The plan asked for a line-number fix: `GlobalState.tsx:1586` → `:1609`, re-verified by direct read (`window.api.frontendReady()` at 1609, confirmed).

But the **same bullet** claimed `frontendReady` "will hit `UNPORTED_CHANNEL_MARKER` under Tauri today". That is false, and false in the dangerous direction: `frontendReady` is **send-kind** (`src/preload/api/misc.ts:93`, `makeListenerCaller`), so it can never surface that marker at all — `dispatchSend` finds no listener and returns, and Rust's `sidecar_send` has no pending/timeout machinery. It failed **silently**. The inventory described a loud failure where the truth was a silent one, which is strictly worse and is exactly why D-11's `logSendHandlerReached` observable exists. Corrected in the same pass.

## The discrepancy I did NOT fix
