## Requirement-Pair Review (Test 5, live-gate-contract-authoring reference)

**Dated 2026-08-23, before any live run.** Test 5's unit of review is the PAIR, not the individual item — "does satisfying requirement A ever destroy, truncate, restart, clear, overwrite, or otherwise invalidate requirement B's own evidence or preconditions?" Per the reference's own practical scoping rule, the pass was run as **state-mutating requirements (M) against evidence-bearing requirements (N)**, not the full cross product.

**State-mutating requirements identified (M = 9):** the winetricks port landing (between Step 0 and Step 4); Step 1's login (establishes the session); Step 2's install/enable/disable/remove sequence (mutates EOS state); Step 3's real-key entry into the Keychain (persistent write); Step 4's install action (mutates the Wine prefix's `winetricks.log`); Step 7's mutate/sync-up/alter-locally/sync-down sequence (can destroy a save); Step 8's logout (clears cookies); a possible mid-run relaunch (e.g. if Step 2's crash-check actually crashes); and precondition 2's per-launch archiving cycle itself.

**Evidence-bearing requirements identified (N = 8):** Step 0's archived-log grep; Step 1's session/Library-render evidence; Step 4's D-11 observable line; Step 5's per-channel evidence (depends on the Step 1 session); Step 6's Library-diff evidence (depends on the Step 1 session); Step 7's content-comparison evidence (depends on both the Step 1 session and precondition 7's backup); Step 8's identity-check evidence; the terminal transcript / `gamelib.log` archive itself.

**Pairs the reference explicitly requires this review to name, both considered:**

1. **Step 0 × Step 4 — the winetricks port landing destroys Step 0's evidence.** Step 0's entire evidentiary value depends on running on a build where the winetricks sidecar registration does **not** exist. Step 4 (and the plan that ports winetricks, `34.6-07`) mutates exactly that state. **Flagged, and already the highest-severity finding in the whole document** — it is why the contract itself states Step 0 is run by a separate, earlier plan (`34.6-04`), not by the plan that runs Steps 1-8 (`34.6-12`). No further mitigation needed beyond the sequencing already stated in the contract's own opening paragraph.
2. **Step 1 × Step 8 — the logout destroys the login's session.** This is precisely why D-12 sequences the gate the way it does (login first, logout last). **Flagged, and this is the reason the whole document's step order exists**, not a defect to fix — Steps 5, 6, and 7 all explicitly depend on the Step 1 session being alive when they run, and Step 8 is the last item in the document for exactly this reason.

**Additional pairs considered during this pass (not exhaustive, but covering every state-mutating item against the evidence-bearing items it could plausibly touch):**

3. **Precondition 2's per-launch archive cycle × a possible relaunch after Step 2's crash-check.** If Step 2's dismissal test actually crashes the app, a relaunch is needed to continue to Step 3. Checked against precondition 2's own text: it mandates `tee -a` (append, never a bare `tee` or `>` redirect) plus a delimiter line before each launch — this is the exact fix this project's own `F-34.4.2-11` finding required, already built into this contract from the start. **Considered, not flagged** — no rewrite needed, the append semantics already prevent truncation.
4. **Step 7's mutate/sync-up/alter/sync-down sequence × precondition 7's backup.** D-14 states directly that the upload half can destroy a save. Checked: precondition 7 requires the backup to happen **before** Step 7 runs, so the ordering already protects against this pair invalidating the fixture. **Considered, not flagged** — the ordering is already correct in the document as written.
5. **Step 2's install→enable→disable→remove sequence × any later step needing EOS installed.** Checked: no scored item after Step 2 (Steps 3 through 8, or the Humble rider) touches EOS overlay state. **Considered, no hazard found** — Step 2 ending in an uninstalled state does not invalidate any later item's evidence.
6. **Step 3's real API-key Keychain write × Step 4/5's own preconditions.** Checked: no later item depends on the SteamGridDB key's *absence*; Step 4/5 are keyed to the Epic/Wine session and channel registrations, not to SteamGridDB state. **Considered, no hazard found.**
7. **Step 1's `keyring_get` timeout rider × later steps' own RPC budget.** Considered whether a timeout firing during Step 1 could exhaust the sidecar's 60s RPC budget and cause a false FAIL in a later, unrelated step. Because all scored items in this contract run **sequentially** (never concurrently — this is a single-operator, single-session gate), a budget consumed during Step 1 does not carry forward into Step 2's own RPC calls. **Considered, low risk, not flagged** — no rewrite needed.

**Reduction applied, stated explicitly per the reference's own requirement:** considered every {port landing, login, EOS sequence, key entry, winetricks install, save-sync sequence, logout, possible relaunch} state-mutating requirement against every evidence-bearing requirement its later-numbered items could plausibly depend on — not the full 9×8 cross product, since most state mutations (e.g. Step 3's key entry) have no plausible interaction with most evidence (e.g. Step 0's marker capture, which already ran on an earlier build via a separate plan). **2 pairs flagged as load-bearing to the document's own structure (already correctly handled by existing sequencing), 5 additional pairs considered and found not to be hazards.**
