## Decisions Made

- **No validation, normalization, or path-containment guard added** (D-02, explicit plan scope boundary) — `moveInstall`/`importGame` forward renderer-supplied paths unchanged, exactly as `main.ts` did. This is deliberate: a live-gate FAIL on either channel must be bisectable as "the port broke it" vs. "validation rejected it", and that bisectability is D-02's whole point. `T-34.5-C6-49-03` hardening for these two channels is owned by plan 34.6-11 (wave 4), a dedicated, separately-committed plan.
- **`.toStrictEqual` over `.toBe` for the `wineVersion` object argument** — the sidecar harness's `writeInvoke`/`dispatchInvoke` round-trip is a real JSON-lines RPC transport, not an in-process call; object references cannot survive real serialization even when the handler itself never reshapes the value. `.toStrictEqual` (deep structural equality) is therefore the correct byte-equivalence proof for objects, while primitives (`trickyPath`, `'Windows'`) do preserve identity by value across the wire and are asserted with `.toBe`.
- **`electronReachLedger.test.ts`'s +2 `visitedFiles` growth was empirically diffed, not assumed** — an earlier draft of the header comment speculated the growth came from `common/types.ts` type additions without directly verifying this. That speculation was caught and replaced before commit: full `visitedFiles` sets were captured via temporary `console.log(JSON.stringify([...visitedFiles]))` instrumentation both before and after the entry-point addition, then diffed with a script, revealing the real +2 files (`installFlowRegistration.ts` itself and `backend/utils/checkGameUpdates.ts`). The committed comment states the verified fact, not the earlier guess.
