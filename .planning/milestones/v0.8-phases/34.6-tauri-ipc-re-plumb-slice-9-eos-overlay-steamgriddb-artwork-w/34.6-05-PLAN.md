<objective>
Build the D-11 observable and port `frontendReady`, the first of the two send-kind channels in
this phase's scope.

D-11 measured a correction to `IPC-PORT-INVENTORY.md`: `frontendReady` is send-kind
(`src/preload/api/misc.ts:93`, `makeListenerCaller`), not invoke-kind. Under Tauri it therefore
fails SILENTLY — the inventory's claim that it "will hit `UNPORTED_CHANNEL_MARKER`" is true only
inside the transport and is never surfaced to the caller. That makes it WORSE than the inventory
implies, and puts it under D-11's observable requirement alongside `winetricksInstall`. There are
TWO send channels in this phase's scope, not one.

D-11 keeps send-kind channels send-kind: converting to invoke-kind would smuggle a behaviour change
into a port and diverge Electron from Tauri immediately before the cutover. The honest limitation
D-11 itself records stands: a log line is not a return value, and the caller still cannot know it
failed.

Purpose: make "the handler was reached" provable from `gamelib.log` for both send channels.
Output: a shared observable module, `frontendReady` registered, and the electron-reach ledger grown.
</objective>
