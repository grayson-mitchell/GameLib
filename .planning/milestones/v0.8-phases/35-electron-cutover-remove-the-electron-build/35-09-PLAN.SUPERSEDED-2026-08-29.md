<objective>
Make logout actually clear the embedded browser, and prove it by reading the state back rather than
by trusting the clear call's own return value.

Purpose: `session.fromPartition` is a D-09 accepted no-op under Tauri, and the Epic logout is the
standing FAIL at Step 8 of the 34.6 live gate — the app reports clearing cookies it does not clear.
On a shared machine that is a credential-exposure bug, not a cosmetic one: the next user of the
profile opens the login window and is still signed in.

The mechanism correction: D-09's text says delete the webview data directory. Tauri's own
`clear_all_browsing_data()` (added 2.0.0-rc.16, present at the pinned 2.11.5) does the same thing —
strictly stronger than `clearStorageData()`, taking localStorage and IndexedDB too — without any
per-OS path resolution. Hardcoding `~/Library/HTTPStorage/{pkg}` and friends is how a clear silently
no-ops on an OS version nobody tested. Honour the intent, use the safer mechanism.

Output: one Rust command, two logout call sites rewired, and a post-clear read proving the jar is
empty.
</objective>
