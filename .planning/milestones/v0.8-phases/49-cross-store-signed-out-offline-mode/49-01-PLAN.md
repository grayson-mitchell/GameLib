<assumption_delta_decision>
**Noun:** per-store sign-in state (`connected | not-connected | expired | unknown`) computed by the
`src/common` selector (`resolveSignInState` / `resolveSignInStates` in `src/common/signInState.ts`).

**Transition:** two store-specific expiry flags (`steamConfigStore.credentialsMissing`,
`humbleConfigStore.expired`) are generalised into one five-store, four-value state.

**Decision: `promote`.** The common selector is the primary identity for sign-in state. Each
store's persisted flag (`credentialsMissing` for Steam, `expired` for Humble, and the three new
`expired` keys 49-03 adds for Epic, GOG and Amazon) is demoted to a variant detail that only the
selector reads, through `collectSignInInputs`. No surface reads a flag and decides a state on its
own.

**Rationale:** D-06 (one module in `src/common`, consumed by the pass, the tiles and the notice;
the parity test pins both surfaces to one function) and D-07 (the selector's rule table is the
contract: a latched flag beats a pending or unknown probe).

**Companion invariant test:** the R7 parity test (`signInStateParity.test.ts`, 49-10) already
encodes this — it fails if `Login/index.tsx` or `LibrarySignInNotice` stops calling
`resolveSignInStates(collectSignInInputs(` or re-inlines a flag comparison. No extra task is needed.
</assumption_delta_decision>
