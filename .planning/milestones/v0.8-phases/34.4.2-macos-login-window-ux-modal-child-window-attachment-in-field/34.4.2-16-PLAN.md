  mid-gate by plan 12 with no discharging item; plan 14 built the single-flight guard and this run is

  changed `src-tauri/src/main.rs` substantially. This phase's own repeated rule is that a fix, or a
  deletion, does not inherit a previous run's result. **Re-measure them. Do not carry them forward.**
