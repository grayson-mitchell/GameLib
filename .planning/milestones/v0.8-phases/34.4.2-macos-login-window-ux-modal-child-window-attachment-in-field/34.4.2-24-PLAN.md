<objective>
Run the blocking live gate on real macOS hardware and record the measured result.

Purpose: this project has now been shown seven times that a fully green automated suite proves
nothing about live login-window behaviour. **Every blocking defect across Phases 34.4.1 and 34.4.2 —
the sheet-attachment wedge, the autofill non-fill, the origin-confusion race, the Humble-disconnect
deadlock — was found by a human driving the UI on real hardware, with `cargo test` and
`npm run test:ci` green throughout every one of them.** This plan is the only source of truth about
whether the login surface actually works end to end.

**What is genuinely new this run:**
- **The capture cannot silently split.** RERUN-4 lost items 1 and 2's decisive `[shell]` evidence to
  a concurrent second `gamelib-shell` instance while `gamelib.log` stayed shared, producing a
  populated log that looked like proof (F-34.4.2-15/F-A). The contract now asserts exactly ONE
  instance before each launch, records the PID at launch and teardown, and ABORTS the launch
  otherwise. **A launch whose instance count was ever not 1 is not scorable — its evidence is not
  cited, and the launch is re-run.**
- **The login-form premise is finally true.** An unscored preparatory disconnect (D-G2) clears the
  WKWebView cookie jar before any item that needs a form, confirmed by a RENDERED LOGIN FORM — not
  by an emptied `humble_store/config.json`, which is exactly the inference F-34.4.2-16 recorded as
  insufficient. Items 1(e), 3(a) and 4 were ALL unmeasurable in RERUN-4 for this one reason.
- **Item 5 is withdrawn (D-G1)**, not deferred. Its scenario cannot be driven from the shipped UI.
  Do not attempt it; do not treat its absence as an omission.
- **Item 6(b) gets an in-session positive control.** RERUN-4's PASS had no same-session confirmation
  that the lines it asserts absent fire correctly for other windows. This run supplies one.
- **Item 6(a) has now been NOT ATTEMPTED twice consecutively** — once foreclosed by the wedge itself,
  once by never reaching its launch. It is the live discharge of F-34.4.2-12's fix, and a source fix
  closes nothing in this phase (T-34.4.2-26).
- **All items are re-measured, none inherited** (D-G3). Item 6(b)'s RERUN-4 PASS is historical record.

Discipline inherited from plans 06, 10, 12, 16 and 20 and not relaxed: transcribe what the operator
observed, verbatim; assert no root cause; record an honest NOT ATTEMPTED rather than a guessed PASS;
apply the no-partial-pass rule at the item level; never use a transcript's silence to prove a backend
path did not run; and propagate whatever result arrives, including a bad one.

Output: a filled gate document, an updated threat roll-up, correct requirement checkboxes, and
ROADMAP/STATE/ledger propagation.
