defect-class tests and each takes a SINGLE requirement as its unit of review. F-34.4.2-11 was an
INTERACTION between two individually-correct requirements — a mandatory truncating `tee` and a

per operator decision D-F3 it must live somewhere durable enough to apply to every future gate
contract, not be re-invented inside RERUN-4 and lost again.

confirmed by source read, and the RERUN-3 contract accounts for neither:
- `[shell]`-prefixed Rust lines go to `tauri:dev`'s own stdout/stderr and reach the tee'd terminal

- Sidecar-side `logInfo`/`logWarning` lines go to `gamelib.log` and reach the tee'd terminal
  transcript NOT AT ALL, because the sidecar's stdout is the RPC frame pipe (this project's own
  `sidecar-console-and-logger-are-invisible` lesson). **Three of RERUN-3's required lines are
  sidecar-emitted** — item 4's `Humble sync finished:` (`src/backend/humble/library.ts:952`), item

  `Humble login-window cookie read UNSUPPORTED_OR_ERROR` (`src/backend/humble/user.ts:460`). The
  contract required them of a sink that structurally cannot carry them. That is why item 4's line was
  absent from the transcript, and no cause was ever assigned to it.
- And `gamelib.log` has its OWN relaunch-truncation behaviour: `src/backend/logger/log_writer.ts:72-74`

  destroys the first launch's sidecar evidence outright.
