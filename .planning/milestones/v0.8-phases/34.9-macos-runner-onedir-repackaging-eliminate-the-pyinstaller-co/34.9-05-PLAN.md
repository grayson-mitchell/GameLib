   convention and append an archive extension — do not impose GameLib's `_onedir.tar.gz` suffix on
   someone else's repository.

Write one `PR-BODY-{repo}.md` per patched repo, in this order:

- One sentence stating the change: switch the macOS PyInstaller build to `--onedir` and ship it as
  an archive; Linux and Windows unchanged.
- The mechanism, stated as cause rather than symptom: on macOS `amfid` performs a Gatekeeper
  assessment per extracted file, and `--onefile`'s randomly-named `$TMPDIR/_MEIxxxxxx` extraction
  directory means that assessment cache never hits, so every cold spawn re-assesses the whole
  bundle.
- The measured evidence for THAT repo's runner, taken from `34.9-MEASUREMENT.md`: its own onefile
  control's cold/warm figures, its own onedir build's cold/warm figures, and the ratio, plus the
  methodology (built from the repo's own CI pyinstaller command with only the flag changed, both
  adhoc-signed, cold samples separated by 400s idles, order interleaved to defeat ordering
  artifacts). If a runner did not reproduce an order-of-magnitude win, say so plainly and let the
  smaller number stand — a PR that overstates its own benefit will be caught and will cost
  credibility on the other two.
- The cost, honestly: roughly 2.2x bundle growth and around 100 extra files per macOS asset, and
  the asset becomes an archive consumers must extract.
- Who benefits: Heroic Games Launcher itself vendors these binaries and its macOS users pay this
  tax today.
- A closing note that GameLib is currently building these same onedir bundles in its own CI as an
  interim measure and will drop that local build once upstream ships them.
