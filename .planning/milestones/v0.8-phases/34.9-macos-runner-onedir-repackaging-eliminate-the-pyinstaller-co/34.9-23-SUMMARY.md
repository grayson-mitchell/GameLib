# Phase 34.9 Plan 23: C2-01 Pipe-Swallow Census and Impact Audit Summary

## Accomplishments
- Derived the C2-01 census by a mechanical predicate against the live `package.json` (JSON.parse,
  never line-range grep) and found **13** instances, one more than gap-planning's own 12-item list
  -- `build:sidecar-sea` at `package.json:35`, outside the line-61-72 window both prior scoping
  passes used.
- Ran the repo's real esbuild against all 12 distinct entry files into a scratch dir outside the
  repo: all compile clean today (exit 0) -- no script is silently passing over an already-broken
  compile.
- Enumerated every executable caller across `.github/workflows/` (18 files), `.husky/` and
  `package.json` itself: 7 of 13 scripts have a real caller, 6 have none. Every CI caller is
  currently BLOCKED UPSTREAM by one of two distinct mechanisms (install-deps' unconditional
  digest-sentinel throw, or a workflow file absent from the default branch).
- Measured `__dirname`/`require.main` against the EMITTED BUNDLES (not source) for all 12 entry
  files: zero references everywhere. No BLOCKER exists for the conversion plan.
- Empirically proved (real Node v26.2.0) that `process.argv` is identical between `node - args` and
  `node <file> args` for the two arg-receiving callers, and that bare `| node` (no dash) crashes on
  any appended argument today, before ever reading stdin -- the conversion fixes this latent defect
  for the ten bare-`| node` scripts.
- Enumerated a 13-location stale-comment inventory (exact file:line) that plan 34.9-25 must correct
  once the idiom changes.
