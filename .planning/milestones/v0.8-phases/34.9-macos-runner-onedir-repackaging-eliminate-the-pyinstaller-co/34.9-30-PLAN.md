<confirmation_rule_interaction priority="read-this-first">
A PAIRWISE DEFECT that must be designed in, not discovered at execution.

The existing FIXED-row rule requires the Independent-confirmation cell to cite a non-`.planning/`
artifact. But C3-03's own fix IS a `.planning/` artifact -- the sweep script itself. Under the
existing rule, C3-03's own FIXED row could never pass the very check C3-03 asks us to tighten. Two
individually-correct requirements produce a contradiction only in combination; this project's own
standing rule says item-by-item review structurally cannot catch this class, so it is stated here
up front.

Resolution: the non-`.planning/` ban was always a PROXY for its real purpose -- "do not accept the
phase's own narrative as proof of its own effect." Re-express the rule at that purpose:
  - REJECT any confirmation citing a plan SUMMARY (unchanged, this was the real target).
  - ACCEPT a confirmation citing `meta/`, `src/`, or `package.json` (unchanged).
  - ALSO ACCEPT a confirmation that cites a reproducible command invocation together with its
    observed result, even when the artifact lives under `.planning/` -- a command anyone can re-run
    is independent evidence, whereas a document asserting its own correctness is not.
  - REJECT, in all cases, a confirmation whose text denies the fix (the new C3-03 polarity check).
</confirmation_rule_interaction>
