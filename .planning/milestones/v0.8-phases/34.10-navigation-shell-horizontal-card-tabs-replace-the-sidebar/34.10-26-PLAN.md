   frontmatter — and IDs legitimately appear earlier, in `items[].findings` cross-reference arrays;

None of the six altered a measured verdict, but each would have read to a later reader as "the gate
did not complete." The generalisable rule, and this plan's charter: **an automated gate check must
be executed against a filled specimen at authoring time, not merely reasoned about.**
