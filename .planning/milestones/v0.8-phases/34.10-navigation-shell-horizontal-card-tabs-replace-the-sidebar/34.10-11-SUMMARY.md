- **Item 1 (theme survival, REQ-34.10-06) -- PASS.** All three scored themes (`midnightMirage`, `gruvbox_dark`, `dracula`) plus the `dracula-classic` spot-check confirmed the card/folder tab merges into the content surface with no visible seam, surviving `dracula`'s navbar-lighter-than-body inversion exactly as `navigation-shell.md` predicted.
- **Item 2 (window dragging, REQ-34.10-04) -- PASS.** All five sub-checks passed in both frameless states: empty-navbar drag moves the window, drag by a tab/the Downloads ring/inside the window-controls area does NOT move it, and the native titlebar still drags with `framelessWindow` OFF. This is the item the "three fixtures, not two" trap targeted (34.10-08) -- the `.Sidebar` -> `.NavShell__navbar` migration is now confirmed live, not just against a mocked `closest()`.
- **Item 3 (tier-2 portal live, REQ-34.10-09) -- FAIL, F-34.10-01.** Search filters the grid correctly -- decisive proof the portal seam itself is sound and `LibraryContext`'s no-op `initialContext` trap did NOT ship, exactly as 34.10-06's design intended. But Categories and Filters are unusable: `Dropdown/index.scss`'s `.dropdown { position: absolute; right: 0; min-width: 250px }` anchors a 250px popup inside a 204px (`--tier2-width`) column, and `popUpOnHover` (`CategoryFilter/index.tsx:78`, `LibraryFilters/index.tsx:265`) opens it on mouseover, not click. The popup overflows off the left edge of the window with the checkboxes unreachable. The game-page collapse sub-checks were never reached (session halted at the failure) and are correctly recorded NOT ATTEMPTED, not inferred.
- **Item 4 (Downloads ring, REQ-34.10-08) -- FAIL, F-34.10-02.** No ring renders: invisible at rest, a solid disc on hover, no progress fill during a real download -- only the count badge works. Two independent causes diagnosed from source: (a) the ring's `::after` hole paints `var(--navbar-background)` while the `Link`'s own `:hover` repaints its parent `var(--navbar-active-background)`, so the hole stops matching its backdrop on hover (reading as a solid disc) and the idle track (`--divider` at `opacity: 0.5`) has near-zero contrast at rest; (b) `--dl-progress` never leaves `0turn` because `hasProgress(appName, runner)` yields no percent for the head element in this mount context, even though the same queue subscription correctly drives the count badge -- the count and the arc read from different sources.
- **Item 5 (Electron, REQ-34.10-05) -- NOT ATTEMPTED.** Shell renders and the traffic-light observation were both measured (macOS traffic lights do NOT overlap the tab bar -- recorded per D-03's "record, don't fix" framing), but the all-4-tabs-navigate sub-check was not reached in the operator's session and is correctly recorded NOT ATTEMPTED rather than inferred either way.
- **Why no automated check caught either finding:** the `Frontend` jest project is `testEnvironment: 'node'` with no jsdom and no component rendering (`34.10-VALIDATION.md` Test Infrastructure). No test in this phase can resolve a CSS custom property to its computed value, compute contrast, observe geometry/overflow of an absolutely-positioned popup, detect a `mouseover` vs `click` trigger, or catch a hook returning no percent against a live download queue. Both findings are exactly the class of defect this gate exists to catch -- the project's own recorded lesson ("a live gate beat a green suite three times") now stands at four.
