# Phase 34.10 Plan 13: Dropdown click-toggle + in-flow tier-2 geometry Summary

**Turned the two Header filter Dropdowns from a detached, right-anchored, 250px-min-width hover popup into a click-toggled, in-flow, 50vh-capped disclosure that fits the 204px tier-2 column, and repaired gamepad.ts's `closeDropdown()`, which had never actually closed anything.**

## Accomplishments
- `Dropdown` opens/closes on click only; hover trigger is deleted, not merely unused
- `aria-expanded` on the trigger button, driven by the same state the panel's class reads
- Blur-close is containment-checked at the container, fixing the bug where clicking the trigger while the panel held focus would silently refuse to close
- Panel geometry is in-flow (`width: 100%`, `box-sizing: border-box`), capped at `50vh`, with an inset hairline instead of a 7px drop shadow, and the dead `body.isRTL` override is deleted
- `.NavShell__tier2Portal` gained a scroll container so an expanded panel taller than the column stays reachable, without touching `.NavShell__tier2`'s load-bearing `overflow: hidden` / width-collapse transition
- A direct-invocation test pins the full open→close→open cycle, the absence of hover handlers, the `aria-expanded` state, the containment-checked blur, and the `dropdownButton` literal `gamepad.ts:397` depends on
- A comment-immune source gate pins the geometry declarations
