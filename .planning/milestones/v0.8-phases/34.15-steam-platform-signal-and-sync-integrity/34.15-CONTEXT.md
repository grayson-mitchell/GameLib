## Phase Boundary

Close the three pre-existing Steam defects that Phase 34.14's D-08 UAT gate surfaced
but which 34.14 never claimed to fix.

34.14 made the renderer behave **safely** when the depot signal is missing. This phase:

1. **Reduces how often the signal is missing** — the Steam library sync captures platform
   data in bulk instead of relying on lazy per-game fetches.
2. **Gives the library sync a terminal failure state** — and stops a Steam-only failure
   from hiding every other runner's games.
3. **Applies 34.14's own *absent-is-not-negative* principle to the mac side**, where it
   was never applied.

**Explicitly out of scope** (fenced, do not widen):
- The 32-bit-mac orphaned-install todo (`.planning/todos/pending/2026-08-15-32bit-mac-steam-titles-orphan-their-native-install.md`) — install-lifecycle, a different axis.
- The `provisionBottle` CrossOver guard remaining in the GPTK broken-bottle todo.
- `PlatformSupport.tsx`'s unconditional Windows glyph on the game page — deferred by 34.14's route fence, still deferred (see Deferred).
- The **global** foreground-refresh overlay at `src/frontend/screens/Library/index.tsx:1011` (`{refreshing && !refreshingInTheBackground && <UpdateComponent />}`) — see **D-10**.

## Implementation Decisions

### Governing constraints carried forward (LOCKED — not reopenable in this phase)

These are inherited, not decided here. A plan that breaks one of them is wrong regardless
of what else it achieves.

- **The three-valued contract on `is_windows_native` survives.** `undefined` = never
  captured, `false` = confirmed no depot, `true` = depot present. Do **not** collapse
  `undefined → false` to make the data look complete. `library.ts:764-776` documents at
  length why that collapse was removed and 34.14's entire gating layer depends on the
  distinction surviving.
- **`depotSignalCaptured()`** (`src/backend/storeManagers/steam/metadataCapture.ts`, quick
  task `260816-hdg`) is the pinned read boundary. **D-02** below requires it stay
  byte-identical.
- **No `!== false` loosening, on either the Windows or the mac side.** That is the
  `treatsAbsentAsAvailable` saboteur shape that **three shipped gates in
  `steamPlatformRow.test.ts` exist to reject**, and the same trap applies on the mac side.
- **`hasSteamWindowsDepot` and its saboteurs are DO-NOT-TOUCH.**
- **34.14 D-04's fail-open asymmetry must not be "corrected"** toward
  `applyEligibilityFailure`'s fail-closed sibling. Its rationale is written out in
  `steamPlatformRow.ts`; a reviewer finding only one branch is exactly what that
  documentation obligation exists to prevent.

### Item 1 — bulk platform capture at sync time

- **D-01: The bulk source is Steam CM PICS `getProductInfo` (`appinfo.common.oslist`),
  NOT batched `store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails`.**

  This **overrides the ROADMAP's stated direction**, deliberately. Scouting at HEAD found
  the repo already ships a proven bulk PICS path: `depot.ts:430` fetches every package
  license in one `getProductInfo([], packageIds, true)` call, and `depot.ts:476` fetches
  DLC app IDs in bulk the same way — both bounded by `STEAM_PICS_BULK_TIMEOUT_MS`, sized
  precisely because node-steam-user #144 flags large-library PICS as legitimately slow.
  No HTTP rate limit to measure, no undocumented endpoint, no batch size to guess.

  The rejected alternative's risk: multi-appid `appdetails` is widely reported to return
  *reduced* payloads unless `filters=` is set. If `platforms` is stripped from a batched
  response, the ROADMAP's direction is a dead end — and the phase would discover that
  during execution rather than planning.

  **Consequence planning must handle:** `oslist` is a *different source* from
  `appdetails.platforms`. They could disagree. `appdetails` remains the established source
  of truth for the D-17 gate, so a later per-game `appdetails` fetch legitimately
  overwrites PICS-sourced values. That is intended, not a race to eliminate.

- **D-02: A PICS write is a FIRST-CLASS capture, performed as a MERGE, and an empty
  `oslist` writes nothing.**

  - It writes `is_windows_native` / `is_mac_native` / `is_linux_native` **and**
    `platformsCaptured: true`.
  - `depotSignalCaptured()` stays **byte-identical and untouched** — it flips `true`
    naturally, because `is_windows_native` is now present. The quick-task `260816-hdg`
    read boundary is preserved, not widened.
  - **Read-modify-write, never a wholesale `steamMetadataStore.set()`.** That store's
    `set` REPLACES the entire entry (`games.ts:697-707` documents this as T-18-02-04): a
    wholesale write would silently drop `mac_arch_verified` / `mac_arch_source` and the
    D-17 `forcedWindowsViaBottle` verdict. Every carry-forward obligation at
    `games.ts:704` applies to this new writer too.
  - **If `oslist` is absent or empty for a game, write NOTHING.** `undefined` stays
    `undefined` rather than manufacturing a false "captured" — which is the exact residue
    shape quick task `260816-hdg` just spent a whole task cleaning up.

- **D-03: The capture runs INLINE in `SteamLibraryManager.refresh()`, awaited before the
  GameInfo hydration loop — and it MUST fail soft.**

  Placement: between Step 1 (`getUserOwnedApps`, `library.ts:694-698`) and Step 3 (the
  per-game `GameInfo` build at `library.ts:735+`). The CM client is already connected and
  the owned-app ID list is already in hand, so it is one bulk call on a live socket.

  Why here: every `GameInfo` is then correct on its **first** push. No re-push, no second
  IPC event, no convergence window, no resumability machinery.

  **Fail-soft is a hard requirement, not a nicety.** A PICS timeout or error logs and
  continues to today's cache-only hydration. It must never abort the sync — aborting would
  trade "incomplete metadata" for "no library", which is defect 2's exact shape.

- **D-04: Scope the capture to apps where `depotSignalCaptured()` is `false`.**

  Self-limiting and converging: the first sync after this ships repairs the ~370 pre-D-17
  residue entries plus every never-fetched game; subsequent syncs ask for little or
  nothing. It reuses the **same predicate** 34.14's gating reads, so "what the bulk job
  repairs" and "what the install form calls unresolved" are the same set by construction.

  Accepted staleness: a game that later gains a Mac build is not re-asked. `appdetails`'
  existing per-game self-heal remains the refresh path for that.

  No new progress UI is needed — the call is inline in `refresh()`, which the existing
  indicator already covers.

- **D-05: THE NON-CONSEQUENCE — item 1 makes "unresolved" RARE, not impossible. Nothing
  downstream may assume the data is complete.**

  This was established explicitly during discussion and is the single most important thing
  for a later reader not to get wrong. Four durable paths keep an unresolved signal
  reachable for a game that is **visible in the library**, all verified at HEAD:

  1. **PICS fail-soft (D-03).** Sync completes, library renders, `is_windows_native` stays
     `undefined`.
  2. **Per-app absent `oslist` (D-02).** Correctly stays uncaptured.
  3. **The cached-library early returns.** `library.ts:701-702` serves the persisted
     library when `getUserOwnedApps` throws; `library.ts:670` returns early when the Steam
     client is not ready. Neither path runs the bulk step, but games still render.
  4. **Startup, before any sync.** `init()` (`library.ts:510-521`) pushes the persisted
     game list to the frontend independently of `refresh()`, so the library is on screen
     from the first frame of every cold start.

  There is a fifth, milder second chance: opening the dialog fires the eligibility probe,
  which awaits `ensurePlatformsCaptured()` → `fetchMetadataIfNeeded()` → `appdetails` under
  a 15s deadline. That is a race with a deadline, not a guarantee — which is exactly what
  34.14's D-08 gate proved twice.

  **Therefore 34.14's D-04 fail-open, the pending row, and the Install-disable all remain
  load-bearing and must not be weakened, removed, or "simplified" on the grounds that the
  data is complete now.**

### Item 2 — Steam sync terminal failure state

- **D-06: Replace `refreshingInTheBackground` in the spinner guard with a real Steam sync
  state machine (`'idle' | 'syncing' | 'failed'`).**

  Scouting found the guard is worse than the todo describes.
  `Library/index.tsx:1013-1019` reads
  `steam?.username && steam?.library?.length === 0 && refreshingInTheBackground` — but
  `refreshingInTheBackground` defaults `true` (`GlobalState.tsx:295`) **and is reset to
  `true` after every unscoped refresh** (`GlobalState.tsx:1066`). It never means "a
  refresh is running". The guard effectively reads **"Steam is logged in AND the Steam
  library is empty"** — permanently, on failure *and* on a genuinely empty library.

  A tri-state owned by `GlobalState`, set to `'syncing'` when the Steam refresh starts and
  to `'failed'` or `'idle'` when it terminates, makes the terminal state **structural**
  rather than a boolean that happens to clear. That distinction is the whole point: the
  sibling bug at `GlobalState.tsx:1508-1515` was fixed once by ensuring refresh *runs*, and
  this defect is the same symptom from refresh running and then *failing*.

  Rejected: reusing `refreshingByRunner.steam`. It is a boolean (cannot distinguish
  "failed" from "succeeded-and-empty", so it cannot drive an error message), and the
  mount-time refresh is **unscoped**, so it would never be set on the path where the bug
  actually reproduces.

- **D-07: The failure propagates via a `steamSyncStatus` frontend event, mirroring
  `steamMetadataSyncing` verbatim.**

  Today the backend knows and the UI never hears: `library.ts:670` logs
  `Steam client not ready, skipping library refresh` and returns `null`; the
  `refreshLibrary` IPC resolves normally, so `refreshLibrary`'s catch never fires.

  The pattern to copy already ships: `games.ts:587/748` emit
  `sendFrontendMessage('steamMetadataSyncing', { syncing })`, declared at
  `src/common/types/ipc.ts:632`, allowlisted at `src/preload/api/steam.ts:51`, consumed at
  `GlobalState.tsx:1313`. `sendFrontendMessage` works identically on Electron and the Tauri
  sidecar.

  **Two obligations planning must honour:**
  1. Add the new channel to the preload allowlist. A missing entry fails **silently** on
     the sidecar (ledgered: `sidecar-send-channels-fail-silently`).
  2. Emit on **every** exit path of `refresh()` — including the early return at
     `library.ts:670` and the `getUserOwnedApps` catch at `library.ts:699-703`.

- **D-08: On failure, show an inline, non-blocking Steam notice with a Steam-scoped retry.
  The localisation gate is therefore BLOCKING for this phase.**

  Not `ErrorComponent` — that exists (`src/frontend/components/UI/ErrorComponent/index.tsx`,
  with a wired Refresh Library retry) but is a **full-screen replacement**
  (`Library/index.tsx:883-893`), so using it here would recreate defect 2b.

  A compact row in the library flow states that the Steam library could not be synced and
  offers a retry calling `refreshLibrary({ library: 'steam', origin: … })` — the scoped
  path already exists (`GlobalState.tsx:1115-1122`) and already clears its own flag on
  failure (`:1147-1155`). Other runners' games stay visible and interactive behind it.

  **This ships new user-facing strings, so the standing localisation gate is BLOCKING.**
  Accepted deliberately, with the alternatives weighed: silent-clear was rejected for
  contradicting the todo's own instruction ("surface the actual error"); existing-strings-only
  was rejected because generic copy cannot name Steam or the reason.

- **D-09: Prove the failure path with a pure resolver + exhaustive unit tests + a
  RED-proven source gate.**

  Constraint: the Frontend jest project has **no jsdom and no CSS transform**, and
  `Library/index.tsx`'s first line is `import './index.scss'` — nothing importable from it
  can be rendered or unit-tested. `GlobalStateScopedRefresh.test.ts` works around this by
  grepping source text.

  Follow the house pattern this repo already uses three times for exactly this constraint
  (`steamPlatformRow.ts`, `steamSectionGating.ts`, `steamEligibilityProbe.ts`): put the
  sync-state → what-renders decision in a sibling **pure module**, unit-test every state
  including `'failed'` and `'idle'`-with-empty-library, and add a source-shape gate proving
  `index.tsx` calls the resolver rather than re-inlining the boolean.

  **The source gate must be RED-proven** — demonstrated to fail against the current
  known-bad expression — not merely written and observed green. Ledgered requirement:
  `grep-assertion-must-fail-against-known-bad-input`.

  The existing coverage proves the spinner appears and clears on success. The uncovered
  case is refresh **running and failing** — exactly the gap that let the sibling bug recur
  in a new form.

### Item 2b — overlay scoping

- **D-10: The Steam indicator becomes an inline row above the grid, never a centred
  overlay. Syncing and failed are ONE widget in TWO modes.**

  There is no per-runner section to "scope" to — `libraryToShow` is a single merged grid.
  The live repro had 6 GOG games hidden behind a centred Steam spinner.

  Putting the indicator in normal document flow kills defect 2b **by construction**: there
  is no longer anything that *can* cover the grid. It also unifies the two Steam states
  into one surface rather than two unrelated ones.

  Rejected: adding `libraryToShow.length === 0` to the existing guard. It fixes the observed
  symptom without fixing the shape — a Steam-only concern would still own a full-screen
  surface.

- **D-11: The GLOBAL foreground-refresh overlay at `Library/index.tsx:1011` is OUT OF
  SCOPE — fenced explicitly.**

  `{refreshing && !refreshingInTheBackground && <UpdateComponent />}` covers a refresh of
  *every* runner, so covering the grid is arguably correct, and it is driven by a flag that
  does mean what it says. This fence is recorded because a planner reading "no overlay may
  cover the grid" would otherwise reasonably rewrite this one too — changing behaviour for
  four other runners on a path with no reported defect and no UAT evidence.

### Item 3 — mac signal

- **D-12: Extend the EXISTING `resolveDepotAvailability` to return a mac triple. Do not add
  a sibling function.**

  It already receives the probe and the seed and already computes `depotSignalResolved`.
  The mac answer rides the identical signal, from the identical fetch, resolved at the
  identical moment. One function keeps owning "what do we know about this game's
  platforms" — the single-owner property `steamPlatformRow.ts`'s own header claims for
  itself, and two readers of the same fact is precisely the drift it warns against.

  **Cost planning must pay explicitly:** this widens a function whose behaviour three
  shipped saboteur gates pin. Those tests must be reconciled **explicitly**, and the plan
  must state which fixtures changed and why each saboteur stays valid. (This is 34.14's own
  landmine #1, recurring one level out.)

- **D-13: While the depot signal is unresolved, suppress the ENTIRE glyph row in the
  SteamDialog header — including the Windows glyph.**

  Confirmed at HEAD: `SteamDialog/index.tsx:380-391` renders the row from
  `availablePlatforms`, and the Windows entry is `available: true` **unconditionally**
  (`InstallModal/index.tsx:224-228`). On a Mac host with an unresolved signal, Linux is
  `false`, macOS is `isMac && isMacNative` = `false`, Browser is `false` — so the row is
  exactly one Windows glyph. That reproduces the operator's observation precisely.

  The row is an **assertion about the game**. With the signal unresolved the app cannot make
  it, and a bare Windows glyph is the strongest possible form of a claim it cannot back. No
  icons is honest; a Windows-only icon is not.

  Dropping the unconditional Windows glyph along with it is **correct**, not collateral — it
  too is unbacked. **Scope: the SteamDialog header only.** The other four dialogs' headers
  are untouched, and `PlatformSupport.tsx` stays deferred.

- **D-14: `getDefaultplatform` returns Windows on unknown — with the rationale written into
  the code — AND the default re-derives once the depot signal resolves.**

  The rationale to comment: Windows-via-bottle always works, and most Steam titles are
  Windows-only. Note carefully that the operator-locked constraint says **mac-ONLY** games
  are a null set, which does **not** imply most games have a Mac build — do not misread it
  as an argument for defaulting to Mac.

  But the constant is not the defect. `getDefaultplatform()` is a `useState` **initializer**
  (`InstallModal/index.tsx:240-250`), so it runs once at open from the frozen seed and stays
  Windows even after the probe resolves "this game IS mac-native" — which is the observed
  Terraria symptom. Re-derive when the signal resolves, mirroring 34.14 D-05's seed/resolve
  shape.

  Safe to re-derive because **Install is DISABLED for the whole pending window** (34.14
  D-01), so the value can never change under a user mid-commit.

  Per D-05, unresolved-at-open stays reachable, so this branch is not rare.

- **D-15: 34.14's pending-row pin stays as-is — do NOT reopen it.**

  `readonlyPlatformValue('pending')` deliberately pins the disabled selector to display
  **macOS** during the same window in which D-13 now suppresses the glyph row. That looks
  inconsistent, and it was considered.

  It stays because: the selector is disabled, the Install button is disabled, and an
  `EligibilityLoadingRow` is on screen explaining the wait — so the displayed value is
  **inert, not an offer**. The unknown → known transition is the honest sequence. And 34.14
  made this decision deliberately, wrote its rationale down (avoid a second
  `EligibilityLoadingRow` mount; keep `steamEligibilityWiring.test.ts`'s B1 gate green), and
  guarded it with a shipped gate.

  **If planning finds it genuinely incoherent once both changes sit in the same dialog, that
  is a FINDING to raise — not a licence to change it silently.** Any change must reconcile
  the B1 gate explicitly.

### UAT

- **D-16: ONE BLOCKING human UAT gate covering items 1 and 2, with concrete forcing steps
  per branch.**

  Both are about behaviour a warm, online library cannot reproduce on demand — the same
  constraint that shaped 34.14's D-08.

  **Item 1 needs a real ~378-game library and a live CM socket:** does the bulk PICS call
  actually populate the cache, how long does it take, does fail-soft actually fail soft.
  **Item 2 needs a forced unreachable Steam with other runners' games present** — the
  original repro had 6 GOG games behind the overlay.

  Gate-authoring requirements carried from this project's own ledger, all mandatory:
  - Name a **concrete, repeatable forcing procedure per branch** (34.14 D-08's lesson: a
    gate without an explicit forcing step is authored against a condition that never
    renders).
  - Block with an **unroutable IP such as `203.0.113.1`, never `127.0.0.1`** — the loopback
    address REFUSES in ~1ms, so a timeout path never runs and a pending UI stays invisible.
    Confirm with `curl` exit **28** (hang) vs **7** (refused). `dig` does NOT read
    `/etc/hosts`.
  - **Never record a `pass` for a surface that could not render.** Use
    `blocked`/`n-a` — this project has ledgered a `pass` covering an unreachable surface.
  - The verification file's `status` stays `human_needed` until the gate is discharged;
    switching it to `gaps_found` makes the phase vanish from `gsd-sdk query audit-uat`
    entirely.

### Claude's Discretion

- **PICS chunk size, timeout constant selection, and the `oslist` parser.** Constrained to:
  reuse or mirror `STEAM_PICS_BULK_TIMEOUT_MS`'s sizing rationale rather than inventing a
  new constant silently; the parser must treat an absent/empty `oslist` as "write nothing"
  (D-02), never as "no platforms".
- **The exact reason-code vocabulary carried by `steamSyncStatus`.** Constrained to: it must
  carry enough for the inline notice to say something more useful than "failed", and it must
  be representable across both runtimes.
- **The pure resolver's signature and module name for D-09**, and the inline row's exact
  placement relative to `LibraryHeader` / `AlphabetFilter`.
- **Whether the merge-write in D-02 warrants a new named helper on `electronStores.ts`** or
  stays local to the capture module. Either is fine; a wholesale `set()` is not.

4. **`steamSectionGating.test.ts`'s exhaustive matrix is load-bearing.** Any new mode or

## Specific Ideas

- **The operator's key question, asked and answered during discussion (2026-08-16):** *"If
  you can see a Steam game in your library, will you certainly have the platforms
  supported?"* — **No.** Four durable paths keep the signal unresolved for a visible game.
  This is written up as **D-05** and is the governing non-consequence of item 1. It is the
  reason none of 34.14's safety layers may be weakened after this phase lands.

- **Scouting inverted the ROADMAP's item-1 mechanism.** The ROADMAP proposed batching
  `appdetails` and flagged that its rate limits needed measuring. The better question turned
  out to be whether that endpoint returns `platforms` at all in multi-appid mode — and the
  repo already had a proven bulk path (PICS) that sidesteps the question entirely. Recorded
  because it is the same class of event as 34.14's RESEARCH inverting CONTEXT's landmine #1:
  the roadmap's *problem statement* was right and its *direction* was not.

- **The spinner guard is worse than its todo says.** The todo describes a missing failure
  branch. The actual finding is that the flag driving the guard never means "refresh
  running" at all — it is reset to `true` after every unscoped refresh, so the guard reduces
  to "Steam logged in AND Steam library empty", permanently. A plan that adds a failure
  branch to that flag has fixed a symptom of a broken premise.

- **Defect 2b has no "Steam section" to scope to.** The todo says "scope the overlay to the
  Steam section". There is no such section — `libraryToShow` is one merged grid. Taken
  literally the instruction is unimplementable; D-10 satisfies its *intent* by moving the
  indicator into document flow instead.

- **D-13 drops a glyph nobody filed a todo about.** The Windows entry in the modal's
  `platforms` array is `available: true` unconditionally and reads no signal at all — the
  same unbacked-assertion defect as the mac side, just the half that happens to be right
  most of the time. Suppressing the whole row while unresolved catches both.
