<objective>
Build the lower half of the Games tier-2 filter panel: the **Store** facet group, the
**Runnability** facet group, and the single collapsed **More filters** group — all three
collapsed by default, all facet options carrying an own-facet-excluding live count.

Purpose: this is the "power path" half of the panel. Views and Collections (plan 06) answer
"jump me somewhere"; these three groups answer "narrow what I am already looking at". They are the
only surface in the phase that consumes plan 01's `countFor` and plan 04's `connectedStores` /
`runnabilityRows`, and the only place where D-04's connected-account gate and D-12's
host-computability gate become visible behaviour.

Output: four new components under `NavShell/components/`, one shared SCSS file, four test files,
and four new entries in the i18n gate scope. **Nothing is mounted by this plan** — plan 09 owns
`Header/index.tsx` and does the mounting. Expect no visible change in the running app.

This plan is also the phase's sole citation site for **D-13, D-14 and D-15**: the measured
per-store genre coverage, and the resulting decision to drop the Genre facet entirely. It carries
those as negative truths pinned by a source gate, not as prose.
</objective>
