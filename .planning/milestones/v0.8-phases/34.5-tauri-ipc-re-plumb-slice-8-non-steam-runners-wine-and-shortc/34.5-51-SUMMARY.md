  specified.** The contract's own DevTools-console verification method was struck after the
  operator reported console paste doesn't work on this build; rebuilt around the real "Add
  shortcut"/"Add to Steam" buttons under the game page's dots menu (confirmed from source to be
  the exact `fromMenu=true` call site the console command was reconstructing). Independently
  verified from disk: `shortcuts.vdf`'s `Exe` field matches `GAMELIB_SHELL_EXE` byte-for-byte;
  `run.sh`'s body has the correct quoted/percent-encoded shape; two independent real-game launches
  both showed the clean single-instance flow with exactly one `gamelib-shell`/`sidecar.js`
  throughout.
- **Item 4 (Wine) caught a stale-value illusion before the developer confirmed it.** The
  `autoInstallDxvk: true` setting was persisted and the switch showed ON, but zero corresponding

  for the install/backup direction — it never calls `runWineCommand` at all. The developer
  independently confirmed the switch was already on before it was ever touched, matching the
  suspicion exactly. **`U-34.5-30`, `F-34.5-G6-18`.**
