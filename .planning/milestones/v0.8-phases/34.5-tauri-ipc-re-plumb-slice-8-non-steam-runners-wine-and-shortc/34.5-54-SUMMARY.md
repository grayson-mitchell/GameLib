## Decisions Made

- **F-34.5-G6-18's own text was PARTIALLY WRONG, not fully correct.** Two of its three clauses held (the dispatch-marker line, and the DLL copy being `copyFile` not Wine); the third — "never calls `runWineCommand` at all" — was FALSE. This is recorded as `F-34.5-G6-19`, a finding against a finding, rather than silently adopting the routing document's stronger claim. This matters beyond a citation nit: under the uncorrected reading, a DXVK-ON PASS on a future gate would retire nothing, because gate item 4's actual subject is `runWineCommand` executing for a non-Steam runner under the sidecar (research Pitfall 2). Corrected, a genuine ON-click can retire that pitfall.
- **The evidence set is pinned by a test, not left as prose.** Prose citations are exactly what drifted twice already in this cycle (`launcher.ts:1581` → the real emitter is `:1520`, and a `logInfo` assumption → it's actually `logDebug`). A RED-proven jest gate that fails when any of the 5 facts moves is durable in a way a markdown citation is not.
- **Deferred items 29/30/31 state plainly that none of the three findings came from a live run.** All three were found by reading current source during planning — two because the plan's own brief demanded re-verification, one because a census was recounted with the defect's own predicate. This is stated explicitly per the plan's honesty requirement, to avoid a future reader mistaking a source-read finding for a live observation.
