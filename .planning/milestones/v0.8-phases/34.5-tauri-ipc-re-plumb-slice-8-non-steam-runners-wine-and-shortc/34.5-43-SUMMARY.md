## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` in the frontmatter above. In short: build-before-branch is the real contract
(both ends already agreed; only `ipc.ts`'s names were wrong); the registration uses named consts
specifically to make the parameter-order contract cross-check-testable; the electron-reach
measurement used a `git show`-backed out-of-tree script rather than any working-tree mutation
(this repo's post-checkout hook has a known-broken helper-binary download, per the `<sequential_
execution>` instructions); the gate script's new check is independent of the inventory's own
prose warning so the provenance survives prose edits; and both `U-34.5-14` and `F-34.5-G6-10`
stay explicitly OPEN — this plan buckets one channel, it does not audit the whole preload surface.
