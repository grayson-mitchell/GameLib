  (GOG), 3 (Amazon) all FAIL on `spawn ./{legendary,gogdl,nile} ENOENT`; item 5 (Wine) FAILs by

- Diagnosed and recorded a single, fully-verified root cause for the items-1/2/3/5 blocker: under
  the sidecar, `app.getAppPath()` resolves to `process.cwd()` (`src-tauri/`), so `publicDir`
  (`paths.ts:73`) resolves to `src-tauri/public`, which does not exist — the runner binaries at
  `public/bin/{arch}/darwin/{runner}` can never be found, and `archSpecificBinary`'s x64 fallback
  is never existence-checked, which is why the log shows `x64` on an arm64 Mac (a symptom, not a
  separate defect).
- Recorded that the OAuth capture seam itself worked correctly (all three login windows opened,
  the capture channel registered a matched redirect) — the defect is entirely downstream, in the
  runner-binary spawn path, which is why the developer observed a silent hang rather than a
  visible error.
- Propagated the verdict into `34.5-PORTED-CHANNELS.md`: every `unit + LIVE (item N)` cell for
  items 1/2/3/5 flipped from PENDING to FAIL with a pointer to its gate item; item 4's two cells

  developer's choice, independent of the items-1/2/3 defect).
