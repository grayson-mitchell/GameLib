<correction_established_at_planning_time>
`F-34.5-G6-18` (and `34.5-CYCLE7-ROUTING.md` item 3, which restates it) says:

> The install/backup direction (turning the toggle ON) never calls `runWineCommand`; it copies DLLs
> directly via Node's `copyFile`. The corrected evidence line is `tools/index.ts:369`'s
> `installing dxvk-macOS on...`, not `launcher.ts:1581`.

**Two of those three clauses are right. One is wrong.** Read from current source:

1. **RIGHT:** `tools/index.ts:369` emits `logInfo([\`installing ${tool} on...\`, prefix],
   LogPrefix.ToolInstaller)` and it is the correct dispatch marker for the install/backup
   direction. It sits BEFORE the `already installed!` early return at `:371-374`, so it fires even
   when the copy is skipped.
2. **RIGHT:** the DLL copying at `:376-421` uses Node's `copyFile`, not Wine.
3. **WRONG — "never calls `runWineCommand` at all".** Immediately AFTER the copy, `:423-465` runs
   the DLL-**registration** loops, and every iteration calls `runWineCommand` with
   `['reg','add','HKEY_CURRENT_USER\\Software\\Wine\\DllOverrides','/v',<dll>,'/d','native,builtin','/f']`
   — at `tools/index.ts:438` (64-bit) and `:459` (32-bit). So `runWineCommand` **IS** reached on
   the ON direction, and `Running Wine command: ...` **IS** emitted by it.

**Why this matters far beyond a citation nit.** Gate item 4's actual subject — and research
Pitfall 2's actual claim — is "`runWineCommand` has never executed for a non-Steam runner under the
sidecar." Under `F-34.5-G6-18`'s uncorrected reading, a successful DXVK-ON click would exercise
only `copyFile` and would therefore **retire nothing**, and the fifth contract would have shipped
an item that cannot close its own claim. Corrected, a DXVK-ON click on a storefront-authenticated
runner genuinely executes `runWineCommand` and can retire Pitfall 2.

**Two further corrections to the same finding's text:**
- The `Running Wine command:` emitter is at **`src/backend/launcher.ts:1520`**, not `1581`, and it
  is a **`logDebug`**, not a `logInfo`. (`logDebug` does reach `gamelib.log` — the fourth gate's own
  `F-34.5-G6-17` observation was a DEBUG line in that file.)
- The registration loops are `dlls32.forEach(async (dll) => { await runWineCommand(...) })` — an
  **un-awaited** `async` callback inside a synchronous `forEach`. `installRemove` can therefore
  resolve, and the UI switch can flip ON, **before** the `reg add` commands finish. Any gate step
  that reads the log immediately after the switch flips can miss lines that are still arriving.
  A settle window is required, not optional.

Recorded as `F-34.5-G6-19`. It is a finding against a finding — the exact "weight test design over
explanatory fit" failure mode `34.5-CYCLE7-ROUTING.md` standing rule 6 names, caught here only
because the brief itself demanded re-verification.
</correction_established_at_planning_time>
