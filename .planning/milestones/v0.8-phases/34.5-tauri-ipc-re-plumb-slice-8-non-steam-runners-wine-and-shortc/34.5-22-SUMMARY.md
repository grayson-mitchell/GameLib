## Decisions Made

- **F-34.5-G6-02 = shape (c), exists-but-never-reached** — decided from `main.rs:134-145`
  (`oauthCaptureLogin` absent from `LONG_RUNNING_CHANNELS`), `main.rs:288-326`
  (`SidecarState::invoke`'s 60s `rx.recv_timeout` branch), `oauthLoginCapture.ts:62`
  (`DEFAULT_DEADLINE_MS = 300_000`), and `useTauriOAuthLogin.ts:73-103` (the `try` block closing
  at line 97, leaving the `await` at line 99 unguarded).
- **R-A selected over R-B** — not by symmetry-breaking on absent log lines (both readings predict
  zero renderer log output) but by `sidecar_invoke`'s own command contract: a `#[tauri::command]
  async fn` that has returned a value cannot leave that value unsent to the JS caller, which
  forecloses "the promise never settles at all" as a live possibility for the 60s branch.
- **F-34.5-G6-01 is a separate defect** — Epic's own item-1 evidence (0 of 3 `status=captured`)
  means no redirect ever existed for F-34.5-G6-02's mechanism (losing an eventually-settled
  outcome) to have lost; the "greyed out" symptom manifests before any capture is possible.
  Recorded with an explicit caveat that this is inferential (structural non-overlap), not directly
  falsified by a dedicated live counter-test, per the plan's own instruction not to silently pick
  the nicer reading.
- **Recurrence count is 3** (`oauthCaptureLogin`, `humbleStartLogin`, `humbleReconnect`), scoped
  deliberately to channels with a NAMED deadline constant exceeding 60s — `installWineVersion`
  (unbounded-download shape, no named ceiling) was named but explicitly excluded from the "3"
  count as a different risk shape.
