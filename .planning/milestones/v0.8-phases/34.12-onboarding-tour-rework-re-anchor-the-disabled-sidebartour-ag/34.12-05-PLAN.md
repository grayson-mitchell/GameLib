## Decisions this plan resolves (Claude's Discretion, per CONTEXT.md)

**Launcher label: `t('tour.nav.launcher', 'App Tour')`.** A noun phrase, matching every other
row in the panel (Documentation, Accessibility, Console Mode, System Information) rather than
an imperative ("Take the Tour"), which would be the only verb-phrase row and would visually
stand out — the opposite of what D-01 asks for. Icon: `faCircleQuestion` from
`@fortawesome/free-solid-svg-icons` (verified present in the installed version), matching the
help-affordance the retired sidebar used, without reintroducing a second icon-button entry
point.

**The launcher does not use `TourButton`.** `TourButton` renders a MUI `IconButton` inside a
`Tooltip` — precisely the icon-button shape D-01 rejected. Its three-line `handleStartTour`
LOGIC is ported onto a `NavItem` row instead. `TourButton` itself is untouched and keeps
serving `LibraryTour` (D-08).
