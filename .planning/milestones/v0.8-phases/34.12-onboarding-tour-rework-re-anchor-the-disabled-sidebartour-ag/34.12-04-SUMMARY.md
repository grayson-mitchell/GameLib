### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] `destinationCoverage.test.tsx` needed a `NavShellTour` mock after Task 3's mount change**

- **Issue:** `destinationCoverage.test.tsx` imports the real `NavShell/index.tsx` directly and mocks every other child component, but did not mock `NavShellTour` (which didn't exist as an importer of `NavShell/index.tsx` before this plan).
