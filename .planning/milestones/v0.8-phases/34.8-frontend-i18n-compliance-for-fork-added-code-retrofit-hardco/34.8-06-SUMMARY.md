## Decisions Made

- **Scanner fixes as Rule 1, not scope reduction:** the 9 false-positive categories were auto-fixed inline per Rule 1 (bugs in scanner logic), following plan 05's direct precedent of doing the same for 2 categories while performing its own required measurement task. Not fixing them would have published a 335-item backlog materially wrong per D-15's "100% actionable" requirement for gate trust.
- **A fifth disposition (`not-user-facing`) for confirmed non-violations:** none of the plan's four declared dispositions fit 60 of the 124 raw violations (internal enum discriminators like `'MESSAGE'`/`'ERROR'`/`'REDEEMED'`, storage/URL/User-Agent/CSS-selector constants, icon-size/srcSet descriptors, and — for two files — code confirmed already correctly routed through `t()` one dataflow layer deeper than the scanner's fixes trace). Documented as a Rule 2 deviation (a triage document without a way to record "this was never a real violation" is missing something critical for backlog correctness) rather than silently forced into `retrofit` or `glossary`.
- **`split-plan` selected over `absorb`/`split-phase`:** the developer's verbatim direction is recorded in full in `34.8-AUDIT.md`'s `## Scope Decision`. `split-plan` keeps every plan inside its context budget, closes the phase in this pass, and keeps the gate going blocking on schedule with zero weakening of D-12 — confirmed explicitly in the audit document so a later reader cannot mistake this for scope deferral.
- **Split-plan minting deferred to the orchestrator:** per the `split-plan` option's own text, creating `34.8-08a`/`34.8-08b` (etc.) PLAN.md files is the orchestrator's job, immediately after this plan closes. This executor recorded the decision but created no PLAN.md files, per the option's own division of responsibility.
