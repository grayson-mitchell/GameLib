## Task 2: Signal-Independence Decision

The module docstring claimed "a single broken layer can never reproduce the zero-signal state" but the pre-fix implementation was three bare sequential statements sharing one throw source — false in practice. Two options were available: (a) rely solely on the stringification fix (which removes the only *known* throw source) and amend the docstring to say so, or (b) also wrap each of the three signal calls individually so the independence claim holds even against an unknown future throw source (e.g. a caller-supplied `showDialogModal`/`window.api.logError` that itself throws, which this module cannot prove is impossible).

**Decision: (b).** `console.error` and `window.api.logError` are each wrapped in their own `try`/`catch` (silently swallowing, per T-34.2-53 — never rethrow, never await, this runs on an already-failed path). `showDialogModal` is deliberately left unwrapped — it is the last statement and the payoff signal; if it throws there is nothing left to protect, and disabling its ability to surface an error to the caller/console would work against debuggability. This makes the docstring's independence claim accurate rather than merely no-longer-disproven, at the cost of two additional try/catch blocks.
