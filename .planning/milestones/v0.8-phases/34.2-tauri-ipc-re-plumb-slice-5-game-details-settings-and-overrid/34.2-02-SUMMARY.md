## Accomplishments
- `gamedetails/dispatch.ts` holds all 15 runner-generic/runner-pinned handler bodies, moved verbatim from `main.ts` (same statements, comments, log lines, early returns) and importable by a plain Node process
- `gamedetails/overrides.ts` holds `setGameMetadataOverride`, resolving the `sendFrontendMessage`/`backend/ipc` dependency problem via a `setMetadataChangedNotifier` DI seam instead of a second implementation
- `main.ts` now registers all 19 slice channels (16 `addHandler`, 3 `addListener`) as one-line delegations; `requestGameSettings` (D-09) and the two already-clean override pass-throughs are untouched
- 28 direct-call tests prove fastpaths, the empty-object-to-null conversion, `repair`'s offline/happy/reject-path notify counts, `kill`'s call ordering, `getLaunchOptions`'s default-synthesis predicate, and pinned-manager dispatch (legendary/gog/sideload, and to no other runner) -- plus a by-construction source gate (no `electron`/`backend/ipc`/`../ipc`/`../launcher`/`main_window` reference anywhere under `src/backend/gamedetails/`)
