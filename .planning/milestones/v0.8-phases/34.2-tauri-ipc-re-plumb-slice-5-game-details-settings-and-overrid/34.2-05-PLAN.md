<objective>
Register this slice's three `send`-kind channels — `setGameMetadataOverride`,
`changeGameVersionPinnedStatus`, `addNewApp` — and prove each one by the state it
actually changed, never by the absence of an error.

Purpose: `send` channels have no response frame by construction. An unported `send`
channel and a correctly-ported-but-broken one look identical from the outside: nothing
happens, no reject, no 60s timeout, no `UNPORTED_CHANNEL_MARKER`, no log line. That is
this project's own G-30-01 `logoutSteam` failure — a fully green test suite while the
channel did nothing in production. So this plan gets its own wave, and every acceptance
criterion below is a positive side-effect assertion.

The second hazard is symmetrical: an unhandled rejection out of a fire-and-forget
listener kills the sidecar, which has no `process.on('unhandledRejection')` guard.

Output: three `ipcMain.on` registrations, the sidecar-side `metadataChanged` notifier,
and tests that read the store back.
</objective>
