<objective>
Register the 15 invoke-kind per-game channels on the sidecar's `ipcMain` from a new
curated module, and prove each one end-to-end over the real RPC loop.

Purpose: this is the channel port itself. The module imports the REAL bodies plan
34.2-02 extracted — it does not reimplement them — so the two builds cannot drift. The
15 registrations are one-liners, but the transport KIND for each is not: registering an
invoke channel with `ipcMain.on` (or a send channel with `ipcMain.handle`) fails 100%
silently at runtime, with no error, no timeout and no log line. Every line here is
cross-checked against `main.ts`'s own `addHandler`/`addListener` call before it is
written.

`repair` is also the first place D-02's i18next fix is exercised end to end: without plan
34.2-01 its notification body is literally `undefined`.

Output: `gameDetailsFlowRegistration.ts`, one line in `handlers.ts`, a black-box flows
suite, and an import-graph gate.
