Turn the env-gated spike-003 proof into the production StateFlags policy (D-01/D-02/D-03; REQ-23-01/02/03). Replace `process.env.GAMELIB_SPIKE_STATEFLAGS4` with a real completeness predicate `canWriteFullOwnership(...)` that earns `StateFlags=4` only when every spike-proven load-bearing field is present, and otherwise falls back to Phase 21's unchanged `1026` verify-handoff.
