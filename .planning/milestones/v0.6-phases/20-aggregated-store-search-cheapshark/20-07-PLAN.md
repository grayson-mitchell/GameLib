  <what-built>
    A new "Store Search" sidebar entry opening a screen that searches CheapShark live, shows one row per game with the cheapest price labelled "$X USD", stacks store-named ownership badges ("Owned on GOG") plus a coexisting "Key available" pill, lazily expands to a per-store price breakdown, and hands off a chosen deal to the external browser.   </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    1. `pnpm start` (or the project's dev-run command). In the left sidebar, confirm a "Store Search" entry appears as a sibling directly under "Deals" (magnifying-glass-dollar icon), always visible.
    2. Open it. With no query typed, confirm the centered explanatory prompt with the large muted search icon (STORESEARCH-08 prompt state).
    3. Type 1–2 characters: confirm NO request fires and the screen stays on the prompt (min-3-char gate, STORESEARCH-02).
    4. Type a full title you OWN on Steam (e.g. one in your Steam library) — after ~400ms confirm results appear; confirm the matching row shows an "Owned on Steam" badge and the price reads "$14.99 USD"-style with a visible, same-size, same-weight "USD" (NOT a muted/smaller suffix) (STORESEARCH-04/05, D-13 correctness).
    5. Type a title you own on GOG/Epic/Amazon (not Steam): confirm the badge names that store via fuzzy match (STORESEARCH-05). If you hold an unredeemed Humble key for a searched title, confirm a "Key available" pill shows ALONGSIDE any ownership badge (STORESEARCH-06, D-07).
    6. Expand a result (chevron): confirm a row-scoped spinner then a per-store breakdown; confirm mapped stores (Steam/GOG/Epic) show their runner icon and unmapped stores are text-only (STORESEARCH-03).
    7. Click a per-store deal row: confirm your EXTERNAL browser opens (not an in-app webview) at the correct store's redirect — NOT a 404 (this is the dealID-verbatim Pitfall 1 check, STORESEARCH-07/D-08/D-09).
    8. Simulate a provider failure (e.g. disconnect network, or block www.cheapshark.com) and search: confirm an INLINE retryable error banner (distinct from the no-results state) appears while the search box stays usable; reconnect and click "Retry search" to confirm recovery (STORESEARCH-08, D-14 fail-soft).
    9. Search a nonsense string that returns zero matches: confirm the no-results state (no icon, distinct from both the prompt and the error banner).
  </how-to-verify>
