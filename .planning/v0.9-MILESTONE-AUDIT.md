## Tech debt

The full list is in the frontmatter. The items most worth acting on are:

1. **S9 (56/59)**: a SteamStub title installed while CrossOver is absent may later launch in DRM
   mode on a Wine-Staging-initialised bottle. Fix by calling `prepareBottleForEngine({ next: crossover })`
   in the DRM branch, or refuse with a message.
2. **57 WR-09**: the relay reads the request body unbounded when `Content-Length` is missing. It is network-facing.
3. **56 WR-02**: an execFile handle holds the sidecar event loop up to 3 s. This touches the sidecar exit contract.
4. **Stale records**: the 52-VERIFICATION body, the 58-VERIFICATION follow_ups, the 52/53 UAT `failed`
   rows that were superseded, and the comments in `shimCoverage.ts` and `knownFixReport.ts`.
5. **Test health**: the `macArchKnownFixRouting` failure and three pre-existing sidecar suites. Their current
   state is unverified.
