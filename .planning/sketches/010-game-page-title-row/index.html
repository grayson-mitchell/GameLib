About

Descend into a flooded city lit only by the lanterns you carry. Every chamber you relight redraws the map, opening shortcuts and sealing others behind rising water.

Trade with the tide-keepers, bind spirits to your lantern for new traversal powers, and decide which districts the sea finally reclaims.
