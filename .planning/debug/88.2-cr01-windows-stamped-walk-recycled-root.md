## Resolution

root_cause: In gamelib-proc kill_tree.rs `mod win`, kill_game_tree establishes the recorded process's identity (pid + creation stamp) through throwaway handles (probe::is_alive, probe::start_time, then terminate_stamped_leader's handle, dropped on return) and afterwards acts on the bare pid: collect_descendants re-seeds `accepted` from `(pid, stamp)` without proof on each of up to WALK_PASSES passes, the Job result is unchecked (WR-04), and the leader outcome is discarded (IN-05). Contributing conditions (AND-gate: yes): the Game Job name is gone (A5 failed, so the walk path runs), the leader's pid is free for reuse once its last handle closes, and an unrelated process takes it and spawns children inside the walk window. The snapshot-to-OpenProcess race for child pids is the same unbound-identity defect one level down; the age check does not defend it.
