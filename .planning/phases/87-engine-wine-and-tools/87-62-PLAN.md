<objective>
Fix the two wine-manager findings of the 87-59 gate: F2, a never-installed release cannot be installed because the Rust
argument decoder rejects the stored entry's shape, and F4, the Wine Manager list does not follow a Rust install or remove
until a restart.

Purpose: item 4 of the gate scored `issue` on both. The parse decision (user decision 2, 2026-11-24, "decide: default the
missing keys at parse, or normalize at the store/producer — justify") is taken here: default at parse. The renderer sends
exactly the stored entry; the refresh merge (`manager/releases.rs` `merge`, as the TS before it) appends feed releases
without those three keys, and stores written by released builds hold them that way. Normalizing at the store would
rewrite on-disk bytes that released builds wrote and that golden_wine_manager pins (D-07, D-08); defaulting at the
boundary changes no byte on disk and keeps every other rejection.
Output: the parse fix with its tests, the located and fixed F4 break with its regression test.
</objective>
