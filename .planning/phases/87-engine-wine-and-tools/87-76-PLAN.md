<objective>
Skip the generic offline warning when the engine is going to refuse the launch (RF5; the user's 2026-11-25 decision), using
the same offline-gate verdict the launch step applies.

Purpose: in re-run 1 item 1 the renderer's "This game might not work properly offline" warning (`launch()` in
`helpers/library.ts`, shown when `notPlayableOffline`) appeared before the engine's refusal, which is pointless when the
launch is certain to be refused. The renderer has no verdict today. The engine state (`getEngineState`, a scheduled D-23
resolver over `engine_state_get`) already derives the game's bottle, effective override and CrossOver state, so it carries the
verdict as a read-time field; the renderer reads it fresh before deciding on the warning.
Output: the shared gate inputs, the state field with its tests, and the renderer change with its tests.
</objective>
