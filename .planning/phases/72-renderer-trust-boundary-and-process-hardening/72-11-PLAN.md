<objective>
Replace quit-time and stop-time name-pattern kills with tracked-PID kills (D-13): a minimal registry fed by the two spawn
helpers, four call sites switched, `killPattern` deleted.

Purpose: `pkill -f legendary` (and Windows `Stop-Process -name`) kills unrelated processes, including other users' tools
or a second launcher. Output: `trackedChildren.ts`, spawn hooks, call-site swaps, tests.

Intended behaviour change (record in SUMMARY): `handleExit` now calls `killAllTracked()`, so tracked `runWineCommand` children (games) are also SIGTERMed at quit, not only the legendary/gogdl/nile CLIs. This is the D-20 parity the operator accepted; Unix already group-kills them at shutdown.

Intended behaviour change (Pitfall 11, record in SUMMARY): a runner this sidecar did not spawn (e.g. left over from a
previous session) is no longer killed on quit.
</objective>
