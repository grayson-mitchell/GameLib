<objective>
Trim the main-window capability to what the renderer uses (D-10): drop `opener:default`, `updater:default` and the
scoped `shell:allow-execute`, set `withGlobalTauri: false`, replace the opener plugin's injected link-click script with a
renderer handler that goes through the validated `openExternalUrl` channel, and prove it with a live boot that also
covers the react-router 7 / i18next-http-backend 4 bumps from 72-02 (D-19).

Purpose: remote content rendered in `main` inherits every grant; `opener:default` was a direct webview path to the OS
opener that bypassed every check. Output: config edits, opener builder switch, link handler + test, updated pins, live check.

Hidden dependency (RESEARCH Evidence 1, Pitfall 1): tauri-plugin-opener 2.5.4 injects `init-iife.js`, a click listener
that calls `plugin:opener|open_url` for `<a target=_blank>` and modifier-clicks. ChangelogModal (`linkTarget="_blank"`,
`ChangelogModal/index.tsx:47`) and sanitized game changelogs (`GameChangeLog/index.tsx:38`) rely on it. Dropping the
grant without the replacement silently kills those links.
</objective>
