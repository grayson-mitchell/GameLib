<objective>
Close WR-09 and IN-03 in the Rust delta engine. WR-09: any old-manifest load failure, including a
timeout, a 5xx or not_signed_in, silently becomes a whole-install reconcile that also drops the
retired depots, so obsolete files stay. IN-03: run_commit deletes retired files even after a rename
failed, leaving a tree that is neither the old build nor the new one with its removals applied.

Purpose: D-14's fallback is for a missing old manifest, not a flaky network; a failed commit must
leave the most recoverable tree.
Output: a definitive-vs-transient gate and a guarded removal loop, both with integration tests.
</objective>
