<objective>
Close WR-04 / UAT 12: after Steam finished a registered update in 3 s, GameLib kept the badge and,
on a second click of the now-current game, showed "Updating game" with a grey Play until the
40-tick quiet end. Causes: the update-state cache has no ACF re-read for registered games; the poll
never matches completion when no pending state was seen and the build was already current; the
quiet end does not invalidate; and a refresh already in flight (started by the queue's hand-off
'done') can write back a stale update list after the completion removed the badge.

Purpose: D-11's promise that GameLib reflects a Steam-owned update.
Output: backend completion fixes and frontend refresh/refetch fixes, with tests. No new user-facing strings.
</objective>
