# Phase 34.2: Tauri IPC re-plumb slice 5 — game details, settings and overrides - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-07-25
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Port the **game details, settings and overrides** IPC cluster (26 channels) onto the
Tauri build, following SEAM.md's Incremental-Port Checklist. This is the fifth re-plumb
slice. Additive and reversible — `npm start` (Electron) and `pnpm tauri:dev` must both
work when the phase closes.

**Shape of the work — established during scouting:**
- **19 of the 26 channels are handler bodies inside `src/backend/main.ts`**, the Electron
  entry module the sidecar can never import. Same situation Phase 34.1 faced; same answer
  (**D-03**: extract, per 34.1 D-07).
- **7 channels live in 5 small feature modules** (`anticheat/`, `crossover_index/`,
  `wiki_game_info/`, `recent_games/`, `storeSearch/`). Four of those have clean importable
  exports and need **no extraction at all** (**D-04**).
- The slice's real difficulty is **not** the channels. It is **two startup wirings the
  sidecar never runs** (**D-02**, **D-07**) that this slice's channels are the first to
  expose — both of which are already silently degrading paths shipped in Phases 30/32.

**In scope — all 26 channels, no reassignments (D-08):**
- **Runner-generic dispatch** (**D-01**): `getGameInfo`, `getExtraInfo`, `getGameSettings`,
  `isGameAvailable`, `kill`, `repair`, `changeInstallPath`, `getLaunchOptions`,
  `changeGameVersionPinnedStatus`.
- **Metadata overrides**: `getGameMetadataOverride`, `setGameMetadataOverride`,
  `getAllGameOverrides` (plus `attachOverrides` inside `getGameInfo`). The
  `gameOverridesStore` is **already registered** in the sidecar store layer (Phase 29).
- **Runner-pinned channels, ported at parity** (**D-08**): `readConfig` (Legendary),
  `getGameOverride` / `getGameSdl` (Legendary), `getAvailableCyberpunkMods` /
  `setCyberpunkModConfig` (GOG), `addNewApp` (sideload).
- **Enrichment surfaces**: `getWikiGameInfo`, `getAnticheatInfo`, `getKnownFixes`,
  `getCrossoverIndex`, `searchStores`, `getStoreSearchDeals`, `getStoreSearchStoreMap`,
  `removeRecent`.
- **Two sidecar bootstrap wirings** (**D-02**, **D-07**) — i18next init and
  `fetchLastestReleases()`. These are **fixes to already-shipped defects**, not new
  features, and they are prerequisites for honest behavior in this slice.
- **Three small extractions out of host files** (**D-03**, **D-05**, **D-06**):
  the 19 `main.ts` bodies, `readKnownFixes` out of `launcher.ts`, and
  `buildCrossoverRatingMap` out of `crossover_index/ipc_handler.ts`.
- `34.2-PORTED-CHANNELS.md` declared list + SEAM.md §3→§1 move.

**Explicitly OUT of scope:**
- **Deduplicating `getGameSettings` against the already-ported `requestGameSettings`**
  (**D-09**) — port at parity, record as a cutover cleanup.
- **Any live/hardware gate as a phase-closing condition** (**D-11**) — unit-proven,
  live UAT deferred.
- **The other `releasesInfoReady` consumers' behavior** — `tools/dxmt.ts` and
  `wine/manager/ipc_handler.ts` are starved by the same gap D-07 closes and will
  benefit from it, but their channels belong to slices 6/8 and are not ported here.
- **Optimizing `getCrossoverIndex`'s per-game `loadIndex`/`buildMaps`** — noted as a
  real inefficiency, deferred; D-10 exempts the channel from the invoke timeout instead.
- Any change to the Electron build's *behavior*. As in 34.1, this slice **does touch
  Electron files** (three extractions) — the invariant is behavioral, not textual.

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Runner breadth

- **D-01 — Port the dispatch channels RUNNER-GENERIC, all six managers.** `getGameInfo`,
  `getExtraInfo`, `getGameSettings`, `isGameAvailable`, `kill`, `repair`,
  `changeInstallPath`, `getLaunchOptions` and `changeGameVersionPinnedStatus` are all
  `libraryManagerMap[runner]` dispatches; port them verbatim. **Rationale:** Phase 32
  **D-02** already established that narrowing buys zero import-graph savings —
  `storeManagers/index.ts` force-constructs all six managers in the sidecar today
  regardless. Narrowing here would mean *adding* a runner guard that Electron does not
  have, i.e. deliberately diverging for nothing, and then deleting it at cutover.
  Rejected: Steam-narrow with the rest rejecting per Invariant B (Phase 31 D-01's
  precedent, which does not transfer — D-01 there avoided pulling Epic/GOG/Amazon *tool
  plumbing* into the graph; here the managers are already constructed).
  **MANDATORY declaration rider:** `34.2-PORTED-CHANNELS.md` must record per-channel
  that for **Steam titles** several of these are inert *in Electron already* —
  `SteamGame.repair()` logs `"SteamGame.repair not implemented until Phase 2"`
  (`storeManagers/steam/games.ts:1610`), and `SteamLibrary.changeVersionPinnedStatus`
  (`library.ts:797`) / `getLaunchOptions` (`library.ts:907`) are underscore-arg stubs.
  Porting them faithfully is correct; letting a later reader parse "ported" as
  "repair works for Steam games" is not.

- **D-02 — INITIALIZE i18next in the sidecar bootstrap.** `i18next.use(Backend).init({...})`
  exists in exactly one place in the whole backend — `main.ts:460`, inside
  `app.whenReady()` — so under the headless sidecar the shared singleton is never
  initialized. **Verified empirically against the installed i18next 22.5.1:**
  `i18next.t('notify.error.reparing', 'Error Repairing')` on the uninitialized singleton
  returns **`undefined`** — it does **not** throw, and it does **not** fall back to the
  inline English default. This slice's `repair` handler
  (`main.ts:1150`/`1154`) would therefore fire `notify({ title, body: undefined })`.
  **The blast radius is far wider than this slice: 103 `i18next.t()` call sites across 16
  backend files, and `downloadqueue.ts`, `utils.ts`, `storeManagers/steam/library.ts`,
  `steam/depotErrors.ts` and `storeManagerCommon/games.ts` are ALREADY in the sidecar's
  import graph** — so the Tauri build has been emitting `undefined` user-facing strings on
  paths shipped in Phases 30 and 32. Phase 34.1's **CR-01** found the `changeLanguage`
  half of this bug (which throws loudly); the `t()` half fails silently and was never
  noticed. One small bootstrap addition fixes all of it and stops every later slice
  inheriting the landmine. Rejected: inline English literals for `repair`'s two calls
  (leaves 100+ sites broken and makes every future slice re-solve it per-channel);
  declaring it and deferring to Phase 35 (ships knowingly-broken notification text).
  **Planner notes:** init must complete before any handler can run (ordering matters, same
  class as `initOnlineMonitor()`'s ordering constraint at `bootstrap.ts:118-124`); the
  sidecar must resolve locale files from its own resource path — this is the
  `publicdir-getapppath-chunking` / bundled-asset family of gotcha, so verify the path
  resolves in a **packaged** build, not just under `tauri:dev`.

### Handler home — where the ported code lives

- **D-03 — EXTRACT the 19 `main.ts` handler bodies into shared Electron-free modules.**
  Carries forward Phase 34.1 **D-07/D-08** unchanged: export handler bodies as plain
  functions; `main.ts` keeps its `addHandler`/`addListener` lines as one-line delegations;
  the new curated sidecar module registers the same functions on `electronStub`'s
  `ipcMain`. One source of truth, no silent drift between builds. **34.1 already gave up
  the textually-untouched-Electron-entry-point invariant and re-expressed
  `electronUntouched.test.ts` as behavioral rather than textual — this slice pays no new
  price for the same move.** Follow the `src/backend/appshell/{language,releases,themes}.ts`
  shape 34.1 created (note the directory is lowercase `appshell`). Rejected:
  re-implementing the 19 bodies fresh in a curated sidecar module with `main.ts` untouched
  (ships two implementations that can drift, and reverses a decision made one phase ago);
  hybrid extract-only-where-non-trivial (two patterns in one slice, a judgment call per
  channel).

- **D-04 — For the 5 feature modules, import the UNDERLYING module, never the
  `ipc_handler.ts`.** The `ipc_handler.ts` files call `addHandler` from `backend/ipc`;
  side-effect-importing them would drag `backend/ipc` into the sidecar's graph, which is
  exactly what the curated-import discipline (Phase 30 **D-08**, 34.1 **D-09**) exists to
  prevent. Four of the five need **no extraction at all** — the functions are already
  clean exports:
  `anticheat/utils.ts:92` → `gameAnticheatInfo`;
  `wiki_game_info/wiki_game_info.ts:20` → `getWikiGameInfo`;
  `recent_games/recent_games.ts:50` → `removeRecentGame`;
  `storeSearch/cheapshark.ts:121/157/182` → `getStoreMap` / `searchGames` / `getGameDeals`.
  **No file under `src/backend/sidecar/` imports the real `electron` module** — unchanged.

- **D-05 — Extract `readKnownFixes` out of `launcher.ts`.** `getKnownFixes` calls
  `readKnownFixes` at `launcher.ts:1042`. The function is 20 fully self-contained lines
  (`join(fixesPath, …)` + `existsSync` + `readFileSync` + `JSON.parse` + `logWarning`,
  zero coupling to the launch pipeline), but it sits inside a **2170-line** module that
  Phase 30 **explicitly kept out of the sidecar graph**
  (`steamFlowRegistration.ts:22` — "Deliberately does NOT import `launcher.ts`'s
  `launchEventCallback`"). Move it to its own small Electron-free module; `launcher.ts`
  imports it back for its own `installFixes`. Rejected: reassigning `getKnownFixes` to
  slice 8 under the D-14 precedent (defensible — known-fixes is launcher-adjacent — but
  it leaves a game-details surface unported and needs an inventory edit in two lists for a
  20-line function); importing `launcher.ts` into the sidecar (drags `prepareWineLaunch` /
  `runWineCommand` / `callRunner` and the whole Wine pipeline in for a JSON read).

- **D-06 — Extract `buildCrossoverRatingMap` out of `crossover_index/ipc_handler.ts`.**
  It is the one feature module where the reusable function is defined in the *same file*
  as its `addHandler` call, so importing it would be a side-effect import (the thing D-04
  forbids). Move it to a sibling; its own dependencies (`isCrossoverIndexEligible`,
  `getCodeweaversFromIndex` in `crossover_index/index.ts`) are already clean exports.

### Enrichment surfaces

- **D-07 — WIRE `fetchLastestReleases()` into the sidecar bootstrap.** `getAnticheatInfo`
  reads a data file that is downloaded by a module-scope
  `backendEvents.on('releasesInfoReady', …)` hook (`anticheat/ipc_handler.ts:12`). The
  **only** emitter is `utils/releases.ts:19`, inside `fetchLastestReleases()`, which is
  called **only from `main.ts`** — never in the sidecar bootstrap. So under Tauri the
  event never fires, the file is never downloaded, and the channel returns `null` with a
  log warning: **a channel that structurally cannot work**, which is exactly what Phase 32
  **D-01** and 34.1 **D-11** refused to ship. Call it in the bootstrap as `main.ts` does.
  Same family as the Phase 33 finding that Electron `app.whenReady()` inits are not
  auto-run in the sidecar, and symmetric with **D-02**. **Precondition already met:**
  `fetchLastestReleases` wraps its work in `runOnceWhenOnline`, and the bootstrap already
  calls `initOnlineMonitor()` (`bootstrap.ts:124`) — so wire it **after** that call.
  Its own `process.env.CI === 'e2e'` and `isWindows` early-returns are preserved as-is.
  Rejected: porting at parity with a declared cold cache. **Declaration rider (from the
  rejected third option, kept because it is honest bookkeeping):**
  `34.2-PORTED-CHANNELS.md` must record that `gameAnticheatInfo` matches on
  `info.storeIds.epic?.namespace` and early-returns on Windows — so even fully primed it
  returns `null` for Steam titles on every platform, and `null` for everything on Windows.
  "Ported and primed" must not read as "anticheat badges appear for Steam games."
  **Bonus, not scope:** this also un-starves the `tools/dxmt.ts` and
  `wine/manager/ipc_handler.ts` listeners that slices 6/8 will need.

- **D-10 — Exempt `getCrossoverIndex` from the 60s invoke timeout; MEASURE
  `getWikiGameInfo` before exempting it.** Renderer→sidecar invokes are bounded at
  `INVOKE_TIMEOUT = 60s` (`src-tauri/src/main.rs:102`), with an established exemption list
  `LONG_RUNNING_CHANNELS` (`main.rs:121`) added by Phase 30 **CR-03**, currently
  `install`, `updateGame`, `uninstall`, `checkGameUpdates`, `refreshLibrary`.
  `getCrossoverIndex` fans out over **every game in every manager** — the same
  library-wide shape as the two already-exempt channels — and calls `loadIndex` +
  `buildMaps` *per game*. Add it. For `getWikiGameInfo` (four external APIs per game on a
  cold cache), **measure a cold-cache call during research** and exempt it only if it
  genuinely runs long: a per-game enrichment call that takes 60s is a bug worth seeing,
  not a timeout worth raising. **Adding strings to an existing list is not a new Rust
  arm** — the zero-new-Rust-arms expectation holds. Rejected: exempting both up front
  (turns a wedged network fetch into a never-settling promise, losing the guardrail on the
  one network-facing path); keeping the bound and hoisting `getCrossoverIndex`'s per-game
  `loadIndex`/`buildMaps` out of the loop (a real and worthwhile fix, but optimization
  work inside a port slice — recorded as a deferred idea instead).

### Slice membership

- **D-08 — Port ALL 26 channels at parity; NO reassignments to other slices.** The six
  runner-pinned strays stay here: `readConfig` (Legendary refresh + Epic `displayName`,
  despite its generic name — `main.ts:988`), `getGameOverride` / `getGameSdl`
  (Legendary; frontend callers are the Epic download dialog only,
  `InstallModal/DownloadDialog/index.tsx:444`/`446`), `getAvailableCyberpunkMods` /
  `setCyberpunkModConfig` (GOG; `ModifyInstallModal/GOG/index.tsx:211`, and
  `setCyberpunkModConfig` has **zero** frontend callers), and `addNewApp` (sideload;
  `SideloadDialog/index.tsx:182`). Consistent with **D-01** — the managers are all
  constructed in the sidecar anyway, so these are cheap one-line delegations with no
  import-graph cost. Avoids a second boundary rule reasoned about per-channel, and means
  the Phase 35 gate closes with no residue of skipped channels. Declare in
  `34.2-PORTED-CHANNELS.md` that they are unreachable in a Steam-only workflow.
  Rejected: reassigning `addNewApp` to slice 8 under the 34.1 **D-14** precedent
  (defensible on domain grounds — it is the sideload runner — but it buys nothing here
  and costs an inventory edit in two lists); leaving all six rejecting per Invariant B
  (blocks the Phase 35 completion gate on six channels someone must revisit).

- **D-09 — Leave `requestGameSettings` at parity; do NOT dedupe against
  `getGameSettings`.** They are near-duplicates — both end at
  `getGame(appName).getSettings()` — but `requestGameSettings` (already ported, Phase 31,
  `settingsFlowRegistration.ts:113`) has no `runner` param and therefore carries a
  Steam-detection workaround (`steamLibrary.has(appName)`, `main.ts:997-1013`) added
  during the `debug/steam-bottle-game-no-launch` investigation. That is a shipped,
  hardware-proven path fixing a real bottle-launch bug; this port does not touch it. The
  duplication is Electron's, not something this slice introduces. Rejected: sharing one
  `getGameSettingsFor(appName, runner)` body (removes drift risk but modifies a proven
  path); recording it as a deferred cutover cleanup **is** adopted — see Deferred Ideas.

### Sign-off

- **D-11 — Unit-proven; live UAT deferred.** Carries forward Phase 30 **D-04** →
  34.1 **D-15**. **But state the difference plainly rather than copying 34.1's wording:**
  34.1's honest tension was that its deliverable was *visual* and jest structurally could
  not see it. This slice is almost entirely **data in / data out** — 26 channels with
  assertable return shapes — so "wired and unit-proven" is genuinely close to "works".
  **The two honest exceptions are D-02 and D-07**: startup wirings whose effects only
  appear in a live sidecar boot, and which unit tests will mock away. The deferred-UAT
  item must name them specifically — "a translated notification string actually renders"
  and "the anticheat data file actually appears on disk" — rather than a generic
  live-UAT placeholder. Rejected: adding a narrow live boot gate for exactly those two
  (a genuinely good idea, and the class of defect Phase 33 D-13's gate exists for —
  recorded as a deferred idea, and the planner should treat it as the highest-value
  optional gate if scope allows); a full live gate on the slice (mostly redundant with
  tests that can genuinely assert these return values).

### Claude's Discretion

- **The exact module decomposition** under D-03 (how many shared modules, their paths, how
  many sidecar registration modules this slice adds) is a planner call, provided the
  modules are Electron-free, `main.ts` keeps its registration lines, and D-04's
  curated-import discipline holds.
- **`setGameMetadataOverride`'s `sendFrontendMessage('metadataChanged', …)` push**
  (`main.ts:1530`) should ride the existing generic `frontend_message` relay, exactly as
  Phases 30/32's push riders do. **Expect zero new Rust arms.** If it needs new Rust work,
  stop and ask why (the Phase 29 `storeChanged` precedent). Same for `repair`'s
  `sendGameStatusUpdate`, which already has a relay.
- **`kill`** calls `callAbortController(appName)` then `.stop()`. Both halves already live
  in the sidecar — `launch` was ported in Phase 30 (so the process handle is there) and
  `abort` in Phase 34.1 — so this should be genuinely real, not a no-op. Confirm during
  research rather than assuming.
- Whether the D-02/D-07 bootstrap wirings earn their own plan/wave ahead of the channel
  ports is a planner call — but they are **prerequisites** for honest behavior in
  `repair` and `getAnticheatInfo`, so they must land first in dependency order.
- Whether `getWikiGameInfo` needs the D-10 exemption follows from the measurement; the
  "measure before exempting" boundary is fixed.
