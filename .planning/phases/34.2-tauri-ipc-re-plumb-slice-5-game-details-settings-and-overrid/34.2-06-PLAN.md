<objective>
Register the eight enrichment channels from a second curated module, and settle the
D-10 timeout question with a measurement rather than an assumption.

Purpose: these are the six externally-network-facing surfaces of the slice plus two local
reads. Their porting risk is not logic — it is the import graph (four of the five feature
modules must be reached through their underlying file, never their `ipc_handler.ts`) and
the error contract (`storeSearch` deliberately rethrows so the frontend owns the "provider
failed" state; a bare pass-through would silently change that). `getAnticheatInfo` is also
the payoff for plan 34.2-01's D-07 wiring — before that, it structurally could not return
data at all.

over every game in every manager and gets exempted from the 60s bound; `getWikiGameInfo`
gets MEASURED first, because a per-game enrichment call that takes 60s is a bug worth
seeing, not a timeout worth raising.

Output: `enrichmentFlowRegistration.ts`, one `handlers.ts` line, one string in a Rust
array, a flows suite, and a recorded latency number.
