# Phase 34.2 Plan 09: Sidecar unhandledRejection Hardening Summary

**A single unreadable anticheat-cache file can no longer take down the whole sidecar process: the rejection is caught at the call site, backed by a log-only process-level `unhandledRejection` guard, both pinned by an 8-test suite that drives the real listener with a rejecting promise and proves zero unhandled rejections fire.**

Group 2 unit-tests the guard's contract in isolation (idempotency, non-throw, stderr fallback when `logWarning` itself throws).
