This phase ran **five** reviews (round 1 plus `34.2-REVIEW-GAP-CYCLE-{1,2,3,4}.md`), and **each
round restarts numbering at `CR-01`/`WR-01`**. Round 1's `WR-06` (path traversal in
`readKnownFixes`) and round 3's `WR-06` (a hoisted-factory TDZ naming nit) are unrelated findings
sharing a string. **Every row below is keyed by `(round, ID)`.** Anything that keys on the bare ID
merges findings that have nothing to do with each other.

**This ledger covers round 1 only.** The four gap-cycle reviews are NOT dispositioned here. Two of
them still read `issues_found`, and none of the four carries an explorer badge at all —
`artifactKind()` matches `name === kind || name.endsWith('-' + kind)`, so `-REVIEW-GAP-CYCLE-N.md`
matches nothing. They are invisible to tooling and remain outstanding.
