# Phase 88.1 Plan 10: System information, runner versions and the native channels Summary

**`getSystemInfo` and `copySystemInfoToClipboard` are native channels of the Rust `info` slice over `gamelib-info`: the runners' `--version` probes, a byte-exact port of `formatSystemInfo` and the npm `filesize`, and the single-flight cache all replay golden/sysinfo with nothing deferred.**

- **Tasks:** 3 (tracer, versions/format/copy, host smoke)
- **Commits:** 3 (`13c464fbb` crate, `2561226e9` channel wiring, `0c973787a` host smoke), measured from the ledger base `cb6135b04`
- **Completed:** 2026-11-25

## Accomplishments

- **`sysinfo::cache::SystemInfoCache`** (`createSystemInfoCache`): one value and one in-flight read per `InfoCtx`. `cache=true` callers share the in-flight fetch or take the stored value with no probe; `cache=false` always fetches and refreshes the value; a leader that is dropped (shutdown) answers its waiters `Cancelled` instead of leaving them waiting.
- **`sysinfo::versions`**: `resolve_runner_bin` is the pure `archSpecificBinary` and `get*Bin` rule (alt setting, bundled `public/bin/<arch>/<platform>[/<name>]/<name>`, the macOS onedir rule for legendary, gogdl and nile only, `.exe` on Windows, x64 fallback, stale-flat-layout error, `fixAsarPath`); `runner_versions` runs the four `--version` probes together in the TS's start order as Helper `ProcSpec`s (comet 5 s, the others 30 s); `parse_legendary_version` implements `/"([\d.]*)".*"(.*)"$/m` without a regex engine; the app version line reads `versionNames` from the compiled-in `package.json`.
- **`sysinfo::format`**: `filesize(bytes, base2)` ports filesize 10.1.6's exponent, rounding, carry and `kB` rules; `format_system_info` reproduces the template per platform (Flatpak and AppImage lines on Linux only, `undefined` for a missing GPU field as JS prints it).
- **`fetch_system_info` / `get_system_info`**: the machine probes, then the version probes, then (last, as the TS did) the no-CPU check; the production wrapper builds `RealHostFacts` and `SystemTxtResolver` and goes through the context's cache.
- **Shell**: `info_dispatch::get_system_info` (optional boolean decode, invalid-args marker, `getSystemInfo: not ready`, blocking pool, `guarded`) and `copy_system_info_to_clipboard` (info slice worker, cached read, format, clipboard write, fixed failure lines). `install` hands the context the `SpecProcRunner`, `FLATPAK_ID`/`APPIMAGE`/gamescope facts and an alt-binary reader over the engine's global settings.
- **TS**: both zod entries, the `getSystemInfo` handle and the `copySystemInfoToClipboard` listener are deleted; the census (clipboard send 2 to 1, settings invoke 10 to 9), the settings registration's sha256 pin and its channel set, and the schema and flow tests follow. The three renderer types moved to `src/common/types/systemInfo.ts`; the six SystemInfo screens and `settings.ts` import them there, and the backend re-imports them from the same file.
- **`tests/sysinfo_host.rs`**: five real-host reads (CPU model, memory, release, env facts, os-release paths) plus a Linux-only OS probe test.
