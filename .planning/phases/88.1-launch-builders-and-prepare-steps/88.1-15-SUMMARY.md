# Phase 88.1 Plan 15: prepareLaunch and prepareWineLaunch pointed at Rust Summary

**The Launcher's `prepareLaunch` and `prepareWineLaunch` are now thin adapters over three rustInvoke shims: they gather the TS-only facts, run the Rust step (the wine one in two halves around the TS store-setup hook), replay Rust's returned log into the same `LogWriter` in order, and answer the old return shape. The TS prepare bodies, Discord presence leftovers, the legendary offline-mode rule and seven retired shims are gone.**

### Design points left open

- The prepare reply `log` entries are narrowed in the adapter (`level` one of info, warning, error, debug; `parts` an array) and a malformed reply throws a named error rather than a half-written log.
- `writeLaunchLog` calls `logWriter[level](...parts)`, so a part that is itself an array (as `['Launching', title]` is) stays one message, exactly as the TS passed it.
- `Created/Updated Wineprefix at` is written by the adapter through the backend logger (it names the prefix path), not returned by Rust, as 88.1-12 decided.
