# Phase 34.13 — Code Review (iteration 3)

Re-review at **standard** depth of the 18 iteration-2 fix commits
(`996c192d1`..`6a0066175`, fix report `34.13-REVIEW-FIX2.md`), plus a **first-pass

Scope is the union of the 14 SUMMARY artifacts' `key_files` and every file touched by

## Executive summary

Iteration 2 claimed 29 of 32 findings fixed. **Most of those fixes are real** — the
reviewers independently re-derived known-bad inputs and confirmed them. The
highest-risk claim in the whole fix pass (C-07/C-08/C-09, "six gates made able to
fail, proven against derived known-bad inputs") **held up under independent
re-derivation: all six gates tripped.**

What iteration 3 found instead falls into three groups.

**1. The new uninstall commit carries two blockers.** `539bc979c` was never reviewed.
Its deletion safety is mostly sound — the SharedDepots real-filesystem test is
genuinely good — but two reachable paths report a successful uninstall while deleting
nothing.

**2. Five iteration-2 fixes are half-closed, and three of them introduced new
defects.** The recurring shape is **incomplete enumeration**: the fix was applied to
the file where the finding was reported and not to the sibling file with the same
code, or it closed one of three corners of a resolver, or it cleared state that only
a differently-keyed effect can restore. Two fixes are net regressions against the
pre-fix behaviour.

**3. Both fixer-declared skips (A-04, A-09) have expired rationales**, and B-WR-09's
procedurally-valid skip now has a wider blast radius than when it was deferred.

Three of the fix pass's own new gates guard nothing — one is a literal tautology
(`toBeGreaterThanOrEqual(0)`), one is `describe.skip` on every CI run, and one
**mandates the vulnerability** it was written to close.
