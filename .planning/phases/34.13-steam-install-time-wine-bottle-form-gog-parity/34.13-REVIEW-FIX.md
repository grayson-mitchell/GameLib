Had I followed the review literally, the phase would have shipped a CI-red commit and a permanently untranslatable string in the wrong namespace.

**CR-02(b) — widening the gate scope exposed two blocking violations.** Adding the eight files to `meta/i18nGateScope.json` immediately failed the blocking hardcoded-string gate on `consoleSteamTarget.ts:112,114` — the bare `return 'consoleMode.steamInstall…'` catalog keys. This is exactly the "re-run the gate against the new scope" step the review asked for, and it means **WR-01 had to land before CR-02**, not after. I reordered the two commits accordingly; every commit is independently green.
