<objective>
Give `gamelib-proc` the identity check and the kill that re-adoption (D-08, D-31) and the quit-kill of a re-adopted game
need: a proof made of the leader's pid and start stamp plus the live members seen while GameLib ran, a check that answers
which part of the proof still holds, and a tree kill over that proof.

Purpose: after a GameLib crash the game's leader is reaped by init, and for a Wine or CrossOver launch it has usually exited
already (Pitfall 10), so `kill_game_tree(pid, stamp)` answers `Gone` while the game runs. Re-adoption must match and later kill
such a game without ever signalling a process the stamp has not proven.
Output: `adopt.rs`, `probe::group_pids` and `probe::pgid_of`, `kill_tree::kill_adopted_tree`, stand-in flags and orphan arms.
</objective>

## Facts at planning time

- `probe::{is_alive, start_time, group_alive}`: Linux reads `/proc`, macOS uses `proc_pidinfo` and `proc_listpids` with a
  local `PROC_PGRP_ONLY = 2`, other unix falls back to `kill(-pgid, 0)`; Windows `start_time` is the creation FILETIME.
- `kill_tree::kill_game_tree(pid, stamp, grace) -> KillOutcome { Killed, NotOurs, Gone }`; the unix side shares
  `terminate_known_group` (SIGTERM, poll, SIGKILL only if still alive) and `signal_group` (pgid <= 1 refusal), both
  `pub(crate)`. Windows uses `GameJob::{create_for, open(pid, stamp), terminate, active_processes}` named
  `Local\GameLib-Game-<pid>-<stamp>`.
- A Game-class leader is a session leader (`setsid`), so its pid is its pgid and its sid.
- The stand-in already has modes `game`, `game-tree`, `parent` and the flags `--game-stamp` and `--terminate-one`; the orphan
  tests poll with `wait_until` and register survivors in `Strays`.
- Concurrency: commit with `gsd_run query commit "<msg>" --files <paths>` only; no STATE.md edit.

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-88.3-05 | Tampering | a recycled pid or a forged proof makes kill_adopted_tree signal an unrelated process | high | mitigate | identify proves the leader by stamp or a member by stamp and pgid in the same call that signals; a live leader with a wrong stamp is NotOurs before any member is read; group signals keep the pgid <= 1 refusal |
| T-88.3-06 | Denial of Service | a member forked between the snapshot and the kill escapes the kill | low | accept | the group signal reaches every current member of the proven group, not only the snapshot; the snapshot is only the proof |
| T-88.3-07 | Elevation of Privilege | a proof naming another user's process | low | mitigate | identify only reads public process facts; a signal to a process the user does not own fails with EPERM and is reported as not killed |
| T-88.3-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | no crate is added; libc and windows-sys are already locked |
