# Phase 88.3 Plan 02: Record the single flight, runs-for-me and window raiser goldens Summary

**The launch single-flight table, the runs-for-me state machine and the Wine window raiser are pinned as goldens (24 cases, 58 vectors, 25 cases) recorded from the real TS before any Rust port exists, with every title of the named test files a case or a reasoned exclusion.**

## What was built

- `singleFlight.oracle.test.ts` (slice `single_flight`, floor 24). A flights case is a step list over `createLaunchFlights()` tables: `begin`, `settle`, `reject`, `await`, `probe`, `flush`. The transcript records `run <caller>`, `joined <caller>` (with whether the joiner got the owner's own promise object), `onJoin <caller>` (what the notifier did: record, throw, reject, or the Steam "already starting" notice), `received <caller>`, and `released <key>` / `still-held <key>`. The private map is observed by a probe: a later begin with an immediate body RUNS when the entry was released and JOINS when it was not. The launcher family drives the real `createLauncher(createFakeLauncherDeps(...).deps).launchEventCallback` with the real `createLaunchFlights()` and a game whose launch is held, recording the WR-03 joiner line (`logInfo` spy), the gameStatusUpdate pushes, every Rust request and every per-game log entry, plus a call-through spy on `createGameLogWriter`, so the golden shows the joiner opened no second log (one `openGameLog`, one `rotate: true` frame, one `launch`).
- The 24 cases: the tracer `single-flight--a-second-begin-joins-the-first`; the 5 titles of `launchSingleFlight.test.ts`; the 5 titles of `launcher_launchSingleFlight.test.ts` (including both CR-41 titles); 3 of `appRuntime.test.ts`; 3 of `launchDispatch.test.ts` (two concurrent dispatches, release after settle, the without-Steam join); 1 of `adoptionStatus.test.ts` (the restart join); and 6 more (a held leader, a rejecting notifier, the joiner's own promise, a colon key collision, a rejected owner then a fresh begin, an owner that settled before the joiner asked).
- Census: `NOT_PORTED` names the non-flight titles with their owning phase: 4 of `appRuntime.test.ts` (Phase 98), 10 of `launchDispatch.test.ts` and 39 of `adoptionStatus.test.ts` (Phase 90.2). `assertCasesCoverTitles` runs over each of the five files, and a count check ties cases plus exclusions to the scanned titles.
- `runsForMe.vectors.test.ts` (slice `runs_for_me`, floor 58, layout of golden/redaction): 21 `nextRunsForMeAfterSession` vectors (unset, auto, user-on, user-off crossed with elapsed 0, threshold minus 1, threshold, threshold plus 1, an hour, plus a negative), `nextRunsForMeAfterUserToggle`, `isRunsForMeOn`, `runsForMeKey`, `getRunsForMeMap` (garbage dropped, null, number, string, array) and 19 `recordPlaySession` vectors (darwin and linux and win32, a user choice never overridden, the whole record written with other games kept and garbage dropped, dotted app names, a different runner as a different key, seconds rounding in the log line, a store `get` or `set` or push failure logged and swallowed). It stays after 88.3: the TS stays for the Steam bottle monitor and the renderer, so the Rust port and the TS answer the same vectors.
- `windowRaise.oracle.test.ts` (slice `window_raise`, floor 25): `createWineWindowRaiser({ clock })` over the real `createFakeClock` behind a recorder, a recording `spawnAsync` that answers from the case, `isMac` injected with `injectProbe`. The transcript holds every `osascript` argv with the time of the call, every `setTimeout` delay, every `clear`, the info and warning lines and whether each raise settled. 10 cases are the 10 titles of `wineWindowRaise.test.ts` (one of them is the `windowsExeNameFromCommand` vector list, 12 inputs); 15 more cover a hit on attempt 12, the warning's pid and name lists, every spawn rejecting, a rejecting settle re-raise, invalid pids filtered and empty names dropped, names only, empty input and empty arrays, a hostile name, an empty stdout and a padded `none` as misses, the trimmed hit line, a stop between the hit and the settle, a raise after a stop, and a stop with nothing in flight.
- The three MANIFEST floors equal their counts (24, 58, 25) and are asserted in the test files; an empty case list fails each guard.

## D-20 candidates

Recorded as the TS behaves; the plan that copies each one into Rust files its todo with `severity:`, `platform:`, `ready:` in that order.

For 88.3-04 (single flight):

1. **The key is `runner:appName` joined by a colon**, so `('a', 'b:c')` and `('a:b', 'c')` share one flight (`single-flight--the-key-is-runner-and-app-name-joined-by-a-colon`). Runners are a closed set, so only an app name containing a colon on a runner whose name is a prefix could collide; no live case known. Suggested severity: minor.
2. **The non-Steam funnel joins silently, the Steam funnels tell the user** (`launcher_launchSingleFlight--...runs-the-body-once...` against `launchDispatch--...two-concurrent-dispatches...`): the launcher's notifier only logs the WR-03 line, the Steam dispatch shows an "already starting" notice. Suggested severity: minor.
3. **The restart flight resolves false so a joining Play gets `false`** (`adoptionStatus--...join-the-flight...`): by design (no recent game), but a Play pressed during a Steam restart is answered `false`, the same answer as a launch that failed. Suggested severity: minor.

For 88.3-05 (runs-for-me):

4. **`recordPlaySession` reads the store twice per write** (every `record-darwin-unset-*` vector: two `get('games', {})` reads, one `set`): the map is read once to decide and once more inside `writeAndPush`. Suggested severity: minor.
5. **A push failure after a successful write is reported as "could not record play session"** (`record-darwin-push-throws-after-the-write-is-logged-and-swallowed`): the write persisted, the warning says it failed. Suggested severity: minor.
6. **A hand-edited store holding an array is read by index** (`map-array-keeps-valid-items-by-index`, `record-darwin-array-store-value-is-read-by-index`): `{"0": "auto"}` survives into the written record. Suggested severity: minor.

For 88.3-13 (window raise):

7. **A stopped raise never settles** (`wineWindowRaise--...stop-mid-loop...`, `window_raise--stop-between-the-hit-...`, `window_raise--a-raise-after-a-stop-runs-normally`): `stop()` clears the pending sleep timer, so the loop's promise stays pending forever; a caller that awaits `raise` after a stop hangs. The call sites decide whether that matters. Suggested severity: minor.
8. **`windowsExeNameFromCommand` splits on a backslash only and cuts at the first `.exe`** (the `exe-names` case): `D:/Games/Forward/Slash.exe` returns the whole path, `C:\a\b.exe.bak` returns `b.exe`, `run.exe other.exe` returns `run.exe`. Suggested severity: minor.

## Deviations from Plan

None that changed the plan's intent. Judgement calls:

- **Launcher cases use their own driver.** The plan said to drive `createLauncher(createFakeLauncherDeps(...).deps).launchEventCallback`; the oracle builds the rest of the funnel's collaborators itself (the recorder of 88.3-01 plays sequentially and cannot hold two concurrent Plays). Its transcript has the same shape as the flights cases (steps, `received`) plus the launcher's own events.
- **Case ids.** The census compares `slug(title)` against the id after `<stem>--`, so the ids carry the title's `describe` prefix (`launchSingleFlight--singleflightlaunch-joins-...`), as in 88.3-01. The stems are `launchSingleFlight`, `launcher_launchSingleFlight`, `appRuntime`, `launchDispatch`, `adoptionStatus`, `wineWindowRaise`.
- **Release is observed by a probe.** The map is private, so `released <key>` / `still-held <key>` come from a later begin that runs or joins; the probe's own run and its receipt are part of the case. The `appRuntime` release title is modelled with one microtask after the awaited owner (the finally runs before the awaiter), which pins the timing the test relies on.
- **Spawn list in `events.json`.** The harness writes `spawns.json` as `[]`; the window raiser's spawns sit in `events.json` beside the timers and log lines so their order is part of the record. The five fixed files are present in every folder, three of them empty (`requests.json`, `spawns.json`, `tree.json`).
- **Script recorded once.** `result.script` holds the AppleScript verbatim and each spawn's argv holds `<script>` only when element 1 equals it (T-88.3-03 kept without a kilobyte per spawn).
- **Floors.** Set to the recorded counts (24, 58, 25); the plan's minimums were 14 and greater than 0.
- **Title counts at HEAD** matched the planning facts: `launchSingleFlight.test.ts` 5, `launcher_launchSingleFlight.test.ts` 5, `wineWindowRaise.test.ts` 10.
- **`requirements:`** SVC-03 was NOT marked complete: it is a phase-level requirement and this is plan 2 of 22.
- **Flagged assumption (spec-less edge probe):** SVC-03's edge-coverage row is `unclassified` and unresolved; the D-14 matrix stands in for edge coverage and is not claimed as a verified edge.
- **Gates run.** The three new test files, `fakeHomeIsolation`, `moduleStateCensus`, `diPhaseGates`, `launcherOverlayCensus`, `engineWineFixtureRedaction`, `pnpm codecheck`, `pnpm planning-gates` (12/12) and eslint over the three files (no messages); `pnpm lint` ran to exit 0. The full Backend jest project was not run: it would only re-run the known reds, which are not mine.
