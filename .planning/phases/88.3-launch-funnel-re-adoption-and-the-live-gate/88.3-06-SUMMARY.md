# Phase 88.3 Plan 06: launch_run, the funnel hooks over launchManagerCall and ShellFunnel Summary

**The sidecar can now start a Play in the Rust funnel: `launch_run` goes from the `rustInvoke` channel through the shell arm into `run_launch`, which asks the TS managers for facts and the launch over `launchManagerCall` and does its window, wake lock and CLI exit work through `ShellFunnel`.**

## What was built

- Crate: `launch_run` in `OPS` (sorted) and a `route` arm taking `[params, host]` (both objects), decoding `LaunchParams` and `FunnelHost` strictly, bounding the app name, running `run_launch` under `guarded` and answering `{ status }`. `FunnelHost` gained a strict `Deserialize` (`{ runner, wine }`, each optional).
- Channel: `RUST_LAUNCH_RUN = 'launch_run'` and its list entry, a strict zod tuple for `[params]` (`appName`, `runner`, `launchArguments` an opaque object, `skipVersionCheck`, `args`, no other key), the regenerated `ipc_contract.rs` (`LaunchRunArgs`), the `rust_dispatch.rs` arm, and the `UNBOUNDED_RUST_CHANNELS` entry (twenty-seventh doc line) pinned in `dialogOptionForwarding.test.ts`.
- Shell: `rpc_launch_run` over `launch_run_over` (generated decode and arity check first, then `launch_run:not-ready`, then the crate with `[params, { runner: runner_host(), wine: wine_run_host() }]`).
- `SidecarManagerHooks` now has `ask_with(op, payload)` and the eight funnel ops; each call is its own `launch-manager-hook` thread, a transport failure is `HookError("sidecar")`, a reply of the wrong shape `HookError("bad-reply")`.
- `ShellFunnel { app }`: hide through `rpc_main_window_hide`, wake lock through `wake_lock_start`/`wake_lock_stop`, exit through `rpc_app_exit`; a failed hide or wake lock is one of three fixed `shell_log` lines and never fails the launch. `install` sets it with `with_shell` on the one context.
- `main.rs`: the `launchManagerCall` comment now says the funnel's `launch` hook spans a play session on it (membership unchanged).
- Tests: shell `launch_run_reaches_the_funnel_and_the_launch_hook` (native game in a temp dir, scripted invoke: `launch_facts` first, `launch` after, answer `{ "status": "done" }`, absent Play arguments left out of the frame), `launch_run_is_not_ready_before_the_install_and_rejects_a_wrong_call_the_same_way_after`, the funnel-host test, one test per hook op (payload, `sidecar` and `bad-reply`) through a macro, the `unavailable` and `failed` mappings, the wine-write omitted-key case, a panicking invoke answering `sidecar`, and a source pin of `ShellFunnel` and `install`. Crate: `the_launch_run_op_refuses_a_bad_call_with_a_fixed_word_and_needs_the_hooks`, and the op-table test now also asserts `OPS` is sorted. Jest: the channel counts (218 to 219), the dispatch-arm pin, and accept and reject vectors for the `launch_run` schema.

## Deviations from Plan

### Judgement calls and auto-fixes

**1. [Judgement] Task 2's hook ops and `ShellFunnel` landed in Task 1's commit.** They all live in `launch_dispatch.rs`, and the file cannot be split into two buildable commits without interactive staging. The second commit holds the files that are only Task 2's (the unbounded entry, its pin, the `main.rs` comment).

**2. [Judgement] Payload shapes.** The plan leaves the six remaining payloads open ("their payload shapes"); the shapes in `key-decisions` are what 88.3-08 must answer. `SyncDirection` travels as the flag text the TS passes (`--skip-upload`, `--skip-download`).

**3. [Judgement] Reply mapping for the optional store members.** The plan says an acknowledgement `true`, anything else `bad-reply`. A literal reading would make every non-GOG launch write `Setting presence failed (bad-reply)` and fail on `clear_presence`, because the funnel treats only `unavailable` as "the store defines none". So `set_presence`, `clear_presence` and `persist_bottle_wine_version` answer `false` for a store with no such member (mapped to `unavailable`), and `sync_saves` answers `false` when the TS call threw (mapped to `failed`, the word the funnel already treats as "the handler wrote the line"). Everything that is neither `true` nor `false` is still `bad-reply`.

**4. [Judgement] Failure text of a launch.** `run_launch` answers `Err("launch:<reason>")`; the `launch_run` arm passes it through rather than re-prefixing it `launch_run:`, so the funnel's fixed words reach the sidecar unchanged. Argument errors are `launch_run:bad-args`.

**5. Nothing in CI runs `cargo fmt`.
