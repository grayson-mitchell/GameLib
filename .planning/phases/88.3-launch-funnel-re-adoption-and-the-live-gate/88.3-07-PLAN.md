## Facts at planning time

- `call_runner.rs` `Run::execute_game` (around line 503): creates the capture file
  (`capture::capture_path(ctx, runner, name)` under `<app_folder>/launches/`), spawns through `ctx.game_spawner`, calls
  `ctx.registry.register_game(ids, now, abort, handle)`, then tails while `end::wait_end` polls the leader (100 ms) and the
  group (250 ms). `wine_command.rs` has its own `run_game` (line 305) with the same shape.
- `end::wait_end` keeps the leader unreaped until the group is empty (`GameEnd::Close`), so the pgid cannot be recycled while
  GameLib runs.
- `lib.rs` routes `launch_tracked_kill_all` to `ctx.registry.kill_all(&ctx.engine.shutdown)` and `launch_tracked_kill` to
  `kill_tracked`; this plan does not edit `lib.rs` (88.3-06 owns it this wave): the quit-kill bookkeeping is a kill observer
  the registry calls, installed by `LaunchCtx::new`.
- At planning, `trackedKillAll`'s one non-test TS caller is the quit handler's `killTrackedRust` (88.2-19). Re-grep
  `trackedKillAll(` at HEAD; if another non-test caller exists, stop and report, because kill-all could then not be read as
  the quit.
