# Phase 88.3 Plan 16: The launcher as a module of scheduled shims Summary

`Launcher`, `LauncherDeps` and `createLauncher` now live in `src/backend/launchrust/launcher.ts`, every member a scheduled shim or the 88.1 prepare adapter; `src/backend/launcher.ts` keeps only the TS funnel body and `createTsLaunchFunnel`, and no production module imports it.

## What was done

- **Task 1 (tracer), `226bf4859`:** created `launchrust/launcher.ts` with `Launcher`, `DropFirst`, `toLaunchRunRequest` and `createLauncher`, moved unchanged. `LauncherDeps` is cut to `RustInvokeDeps` plus `libraryManagers`, `launchHooks` and `config: Pick<GlobalConfigSlice, 'getSettings'>`. `launcher.ts` lost its imports of the builders, the funnel shim and the reply narrowers, and gained `TsLaunchFunnelDeps extends LauncherDeps, DialogBoxDeps, AppRuntimeSlice<...>` carrying the rest of the old deps. `storeManagers/index.ts`, `services/types.ts` and `launchAdapters.ts` import the new module; `createStoreManagers` passes the launcher its four deps and no more. `funnel.shims.test.ts` imports from the new module, builds its launcher with only those four deps (the way `createStoreManagers` does), and gained a case: a `setupEnvVars` sends one `RUST_LAUNCH_SETUP_ENV_VARS` request and answers its map. The existing Play cases already pin one `RUST_LAUNCH_RUN`.

**2.
