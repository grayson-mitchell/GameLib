## What changed since the failed gate

- **Presentation mechanism.** `attach_login_window_as_child`/`detach_login_window_from_parent`
  (AppKit child-window attachment) are retired outright (plan 07). `present_login_window_as_sheet`/
  `dismiss_login_window_sheet` (AppKit sheet presentation, `beginSheet:completionHandler:`/
  `endSheet:`) replace them. The authorization registry `ATTACHED_LOGIN_CHILDREN` is renamed
  `PRESENTED_LOGIN_SHEETS`, same authorization role.
- **Retired observer and its retired log line.** The deminiaturize re-raise observer
  (`NSWindowDidDeminiaturizeNotification`) is deleted outright, not adapted -- a sheet is
  presented BY its parent and moves with it, so there is no independent z-order to lose and
  nothing to re-raise. Its log line, `[shell] login-window: parent deminiaturized, re-raising {N}
  attached child window(s)`, **must not appear** under the new mechanism; its absence is a PASS
  signal, not a missing observation.
- **Two new dismissal routes.** Plan 08 lands an injected, self-re-appending "Cancel sign-in"
  strip (primary, macOS-gated, no kill switch) and a page-independent bare-Esc `NSEvent` local
  monitor (backstop), both funnelled through the single `request_login_sheet_cancel` entry point.
  Neither existed when the failed gate ran -- the child window relied on its own OS window chrome,
  which is exactly what went unresponsive.
- **Items 3-6 are carry-overs.** None was ever attempted on the failed run (it aborted after
  items 1/2). The four threats they discharge (T-34.4.2-17, -21, -22, and T-34.4.2-15's
  kill-switch residual) remain exactly as unmeasured as they were the day the failed gate stopped
  -- this rerun does not add or remove risk to any of them, it only re-homes items 1-2 onto the
  mechanism that will actually be running when items 3-6 are finally reached.

(c) Minimize the main window (into the Dock).
