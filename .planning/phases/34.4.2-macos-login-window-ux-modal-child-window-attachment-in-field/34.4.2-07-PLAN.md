<objective>
Replace AppKit child-window attachment with AppKit SHEET presentation for VISIBLE Tauri-managed
login windows, per the BINDING operator decision of 2026-08-04 recorded in `34.4.2-LIVE-GATE.md`'s
"Binding Design Decision" section and `.planning/ROADMAP.md`'s Phase 34.4.2 block.

Purpose: the live gate FAILED 0/6 because the attached child login window became unresponsive after
the main window minimized and restored from the Dock (F-34.4.2-01), and could not be closed
(F-34.4.2-02). The operator's binding fix direction is the presentation switch — **NOT** debugging
the child-window unresponsiveness. No task in this plan may diagnose, instrument, or attempt to fix
F-34.4.2-01's root cause inside the child-window mechanism; that mechanism is being removed.

This plan lands the presentation switch, the registry re-home the switch forces, the deliberate
retirement of the child-window-only deminiaturize observer, and the inversion of the now-superseded
anti-sheet negative tests. The mandated explicit close affordance is plan 34.4.2-08's deliverable —
this plan alone leaves a sheet the user cannot dismiss, which is why plan 08 is a hard dependency
before any gate run (T-34.4.2-33, below).

Output: sheet presentation in `src-tauri/src/main.rs`, a realigned source-assertion suite, and
REQ-34.4.2-01/-02/-03 restated under the binding decision without renumbering any ID.
</objective>
