## Decisions Made

- **D-A is delete, not disable** (operator, binding, pre-dates this plan) -- executed literally: no stub, no `#[allow(dead_code)]` shim, no kept-behind-a-flag copy.
- **`truncate_chars` deleted as a discovered orphan**, not left as dead code -- `parse_autofill_request` was its only caller; leaving it would have been a `dead_code` warning violating the "zero warnings" acceptance bar.
- **The reconciliation grep's zero-hit bar applies to explanatory prose too, not just symbol names** -- my first-pass rewrites of C2/C4/C6/C7 (and the retargeted test's own comment) used the word "autofill" to explain what was removed, which re-triggered the same stem-based grep the deletion itself was supposed to satisfy. Fixed by describing the deleted mechanism without naming it (e.g. "the in-field injected-glyph mechanism," "the deleted mechanism's own sibling clone") -- six sites needed a second pass.
- **`login_window_ns_window`'s stale "plan 34.4.2-04 is a further caller" doc-comment line was deliberately left untouched.** It is inaccurate even pre-deletion (grep confirms `post_autofill_right_click` never actually called `login_window_ns_window` -- it called `login_window_wk_webview` instead, a different function), but it sits outside the plan's explicit seven-site carve-out ("doc comments only, at the seven sites named... no functional code... changes"). Following the plan's own scope boundary literally rather than expanding it.
