**Replaced AppKit child-window attachment with `beginSheet:completionHandler:`/`endSheet:` sheet presentation for visible Tauri-managed login windows, re-homed the poster's authorization gate onto the new `PRESENTED_LOGIN_SHEETS` registry, retired the now-pointless deminiaturize re-raise observer, and inverted the test suite plus REQ-34.4.2-01/-02/-03 to match.**

## Decisions Made

- Sheet presentation replaces child-window attachment per the operator's binding decision (not a decision made during this execution -- implementing a prior locked decision recorded in `34.4.2-LIVE-GATE.md`).
- `dismiss_login_window_sheet`'s membership gate (check-first, AppKit-call-only-if-present) is a deliberate correctness change from the predecessor's unconditional-detach shape, required because `endSheet:` on a non-sheet window is undefined behavior territory, unlike `removeChildWindow:`.
- The deminiaturize observer was deleted rather than adapted into a sheet-flavored re-raise, because a sheet is presented BY its parent and has no independent z-order to lose -- adapting it would have kept dead machinery alive for no measurable benefit.
