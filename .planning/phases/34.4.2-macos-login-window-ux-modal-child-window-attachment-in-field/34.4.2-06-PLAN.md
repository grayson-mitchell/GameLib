completely unproven on hardware:

- **Modal attachment (plans 34.4.2-01/02):** the Tauri-managed login window (Humble/GOG/Amazon) is

  field in the Tauri-managed login window; clicking it delivers the field's rect to Rust over a
  cancelled hidden-iframe navigation, and Rust posts a debounced, bounds-validated
  `RightMouseDown`/`RightMouseUp` pair at the field's centre to pop the real system context menu
  containing `AutoFill ›`. A `GAMELIB_AUTOFILL_GLYPH=0` kill switch disables injection in a packaged
  build.
- **Guards:** the glyph reads no field value and binds no keyboard listener; hidden reveal/clear
  windows are structurally excluded; and the pristine Epic surface is byte-unchanged and provably
  unwired (REQ-34.4.2-10).
