<objective>
Re-point GameLib's renderer bridge onto Tauri, the cheapest leg of the port (spike 012). The entire `window.api` surface funnels through three factory functions in `src/preload/ipc.ts`; re-point ONLY those onto a Tauri↔sidecar transport and leave all 379 call-sites untouched. Also handle the load-bearing wrinkle spike 012 under-counted: the renderer's `storeGet`/`storeNew`/`storeHas` in `misc.ts` run `electron-store` **synchronously and directly in the preload** — a fourth transport primitive the read flow hits (the Steam login-gate reads `steamConfigStore` synchronously). Serve those from an in-memory snapshot hydrated from the sidecar at startup.

Purpose: REQ-27-03 — window.api served by the three re-pointed factories with zero call-site changes, and (BLOCKER-1 fix) the whole surface + 6 preload globals actually ATTACHED to the Tauri webview (the preload compiles to a separate `build/preload` bundle Tauri never loads; `contextBridge` is Electron-only). The renderer files that import `electron` do so as TYPE-ONLY imports (`IpcRendererEvent`/`WebviewTag`/`FileFilter`) — TypeScript erases them at runtime, so they bypass no runtime seam and need no routing.

Output: `tauriTransport.ts`, re-pointed `ipc.ts` + `misc.ts` store funcs, startup snapshot hydration in `index.tsx`, and a headless contract test mirroring spike 012.
</objective>
