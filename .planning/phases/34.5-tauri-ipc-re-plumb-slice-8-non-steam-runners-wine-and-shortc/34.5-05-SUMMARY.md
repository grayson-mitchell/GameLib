**Registered the `runWineCommand` D-14 seam plus 5 more Wine channels (2 probes, 3 version-management) as thin sidecar pass-throughs to unchanged Electron logic, with a 9-test bidirectional registration-kind + curated-import + pass-through-forwarding proof suite.**

## Accomplishments

- `runWineCommand` (D-14 seam 3) is now reachable by name from the sidecar's `ipcMain.handle`, as a pure pass-through to `launcher.ts`'s unchanged `runWineCommand` — its Steam path was already proven transitively via the ported `install`/`launch` channels; its non-Steam path remains live-gate item 5, not something a registration alone can prove.
- `getAlternativeWine` and `wine.isValidVersion` (the two Wine probe channels) are registered, delegating to `GlobalConfig.get().getAlternativeWine()` and `validWine()` respectively.
- `installWineVersion`/`refreshWineVersionInfo`/`removeWineVersion` are ported verbatim from `wine/manager/ipc_handler.ts`, including the `onProgress` → `progressOfWineManager` push, the `notify()` calls with their exact `t(...)` keys, and `refreshWineVersionInfo`'s swallow-and-return-undefined catch behaviour.
- The co-located `backendEvents.on('releasesInfoReady', ...)` subscription (not an IPC channel) is replicated inside `registerWineToolsFlows()` so wine-list refresh behaviour is not silently dropped under the sidecar (T-34.5-16).
- 6 of the module's 9 declared channels are now registered; the remaining 3 (`toggleDXVK`/`toggleDXVKNVAPI`/`toggleVKD3D`) are explicitly left for a later plan per the module's own scaffold.
- A new 9-test suite (`wineToolsFlows.test.ts`) proves registration kind in both directions for all 6 channels, proves no `ipc_handler` import and no deferred-channel leakage (winetricks trio + `runWineCommandForGame`), and proves `runWineCommand` forwards its argument by object identity rather than reshaping it.
