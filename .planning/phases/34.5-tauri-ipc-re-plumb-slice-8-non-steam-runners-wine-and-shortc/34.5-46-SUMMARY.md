- Steam keeps `libraryManagerMap.steam.getGame(appName)` + `game.launch(undefined as unknown as LogWriter)` byte-for-byte, since `launchEventCallback`'s `existsSync(install_path)` precheck and `askForceUninstall` branch would incorrectly abort a valid Steam client hand-off
