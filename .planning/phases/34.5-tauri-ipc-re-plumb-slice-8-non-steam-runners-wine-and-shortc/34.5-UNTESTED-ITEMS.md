anything at all, and cannot produce the single-Keychain-prompt observation `U-34.5-10`'s own row

**Binding statement**: a run using the dev-only secret vault CANNOT serve as evidence for plan
34.5-35's item-3 ("only ONE Keychain approval prompt") claim. `U-34.5-01` and `U-34.5-10` therefore
need EITHER two separate live runs, OR one single run with `GAMELIB_DEV_SECRET_VAULT` left unset
(disabled) so both the real keyring path (retiring `U-34.5-01`) and the read-timeout/memo race fix
(retiring `U-34.5-10`) are exercised by the SAME session. A gate operator who enables the vault to
save time on Keychain prompts is implicitly choosing to leave BOTH rows open for that run, not
just `U-34.5-01`.
