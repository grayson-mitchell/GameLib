  second guaranteed-non-event trap, independent of the already-known VKD3D-on-macOS exclusion. No
  currently-configured non-Steam game on this machine has `wineVersion.type: "wine"`, and no `wine`-type
  Wine build is downloaded at all — recorded as a checked prerequisite with its verifying command, not
  assumed away.
- Evaluated `sideload` as a login-independent item-5 fallback: confirmed it satisfies the item's own
  literal wording and REQ-34.5-03's framing, but is NOT the runner family Pitfall 2's own prose
  describes (storefront-authenticated runners specifically). Named this trade explicitly and flagged the
  GOG-vs-sideload contract-wording question to plan 34.5-40 by name, per the plan's own instruction not
  to silently substitute a weaker target for a stronger one.
