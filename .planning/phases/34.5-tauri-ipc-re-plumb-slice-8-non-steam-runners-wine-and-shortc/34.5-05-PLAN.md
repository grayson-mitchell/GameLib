<objective>
Register the `runWineCommand` seam (D-14 / seam 3) on the sidecar, plus the two Wine probe channels
and the three wine-version-management channels.

Purpose: `runWineCommand`'s consumers span the phase. The plumbing already works for Steam through
the ported `install`/`launch` channels — the gap is that no non-Steam runner has ever reached it,
which is a live-gate problem (item 5), not a build problem. This plan makes the seam reachable by
name and ports the version-management cluster around it.

Wave placement (do not "fix" this to wave 1): D-07 lists `runWineCommand` among the three wave-1
seams, but seam 3 is realized in wave 2 deliberately. Unlike seams 1 and 2 — which modify existing
files (`pathShim.ts`, `main.rs`, `oauthLoginCapture.ts`) and genuinely build something — seam 3 is
pre-existing plumbing needing a live proof (research Pitfall 2), and its code expression is a
registration. Registrations cannot precede plan 34.5-04, which creates this module as an empty
declared contract in wave 1. D-06's purpose is to stop a seam being discovered mid-execution and
carved out late (the 34.4→34.4.1 failure); that risk is already retired here because seam 3 was
fully scoped at planning time and nothing in wave 2 depends on this plan.

Output: six invoke channels and a registration-kind test, with the inherited platform guards
untouched.
</objective>
