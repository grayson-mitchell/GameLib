<objective>
Port the store migration chains to Rust — settings global and game, and the seven GameInfo library chains — driven by a
schema descriptor generated from zod, proven against the golden and the corpus's older-version files, plus the CLI's
headless `open` over a profile.

Purpose: SC1 for profiles from v0.8/v0.9 depends on these chains producing exactly what TS produced; D-04 forbids new
bumps. The TS strip reads zod at call time, so a hand copy in Rust would drift; a generated, drift-tested descriptor
keeps the two in step until 85-24 deletes the TS chains.
Output: genStoreSchemas.ts and schemas.json with a drift test, schema/strip/settings/libraries modules, migrate golden,
corpus-expected outputs from the oracle, the CLI `open` subcommand.
</objective>
