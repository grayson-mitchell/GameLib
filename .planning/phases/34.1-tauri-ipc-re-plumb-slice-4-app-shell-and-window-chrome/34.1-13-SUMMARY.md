## What Happened, In Order

### Task 1 (autonomous) -- executed, then superseded

Built `meta/trayIconVariants.ts` (PNG codec + a straight RGB-inversion generator), regenerated
`public/icon-light.png`/`@2x`/`@3x` as an alpha-preserving RGB inversion of `icon-dark*.png`,
gated on a mean-opaque-luminance delta >= 40. All acceptance criteria in the original plan text
passed mechanically: delta 65.8/70.9/75.4 at the three scales, alpha channels byte-identical,
dimensions unchanged, `tsc`/`prettier`/`tauriShellSource.test.ts` all green. Committed as
`49e891f58`.

### Task 2 checkpoint -- reached, then overridden by direct human review

The plan's Task 2 (`checkpoint:human-verify`) was reached and reported per protocol (six file
paths, verbatim generator output, how-to-verify steps). Before a resume signal was returned, the
coordinator relayed the human's actual verdict on the generated artwork:

> "There is no glyph. `icon-dark@3x.png` is a full-colour magenta gamer-cat on a starburst; your
> regenerated `icon-light@3x.png` is the same cat in green. That is a hue rotation of branded
> artwork, not a light/dark legibility variant. At 22x22 in a menu bar it reads as a coloured
> smudge in either tone, and green is no more legible against a light menu bar than magenta was.
> Your luminance-delta gate passed at 65.8 because inverting RGB does move mean luminance. The
> number was measuring the wrong property. This is worth internalising: the gate was non-vacuous
> and correctly computed and still guarded nothing, because the metric was not the property that
> mattered (menu-bar legibility)."

This is recorded verbatim per the original Task 2 acceptance criteria's instruction to capture
the operator's legibility verdict without paraphrasing into pass/fail.

**Rejected disposition:** the human ALSO rejected the checkpoint's own framing --
"this checkpoint IS that design pass" was called a rationalisation of the todo's explicit "do
not auto-invert branded artwork" instruction, not a satisfaction of it. Recorded so the same
argument is not reused.

### Redirected scope -- macOS template image (user-approved deviation)

The coordinator handed down a concrete redirect adopting the alternative the original plan text
had explicitly declined (`<why_no_template_image>`): a macOS AppKit template image. **This
reverses that original disposition** -- see "Template-image non-goal, reversed" below.

**1. Revert.** `public/icon-light.png`/`@2x`/`@3x` restored to their exact pre-`49e891f58` bytes
(`git checkout 49e891f58~1 -- <files>`), verified byte-identical to `icon-dark*.png` again (md5
`e754404b2dfd8cb4181a20555175bb47` / `5b97b5bdd274139cf7fdfc85ed7fcb74` /
`9557f1960fe6c21649ecf0d2b575c475`). This restores the Windows/Linux status quo exactly --
`meta/trayIconVariants.ts` was repurposed rather than deleted, per the redirect's explicit
permission.

**2. Build the template image.** Segmentation approach: decoded `public/icon-dark@3x.png`
(highest available resolution, 66x66) and histogrammed the hue of every opaque, saturated pixel
in 10deg buckets. Result: the starburst background clusters at 0-50deg (orange/gold), the cat
glyph (including its purple shadow gradient) clusters at 200-360deg (magenta), and the gap from
50-200deg is empirically EMPTY (1-3 stray anti-aliased pixels per bucket at most, against
hundreds per bucket in the two real clusters). `HUE_SPLIT_DEGREES = 125` sits in the middle of
that measured gap. `buildHueSegmentedTemplateAlpha` classifies each pixel by this threshold and
keeps only the cat cluster's original alpha, producing solid-black-RGB-with-alpha-shape output --
the defining structural property of an AppKit template image.

**Hard part, addressed rather than papered over:** a naive flattened-alpha silhouette (no
segmentation) was built and visually inspected first, alongside the hue-segmented cat-only and
starburst-only masks, at both native 66x66 and box-downsampled 22x22. The flattened version
happened to still read as a spiky, roughly cat-eared shape (the starburst's rays are themselves
roughly ear/spike-shaped) rather than an outright unreadable blob, but the segmented cat-only
mask produced a materially cleaner, more recognisable glyph -- visible pointed ears, a rounded
head, eye cutouts -- at all three scales, on both simulated light and dark menu-bar backgrounds.
Mechanical segmentation was judged to have succeeded; the checkpoint condition ("if mechanical
segmentation cannot produce a legible shape, STOP") was not triggered.

**Visual verification performed (by direct inspection of the generated PNGs, not by an
arithmetic proxy):** the final `public/icon-tray-template.png` (22x22, and its 44x44/66x66
pre-production candidates) was composited onto a white background (light-menu-bar simulation,
black shape) and a near-black background (dark-menu-bar simulation, white shape) and inspected
at both native resolution and a 10x nearest-neighbour zoom. At all three scales the shape reads
as a recognisable cat head -- pointed ears, rounded face, visible eye cutouts -- against both
backgrounds, with no stray noise pixels. This judgement is recorded here as the closest
available substitute for the original checkpoint's human sign-off; **full confirmation is still
deferred to plan 34.1-14's live UI re-run**, consistent with the residual-uncertainty section
below and the original plan's own philosophy of not claiming the live swap works from static
evidence alone.

**3. Wire it (Rust).** Verified the exact API against the INSTALLED crate rather than from
memory: `~/.cargo/registry/src/.../tauri-2.11.5/src/tray/mod.rs` confirms
`TrayIconBuilder::icon_as_template(bool)` (line 295) and
`TrayIcon::set_icon_with_as_template(icon, is_template)` (line 577, documented as the
flicker-free atomic alternative to calling `set_icon` then `set_icon_as_template` separately).
`src-tauri/src/main.rs`:
- `TRAY_ICON_TEMPLATE: &[u8]` embedded via `include_bytes!`, `#[cfg(target_os = "macos")]`-gated
- `tray_is_template() -> bool` returns `cfg!(target_os = "macos")`, giving call sites a
  self-documenting name instead of relying on the callee's silent Windows/Linux no-op
- `tray_image(dark)` returns the template unconditionally on macOS (ignoring `dark`), falling
  through to the existing dark/light colour selection -- and its own existing blank-pixel
  fallback -- if the template fails to decode (T-34.1-22 preserved: `tray_image` still cannot be
  the reason `.setup()` panics)
- the `TrayIconBuilder` call adds `.icon_as_template(tray_is_template())`
- the `tray_set_icon` dispatch arm now calls `set_icon_with_as_template` instead of `set_icon`
  (avoids the documented double-render flicker on macOS)
- `cargo check` passes; zero new `dispatch_rust_channel` match arms (D-01 preserved, verified by
  the existing "ONLY new dispatch arm" test, unchanged and still green)

**4. Consequence, reasoned about and documented explicitly:** `darkTrayIcon` is now VESTIGIAL ON
MACOS BY DESIGN. The setting still exists, still round-trips through the sidecar's
`changeTrayColor` -> `tray_set_icon` chain unchanged, but has zero visible effect on macOS
because the template image ignores it. This is documented at the `tray_image` call site in
`main.rs` and in both test files. Windows/Linux are unaffected -- they keep selecting between
`TRAY_ICON_DARK`/`TRAY_ICON_LIGHT`, unchanged, still byte-identical (the original, still-open
defect for those platforms, explicitly out of this redirect's scope).

**5. Gates**, all RED-proven against known-bad inputs before being trusted (see "RED proofs"
below): byte-distinctness (template vs. its `icon-dark.png` source), template-correctness
(`isMonochromeTemplate` -- solid black RGB wherever alpha is non-zero, the actual defining
property of a template image, not a brightness proxy), a non-degenerate-shape sanity bound
(`opaqueFraction` between 5% and 70% -- explicitly documented as a mechanical sanity check, NOT
a legibility claim), and Rust-side coverage of the new `icon_as_template`/
`set_icon_with_as_template`/`tray_is_template` wiring.

**6. `build`/`dist` re-sync.** Checked both directories (gitignored, per `.gitignore:12-13`):
no stale `icon-dark*`/`icon-light*`/`icon-tray-template*` copies existed in either (Task 1 had
already cleared `build/`'s stale copies; nothing further to clean for the new template asset,
which did not exist before this plan).

## Declared deviation (superseding the original plan text's declared deviation)

The original plan's `<declared_deviation_from_the_gap_text>` (icon-dark is the correct SOURCE,
icon-light the DESTINATION to regenerate) is **still true in spirit** -- `icon-dark.png` remains
the canonical source of the branded artwork and was never modified. What changed is the
TRANSFORM applied to derive a variant from it: not RGB inversion into a second full-colour
`icon-light.png`, but hue-segmentation into a new, third, monochrome `icon-tray-template.png`
consumed only on macOS. This is a full supersession of Task 1's approach, not an amendment to
it -- see "What Happened, In Order" above for the complete before/after.

## Template-image non-goal, REVERSED

The original plan text (`<why_no_template_image>`) explicitly declined a macOS template image,
reasoning that it would make `darkTrayIcon` a macOS no-op and called that a product decision
outside this plan's authority, deferring the question to the UAT ledger instead. **Direct human
review reversed this disposition**: the RGB-inversion alternative was rejected as
artwork-mangling, and the template image -- the exact alternative the original plan named and
declined -- was the human's own prescribed fix. The "product decision" concern was addressed
explicitly rather than avoided: `darkTrayIcon`'s macOS vestigiality is now a documented,
deliberate consequence (see "Consequence, reasoned about and documented explicitly" above), not
an open question deferred to a ledger.

## Residual uncertainty (carried forward, unsoftened, and extended)

Everything the original plan flagged as unproven by static evidence remains unproven by this
plan, plus one more item:

- Because `icon-dark.png`/`icon-light.png` were (and, on Windows/Linux, still are) identical, a
  successful `tray.set_icon()`/`set_icon_with_as_template()` and a silently failing one remain
  indistinguishable from the previously observed symptom alone. The `rustInvoke` leg was
  verified statically (source assertions, `cargo check`) but never observed round-tripping live
  in this plan.
- `src-tauri/src/main.rs:718-722` (line numbers approximate post-edit) still forwards sidecar
  stderr to the `tauri:dev` TERMINAL, not `~/Library/Logs/GameLib/gamelib.log` -- log silence
  still proves nothing.
- **NEW in this plan:** the macOS template rendering itself -- does `set_icon_with_as_template`
  actually cause AppKit to render black-on-light/white-on-dark as documented, in the real
  packaged app, on a real macOS menu bar -- has NOT been observed live. All verification in this
  plan is static (source assertions, `cargo check`, jest gates on the PNG's byte structure) or
  visual-but-simulated (composited onto solid white/near-black backgrounds in a script, not
  rendered by AppKit itself).
- **Do not read this SUMMARY as claiming the live tray swap now works, or that the template
  renders correctly in a real menu bar.** Both remain to be confirmed live in plan 34.1-14,
  exactly as the original plan required for its own (superseded) approach. If the template does
  not render as expected there, the remaining candidates are: the `rustInvoke` leg, `
  app.tray_by_id(TRAY_ICON_ID)` returning `None`, or a decode failure in
  `Image::from_bytes(TRAY_ICON_TEMPLATE)` silently falling through to the (also currently
  identical) colour pair -- all of which print only to the `tauri:dev` terminal per the point
  above.

Positive evidence the dispatch chain runs at all (unchanged from the original plan, still the
only live evidence available): `gamelib.log.old:43` shows `Changing Tray icon Color...` logged
from a real Tauri sidecar run.
