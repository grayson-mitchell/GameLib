## Decisions Made

- Gate 1 derives its expected row from `.App`'s live `grid-template-areas` rather than pinning a literal row number. A pinned literal written today would encode the CURRENT correct state, but the recorded project lesson (`appShellLayout.test.ts:171`'s failure mode) is that a literal-consequence gate silently stops guarding anything the moment the underlying premise moves -- deriving from the live structure is what makes a future rewrite turn this gate RED regardless of what the new row numbers are.
- `position: sticky; top: 0px` deleted from `.windowControls` rather than left in place "just in case": both are provably inert on a single `min-content` grid row (zero sticky travel distance), matching the same reasoning `appShellLayout.test.ts:332` already applies to `.NavShell__navbar` for the identical property pair.
