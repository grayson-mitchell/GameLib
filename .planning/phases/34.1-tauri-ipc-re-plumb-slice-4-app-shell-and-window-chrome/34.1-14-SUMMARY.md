### Section B -- The titlebar mechanism, eight SEPARATE questions (Task 1, Block D)

Switch back to `midnightMirage`. Answer each on its own line -- do not answer "the toggle works"
for the whole block.

- **L2.** In Settings, toggle "Use frameless window" ON. Does the titlebar change AT ALL, without
  restarting? Expected: the normal title bar strip disappears, app content extends to the very
  top, traffic lights float over the top-left of that content. If NOTHING changes, that IS the
  answer -- record it as such, do not describe it as "broken" or generalise about Tauri's API.
- **L3.** During that ON transition, did anything flicker, jump, resize, or show a black/white
  frame? Describe exactly what you saw, even if it settled correctly in the end.
- **L4.** With frameless still ON, look at the leftmost nav tab. Expected: it now starts ~78px in,
  clear of the traffic lights, nothing overlapping or clipped.
- **L5.** Toggle "Use frameless window" back OFF. Does the NORMAL native title bar come back?
  Expected: yes -- strip returns, 78px gap disappears. **If it does NOT come back, that is a
  first-class finding ("overlay is one-way at runtime") -- say so explicitly, do not call it "the
  toggle is broken".**
- **L6.** During that OFF transition, did anything flicker, jump, resize, or show a stray frame?
- **L7.** Quit the app entirely. Turn frameless ON in the (now-closed) app's last state (it should
  already be ON from L2 unless you toggled it off for L5/L6 -- if so, turn it back ON), quit, then
  relaunch with `pnpm tauri:dev` in your own terminal. Watch the window from the instant it
  appears. Does it visibly FLIP from a normal title bar to the overlaid one partway through load?
  Expected: no visible flip.
- **L8.** Quit again. Turn frameless OFF, quit, relaunch. Same observation: any flip at all?
  Expected: none whatsoever.
- **L9.** With frameless ON (overlaid): (a) click a different application so GameLib loses focus,
  then WITHOUT clicking GameLib to focus it first, try to drag its navbar to move the window --
  does it move? (Tauri's own docs name this a known overlay-mode caveat.) (b) Separately: with
  frameless ON, does the window TITLE text draw over the navbar? If yes, where and how badly?
  Record (a) and (b) as two separate answers.
