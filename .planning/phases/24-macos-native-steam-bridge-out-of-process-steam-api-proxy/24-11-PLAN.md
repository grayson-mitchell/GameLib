<objective>
Close **D-UAT-24-04 (BLOCKER)**: `placeShimForGame` in `shimGenerate.ts` guards placement with a pure file-**existence** check (`if (existsSync(shimPath)) return` at ~L148). The game's depot ships its own `steam_api.dll` at exactly `shimPath`, and the depot download runs BEFORE `placeShimForGame`, so the guard ALWAYS short-circuits and the real 805888-byte bridge shim never replaces the game's ~118368-byte copy. The entire purpose of the shim is to REPLACE the game's `steam_api.dll`; the existence guard makes it a no-op for every game that bundles one (≈ all of them), so the bridge never engages at runtime.

Replace the existence guard with an **identity** guard: overwrite the target unless it is already byte-identical to `builtBridgeShimPath` (size compare, then hash compare on size-match). Keep the import-coverage rejection and the bottle-containment guard exactly as they are; the overwrite decision happens AFTER coverage passes.

Purpose: makes the bridge shim actually get placed — the single most fundamental "does the bridge engage at all" fix; nothing downstream (install-poll, launch) matters until the real shim is on disk next to the game exe.
Output: identity-guarded `placeShimForGame` + regression tests proving overwrite-of-a-different-dll and no-op-on-identical.
</objective>
