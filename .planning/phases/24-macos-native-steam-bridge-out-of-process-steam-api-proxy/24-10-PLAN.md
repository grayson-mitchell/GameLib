| live Mac Steam identity served through the bridge | Real SteamID64/persona crosses the loopback channel |

| T-24-02 | Information Disclosure | real SteamID64 over loopback | accept | Accepted residual loopback-only risk (recorded at secure-phase); acceptance run confirms the identity is the user's own live session |
