<objective>
Build the codebase's first write-style Humble adapter call (revealKey), capture the previously-untracked keyindex field, extend classification for the D-77 local-redeemed mark, and capture the CSRF cookie the reveal endpoint may require.

Purpose: revealKey() is the single, isolated network primitive the whole claim flow depends on. Its typed AdapterResult drives the D-78 definitive-vs-ambiguous outcome split. keyindex is the reveal request's third form field, absent from today's schema. The isLocallyRedeemed tier lets a local "Mark as redeemed" behave as REDEEMED while remaining distinguishable (undoable). The csrf capture defends against Pitfall A (CSRF-403 masquerading as a rate-limit cooldown).
Output: revealKey/humblePostRequest in adapter, keyindex in schema + a backend keyindex extraction, the new classify tier, and optional csrf_cookie capture — all tested with redaction assertions.
</objective>
