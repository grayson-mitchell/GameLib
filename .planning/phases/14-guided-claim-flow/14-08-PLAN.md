<objective>
Close UAT test 8 (Keys waiting fills-then-empties on every sync) at its confirmed root cause, plus the two serious side-findings that share the same fix locus: a T-14-03 security regression window in the C2 owned-key block, and the permanent loss of the D-24 freeze benefit (19/26 orders re-fetching from Humble forever, raising Cloudflare/429 exposure).

Root cause (debugger-confirmed 2026-07-09, live log + store evidence): `fetchAndCommitOrder` commits freshly-classified keys whose ownership overlay is hard-reset — `classifyOrder` emits `ownedElsewhere:false, matchConfidence:'none'` (classify.ts:399,433) and the commit carries forward only keyindex/revealedKeyValue (library.ts:183-212). D-26 broadcasts `humbleKeysUpdated` after every per-order commit (library.ts:795-798), but `recomputeOwnership()` runs only once at sync end (library.ts:870-877). With 21/26 keys `ownedElsewhere:true` in the live cache, owned keys transiently read un-owned and flood Keys waiting order-by-order (FILL), then vacate en masse after the final dedup pass (EMPTY).

Purpose: Make intermediate sync snapshots carry CORRECT ownership so D-26 progressive fill stays intact (locked deviation cd9af8d8 — do NOT batch/defer/debounce broadcasts) while the churn, the C2 window, and the freeze-loss all resolve. Scope is backend-only — no renderer changes unless a test proves one necessary.

Output: overlay-integrity commit path, a single-sourced server-terminality freeze predicate, and tests covering all five behaviors.
</objective>
