<objective>
Give the renderer a typed exception for schema rejections, keep the boot-error page from hijacking
on one, and gate zod out of the renderer bundle.

Purpose: D-08 promises "a recognisable typed error" at the renderer promise; D-04 promises zod does
not enter the renderer bundle (classic zod 4 is ~454 KB minified; RESEARCH Pitfall 5). Discretion
resolved: class `IpcValidationError` in `src/preload/ipcValidationError.ts`, thrown by
`makeHandlerInvoker`.

Open question 4 resolved by reading `src/frontend/bootErrorSurface.ts:26-80`: it matches only
`[GAMELIB_UNPORTED_CHANNEL]`, and every other pre-mount unhandled rejection paints the page. A
schema rejection during boot (most likely a call-site arity slip during conversion, Pitfall 2)
would therefore make the app unusable. It is a classified contract rejection, not a boot failure:
treat it like the unported marker (warn with the channel, continue). Send-path rejections never
reach this code (preload `send()` routes them to `logError`).
</objective>
