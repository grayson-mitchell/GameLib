<objective>
Replace `electron-store` with `conf` at the nine backend/common sites, leaving `preload/api/misc.ts`
for plan 35-16.

Purpose: `electron-store@8.2.0` is a thin wrapper over `conf@^10.2.0` whose only real addition is a
`require('electron')` on line 3 of its `index.js`. It is the last thing in the tree that forces the
esbuild `--alias:electron=` to exist. Keeping it means `electron` is gone from `package.json` yet
still load-bearing in the build, and D-03's single-grep success test gains an asterisk.

The care point is not the API — this codebase uses a narrow surface (`new Store(options)`,
`.get(k, d)`, `.set(k, v)`, `.has(k)`, `.store`, dot-notation paths). It is the DEFAULT PATH.
`electron-store` derives its storage directory from `app.getPath('userData')`; standalone `conf`
derives its `cwd` from `env-paths` and the package name. A naive swap silently relocates every
persisted setting in the app on first run.

Output: `conf`-backed stores writing to the same files, proven by round-trip, and `electron-store`
out of `package.json`.
</objective>
