<objective>
Rewrite all 67 `from 'electron'` import sites under `src/backend/` to `from 'backend/platform'`, and
shrink the reach ledger's baseline to match.

Purpose: D-02's rationale is the load-bearing part — making this a one-string-per-file change is
what turns the single largest diff in an irreversible phase into the most boring one. It is
reviewable by `git diff --stat` rather than by reading 67 files. By this wave it is also
behaviourally inert: nothing runs under Electron, and the sidecar already resolved `'electron'` to
the very same module through the esbuild alias, so the specifier changes and the loaded code does
not.

Output: zero `from 'electron'` under `src/backend/`, dead modules deleted, and a shrunk baseline.
</objective>
