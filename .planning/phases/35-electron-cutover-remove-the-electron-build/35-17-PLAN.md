<objective>
Delete `isTauri()` and collapse all 140 of its references, in the Tauri direction, across 28 files.

Purpose: the `isTauri()` branch is the shape this phase exists to remove — it exists only because
two shells did. D-17 explicitly rejected a dual-mode platform module for the same reason: it would
reintroduce exactly this branching. With one shell, every branch has a provably unreachable side.

The two traps are measurement and silence. The ROADMAP's count is three times too low, and the
obvious grep (`isTauri(`) undercounts by 39 references because it misses destructured and prop-name
usages — a plan run against that figure would finish, look complete, and be wrong. And a leftover
call site does not throw: it reaches `dispatchInvoke()`, gets the `UNPORTED_CHANNEL_MARKER`
rejection, and `bootErrorSurface.ts` swallows it as a `console.warn`. That is why the gate is a
static grep, not a green test run.

Output: zero `isTauri` in `src/`, proven by a grep that has been seen red.
</objective>
