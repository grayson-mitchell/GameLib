reporting a success it did not achieve. `Runner/index.tsx:54` is the only consumer of that throw and
its entire body is `console.error`, which under Tauri reaches neither `gamelib.log` nor
`gamelib-shell.log`. The UI has already reset to signed-out by the time it runs. The exact condition
the throw exists to surface produces no signal anywhere a support request could recover.

The backend half of CR-04 — distinguishing an empty jar from a broken clear — is plan `35-23`.

path that logs to the backend and tells the user.
