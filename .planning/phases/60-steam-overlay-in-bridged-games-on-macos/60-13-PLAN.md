<objective>
Close G2 and G3. Both are small, desk-fixable defects found in the 60-10 live gate.

G2 (UAT item 4): in DRM mode, the steam.exe helper hop makes Mac Steam attach gameoverlayui about
13 s after launch. The log said `not attached within 10s` at 07:16:02, but console_log shows
`GameOverlay: started ... for game process 62423` at 07:16:05. TCP attaches took 4.0 s, and Staging 7.0 s.

G3 (UAT items 6/7): after Off was stored (`GamesConfig/3205720.json` `"steamOverlayOverride": "off"`,
honoured by the launch), reopening settings showed Automatic. Root-cause lead from planning: settings
writes go to the title's own GameConfig (`settingsFlowRegistration.ts` setSetting -> applyGameSetting).
But `SteamGame.getSettings()` returns the shared `getSteamBottleSettings()` for bottle-eligible games,
and overlays only `steamEngineOverride` (the 59-UAT G11 fix, games.ts ~L1256-1271). So
`steamOverlayOverride` never comes back, and the frontend falls back to 'automatic'. The same split
would make any other per-game key look "unreliable", which matches the operator's report. Task 2
proves this with a failing test before any fix.

Output: the per-route attach window and the settings read-path fix, each with regression tests, plus an audit of the other per-game keys.
</objective>
