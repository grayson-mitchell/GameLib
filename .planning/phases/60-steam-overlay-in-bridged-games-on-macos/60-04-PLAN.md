| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-60-04-02 | Denial of service | watcher lifetime | mitigate | 10 s cap, 1 s poll, unref'd tracked timers, abort controller ends it at stdin EOF |
| T-60-04-03 | Tampering | trace dir symlink plant | mitigate | lstat directory check, chmod 0700, O_EXCL 0600 create (stageWineLog precedent) |
