This plan writes the contract only; plan 11 runs it.

Oracles: (1) `~/Library/Logs/GameLib/gamelib.log` (sidecar `logInfo` sink, where every
`[Steam] CM logon mode=...`, `CM session closed`, `CM session reused`, `go-offline wrap:` and
`CM logon skipped` line lands); (2) Mac Steam's `~/Library/Application Support/Steam/logs/connection_log.txt`
(`user initiated logoff`, `RecvMsgClientLogOnResponse() : processing complete`, `[Logged On|Logged Off, ...]`),
read from a byte offset recorded before each item; (3) the operator's eyes on Friends & Chat
(signed in, and status changeable by setting Away then back to Online). Friends is judged only by
the operator; logs are corroboration.

Items (the contract may refine wording, not drop any):
1. Cold launch, idle 90 s (SC4 launch).
2. Browse the library and open three game pages (SC4 browse/game page).
3. Play an installed title, a bridge title if one is installed (SC4 Play; SC2 launch target).
4. The anonymous session alone (SC4): item 3's `CM logon mode=anonymous reason=launch-target` line is present and Friends is unchanged; blocked (not failed) if no bridge title is installed, with that reason recorded.
5. Explicit Refresh with Mac Steam online (SC6): `user initiated logoff` in connection_log after the item's offset and before GameLib's `CM logon mode=authenticated reason=user-refresh wrap=toggled`; Mac Steam `processing complete` afterwards; Friends signed in and changeable 60 s later; library count unchanged.
6. Depot install with Mac Steam online, started at least 70 s after item 5 so the idle grace has closed the session (SC6): same wrap evidence with `reason=user-install`; install completes; Friends signed in and changeable; at least 60 s after it completes, `CM session closed mode=authenticated cause=idle`.
7. Mac Steam set offline by hand, then Refresh: `wrap=already-offline`; no new `LogOff()`/`processing complete` lines from GameLib's action; Steam stays offline.
8. Guided fallback, conditional: start a game from Mac Steam, then Refresh in GameLib. Pass if EITHER the wrap succeeds (record that the URL verb works with a game running) OR the dialog appears with `CM logon skipped` and no `mode=authenticated` line, and after Steam > Go Offline + Continue the refresh logs on with `wrap=guided-restore` and Steam comes back online. Fail only if an authenticated logon lands while connection_log's last state is `Logged On`.
Notes the contract must carry (recorded behaviour changes, not items to pass or fail): (i) a
bridge title installed before Phase 54 with an unsettled shared-redist ledger logs the deferral
line at Play and launches without the redists until a Play follows a session-holding action
(plans 05/08); (ii) a pre-Phase-54, license-gated bridge title Played before any Refresh shows
the SteamBridgeSetup dialog with `Reason: launch-exe-not-resolved`; one Refresh backfills its launch
target (plans 06/08). Item 3 records which of these the operator's titles hit.

9. Sidecar exit: quit GameLib; within 10 s `pgrep -f build/main/sidecar.js` (or the packaged sidecar name the contract measures) is empty.
