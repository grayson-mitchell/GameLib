## Item content (author these; wording may be tightened, the oracles and literals may not change)

Shared names: `MARKER="$HOME/Library/Application Support/GameLib/mac-steam-offline-owed.json"`,
`VDF="$HOME/Library/Application Support/Steam/config/loginusers.vdf"`. Score `VDF` **only** as a
count: `grep -c '"WantsOfflineMode"[[:space:]]*"1"' "$VDF"`. The file carries account names and
SteamIDs, so it is never copied into the session dir or quoted (T-54-41 privacy rule extended).
Read `VDF` at least 15 s after the event: Mac Steam rewrites it on a state change, and 046a measured
logon complete at about 1.5 s.

**PC9 (new precondition):** before item 1, `test -e "$MARKER"; echo $?` prints `1` (absent) and the
`VDF` count is `0`. If the marker is present, the gate cannot tell a stale debt from this session's,
so stop: record it, let a GameLib launch pay it, and restart from PC1.

**Session procedure change:** items 1-9 stay on launch ordinal 1. Items 10-13 use launch ordinals 2
to 6. Before **every** relaunch, run `cp "$GAMELOG" "$SESSION/gamelib-launch-${N}.log"`, increment
`N`, and write the `=== GATE LAUNCH ${N} — <UTC> ===` delimiter. Move the closing inventory from item
9 to the end of item 13. Item 9 still copies `gamelib-launch-1.log`.

**Item 10. Routed quit mid-wrap (layer 1, handleExit).** Launch 2. Wait for Mac Steam `Logged On`
and Friends signed in. Record offsets. Press Refresh, then press Cmd+Q within about 1 s. The wrap
window is about 3-5 s: LogOff within 0.25 s, the logon, then a 2 s settle before goonline. Wait
20 s, then copy `gamelib-launch-2.log`.
- **Mid-wrap discriminator** (otherwise the run is VOID and repeated, not failed): the launch-2 log
  has `go-offline wrap: Mac Steam state=Logged On, firing gooffline` (or another state word) AND
  `go-offline restore trigger=shutdown: firing goonline`, AND does **not** have
  `go-offline wrap: firing goonline`.
- **Pass:** the CONNLOG slice has `user initiated logoff`, then `processing complete`, and its last
  state is `Logged On` within 20 s of the quit. `test -e "$MARKER"` prints 1. The `VDF` count is 0.
  The operator confirms Friends signed in and status changeable.
- **Fail:** the discriminator holds but Steam's last state is `Logged Off`, or the `VDF` count is 1,
  or the marker remains.
- **Caveat recorded in the item:** gamelib.log is written with async `appendFile`, and `app.exit()`
  is followed by a group SIGTERM. If the restore line is missing but CONNLOG shows the logoff and
  then the logon, mark the item VOID and repeat. Do not pass it on CONNLOG alone, because a quit
  that landed after the wrap's own goonline looks identical in CONNLOG.

**Item 11. Shell killed mid-wrap (layer 2, stdin EOF).** Launch 3. Record offsets. Arm the watcher
in a second terminal:
`tail -n0 -F "$GAMELOG" | grep -m1 -q 'firing gooffline' && sleep 1 && pkill -9 -f 'target/debug/gamelib-shell'`.
The `sleep 1` puts the kill after the LogOff (0.25 s) and before goonline (logon plus a 2 s settle).
Then press Refresh. Within 10 s, `pgrep -f 'build/main/sidecar.js'` must be empty (exit by drain).
Wait 20 s, then copy `gamelib-launch-3.log`. The sidecar lives until drain, so its log is flushed.
- **Discriminator:** as item 10, with `trigger=shutdown` and the stdin-EOF line
  `[sidecar] stdin closed:` present.
- **Pass:** as item 10's oracles, plus the sidecar is gone within 10 s.
- **Fail:** Steam left `Logged Off`, the `VDF` count is 1, the marker remains, or an orphan sidecar
  remains (record its PID and kill it).

**Item 12. kill -9 of shell and sidecar mid-wrap (layer 3, boot).** Launch 4. Record offsets. Arm:
`tail -n0 -F "$GAMELOG" | grep -m1 -q 'firing gooffline' && sleep 1 && pkill -STOP -f 'build/main/sidecar.js'; pkill -STOP -f 'target/debug/gamelib-shell'; pkill -9 -f 'build/main/sidecar.js'; pkill -9 -f 'target/debug/gamelib-shell'`.
Stopping both first means the sidecar can neither see stdin EOF nor be respawned before it dies, so
no in-process restore can run. This is the `kill -9` case the gap names. Press Refresh. Wait 20 s.
Then record the **interrupted state**, which is the evidence that the item reproduces the defect:
CONNLOG last state `Logged Off`, `test -e "$MARKER"` prints 0 (present), and the `VDF` count is 1.
If Steam is already online, the run is VOID (a restore ran); repeat it. Copy `gamelib-launch-4.log`.
Relaunch (launch 5) and wait 30 s.
- **Pass:** the launch-5 log has `go-offline restore trigger=boot: firing goonline` and
  `go-offline restore trigger=boot: Mac Steam back online, marker cleared`. CONNLOG after the
  launch-5 offset has `processing complete` and last state `Logged On`. The marker is absent. The
  `VDF` count is 0. The operator confirms Friends signed in and status changeable.
- **Fail:** any of those missing. `restore-unconfirmed` (`online not confirmed within 10000ms`) is an
  issue, not a pass, even if Steam later comes online.

**Item 13. The user's own offline choice survives quit and relaunch (never fire when GameLib did not
take Steam offline).** In launch 5, after item 12, the operator sets Mac Steam offline by hand
(Steam > Go Offline) and waits for `[Logged Off` in CONNLOG. Confirm `test -e "$MARKER"` prints 1.
Record offsets. Cmd+Q GameLib. Copy `gamelib-launch-5.log`. Relaunch (launch 6) and wait 30 s.
- **Pass:** neither the launch-5 log slice after item 13's recorded `gl=` offset (item 12's boot
  restore lines sit before it) nor the launch-6 log contains any `go-offline restore trigger=` line. CONNLOG after the offset has no `processing complete` and its
  last state is still `Logged Off`. The `VDF` count is 1 (the user's choice, persisted by Steam).
- **Fail:** any restore line, or Steam comes back online.
- **Afterwards:** the operator restores Steam online by hand and waits for `[Logged On`. Quit
  GameLib, copy `gamelib-launch-6.log`, and take the closing inventory.
