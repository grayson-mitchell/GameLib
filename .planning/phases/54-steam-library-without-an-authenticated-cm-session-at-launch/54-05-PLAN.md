<objective>
Move every depot-path authenticated use onto the lease API (ROADMAP SC3, SC6) and fix `LastOwner`.

Disposition per RESEARCH Finding 2 rows 6-9, plus one decision the research did not cover:

- Shared Steamworks redists (`installSteamBridgeSharedRedists` -> `downloadSharedRedistDepots`) run
  today at bridge **Play** time (`games.ts` `fireBridgeWineLaunch`), and `downloadSharedRedistDepots`
  calls `ensureConnected()`. That is an authenticated logon on Play, which the phase goal forbids.
  Decision (Claude's discretion, recorded): the download path gains `allowLogon`; Play keeps
  calling without it (reuse an open session, else the existing `'offline'` deferral), and plan 08
  runs the shared-redist step at the end of a bridge install, where the install's session is still
  in its idle grace, with `allowLogon: true`. The redists are then fetched under the install's
  logon instead of Play's. The in-flight quick task `261027-nd3` (null display driver) is already
  committed (`2ac9d00e7`, `e9bd429a6`); this plan touches only the session acquisition and the new
  option, not the display-driver ledger logic.

`downloadSteamDepots` itself does not acquire: its only caller, `games.ts runNativeDepotDownload`,
holds a lease across plan + download + finalize from plan 08. Nested acquires inside
`buildDepotPlan` are cheap (refcount, canary fast path).
</objective>
