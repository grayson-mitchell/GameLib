## What this gate falsifies

REQ-34.4.1-10, plan `34.4.1-07` is responsible for striking that statement from the
record (via `34.4.1-PORTED-CHANNELS.md` and/or an explicit dated note against 34.4 D-09)

claim standing silently in an earlier phase's artifact.
