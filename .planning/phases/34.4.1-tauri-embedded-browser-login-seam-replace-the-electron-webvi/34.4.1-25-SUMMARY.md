# Phase 34.4.1 Plan 25: Diagnose and close F-10 Summary

**F-10 — "Manage Accounts renders blank" — was the login screen rendering perfectly ~11 000 px below the fold, because `.App .content` carried a `min-height: 100%` that resolved against `.App`'s `1fr` grid row; WebKit resolves that circular percentage by feedback instead of to `auto` and converged on a 23323.0625 px row. Deleting one redundant declaration fixed it, confirmed on hardware.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~2 h 20 min, 9 live cold starts across 4 instrumentation rounds
- **Completed:** 2026-07-31
- **Tasks:** 3/3 completed
- **Reproduction rate:** 8 of 9 cold starts

## Accomplishments

- **Root cause established by measurement, not inference.** `.App` is `grid-template-rows:
  min-content 1fr min-content`; `.content` sat in the `1fr` row with `min-height: 100%`. The
  percentage resolves against the row, the row's height derives from what `.content` contributes,
  and the cycle has no fixed point. Live readback showed `.App{rows=0px 23323.0625px 0px}` with
  `.content`'s `min-height` still reporting the literal unresolved string `100%` — the fractional
  `.0625` being the fingerprint of an iterative solve rather than a content-derived height.
- **The user-visible symptom explained end to end.** Everything below inherited 23323 px;
  `.loginContentWrapper` is vertically centred, so it landed at y=11277 inside a 768 px viewport.
  All that remained on screen was `.loginBackground`, a full-bleed image at `opacity: 0.3`. Nothing
  had failed — which is why five rounds of render-path instrumentation found nothing wrong.
- **The intermittency explained.** The outcome depends on the order WebKit happens to resolve the
  cycle in, with no external trigger. That is why boot proximity, viewport size and navigation count
  each looked explanatory across two or three runs and then failed.
- **Fix confirmed live**: `oversized=0` at sync/paint/t+2000 ms, `.App`/`.content`/`.loginPage` all
  770 px, `.loginContentWrapper` at `top=0` (was 11277), `.content{minH=auto}`.
- **The router's first `errorElement`.** Nothing in the tree had one, so a rejected `lazy()` chunk
  unmounted the whole subtree to an empty document — a blank window indistinguishable from F-10 by
  eye. `makeLazyFunc` also stopped wrapping the import in a `.catch` so rejections can reach it.
- **Verified the two already-present components the phase had never checked**: `UpdateComponent`
  (the `loading` branch) and `Loading` (the top-level Suspense fallback) both render visible
  content. The plan flagged that if either rendered nothing, "blank screen" would have been a
  one-line fix. Neither does.
- **A RED-proven regression gate.** Restoring `min-height: 100%` to `.App .content` fails it
  (`Expected pattern: not /min-height:\s*[\d.]+%/`); removing it passes. Proven by reverting live.

## Deviations

- **`src/frontend/App.css` and `src/frontend/screens/Login/index.scss` are not in the plan's
  `files_modified`.** The plan could not have listed them: it located the defect in the component's
  conditional render, and the defect was in the app shell's grid three levels up.
- **`ff24c6976` shipped a wrong causal claim.** `.loginBackground` measured 23323 px while
  `.loginContentWrapper` measured 770 px, which read as "the background drives the height". The
  equally consistent reading — that `.loginPage` was already 23323 px and the background merely
  filled it, with the wrapper at content height because a ≥850 px media query sets it to
  `height: auto` — was available and was not stated. The fix shipped, the screen stayed blank, and
  the alternative was confirmed. Corrected in `34759cd43`, in the test titles and in the diagnosis;
  the wrong conclusion is retained in the document rather than deleted.

## Issues Encountered

- **The log writer truncates on launch and keeps one `.old` generation.** Runs 1 and 2 were
  destroyed before they could be compared. **Plan 29 must copy `~/Library/Logs/GameLib/gamelib.log`
  aside between runs** or it will lose its own evidence the same way.
- **`electron-vite build` does not empty `build/assets`.** Stale hashed bundles accumulate; at one
  point two `App-*.css` files coexisted, one pre-fix and one post-fix. The referenced entry was
  correct so no false result followed, but `rm -rf build/assets` is required before any build whose
  result must be trusted. Worth its own ticket.
- **The Web Inspector console does not accept keyboard input against this WKWebView.** Live DOM
  questions must be answered by instrumenting code and reading the log.
- **Eight hypotheses were falsified** (A, B, D, render-throw, F-9/keyring, boot proximity, 268 px
  viewport, `.loginBackground`, `100vh`), seven of them by a measurement that did not exist when the
  hypothesis was formed. The recurring error was promoting a correlation before building the
  discriminator. Full table in `34.4.1-F10-DIAGNOSIS.md`.
