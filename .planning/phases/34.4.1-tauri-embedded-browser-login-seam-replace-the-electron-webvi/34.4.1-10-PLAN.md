<objective>
F-1 and F-6 are the same mistake twice: an Electron capability silently dropped at the Tauri
seam, invisible to a fully-green 3279/3279 suite. Before either is patched, enumerate what
**else** the seam dropped — mechanically, not by hand — and encode the lesson as a test.

Purpose: the gate's own first recommendation ("sweep first, fix second") and D-GAP-02's stated
reason for ordering (the keyring slot design must be settled once, with **all** callers known,
rather than reworked after a third caller surfaces).

Output: a repeatable sweep instrument, an enumerated classified findings list that plans 11-18
consume, and a regression guard that fails when the branches diverge again.

**The single most transferable lesson from the gate, and this plan's reason for existing:** one
of the tests cited as proof F-6's area was "closed" is *"the untouched Electron five-step
regression"* — a test that pins the Electron branch at 5 steps. The asymmetry was therefore
**visible to the suite and tested around**. The suite locked in what Electron does and
separately checked that Tauri clears cookies; nothing ever compared the two branches' coverage.
A green suite is not evidence of parity unless something asserts parity.
</objective>
