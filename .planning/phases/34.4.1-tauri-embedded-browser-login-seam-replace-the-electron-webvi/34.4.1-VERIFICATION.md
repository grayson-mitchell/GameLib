`session.fromPartition()` cookie capture) with something that works under Tauri, and port the six
browser-auth channels carved out of Phase 34.4.

## Requirements

All 26 (`REQ-34.4.1-01..13`, `REQ-34.4.1-GAP-01..13`) carry evidence. **25 are checked; one —
`REQ-34.4.1-GAP-11` — is deliberately `[ ]`**, because its own body says *"This box stays UNCHECKED
— live-only"* and no live run has observed it.

Swept **by hand**, with a control, because `plan-phase`'s gap analysis is not phase-scoped and is
blind to every decimal-phase REQ ID — and this phase is doubly decimal (`N.M.P`), so every ✓/✗ the
tool prints for it is unreliable.

## What this phase's history should be read for

**A fully green test suite is not evidence of a live-observable claim here.** The suite was green —
3279/3279, then 3387/3387 — for all three prior gate runs **while F-1 and both of F-6's defects were
live**. Every blocking defect in this phase was found by a human driving the UI; none by automation.

That is why six items remain open above rather than being closed on source evidence, even where the
source evidence is strong.

## The one that is not like the others

struck the planted non-Humble cookie as moot, then required an outcome only that cookie could
produce. `survivingNonHumble=0` against `before(total=34, matched=34)` was arithmetically forced.

**The contract was the defect, not the operator and not the code.** Run 4 unstrikes precondition 6.
