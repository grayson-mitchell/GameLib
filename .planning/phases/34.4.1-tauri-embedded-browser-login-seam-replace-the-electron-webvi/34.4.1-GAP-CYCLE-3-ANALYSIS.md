## D-29-02 — the mandatory discriminator, settled by reading the code

The finding required this before either it or D-29-01 was touched:

> "**Discriminator, cheap and mandatory before either is fixed:** determine whether Manage Accounts'
> update path is gated on `getAccountIdentity`'s result. If yes, one fix closes both. If it renders
> from the login-accepted signal independently, these are two unrelated defects that coincided.
> **Settle it by reading the code, not by assuming the tidier answer.**"

**Settled: they are two unrelated defects that coincided.**

`HumbleUser.finishLogin` (`src/backend/humble/user.ts:678`) gates login acceptance on
**`getGamekeys`**, not `getAccountIdentity` — *"Only a gamekeys-endpoint 'ok' proves login (D-16)"*.
Acceptance (`storeHumbleSecret` + `configStore.set('isLoggedIn', true)`) happens on that signal
alone. `getAccountIdentity` is called afterwards, best-effort, and its throw is explicitly caught.

So a 404 on `/api/v1/user/info` **cannot** have been what left Manage Accounts stale. The tidier
answer was the wrong one — which is the outcome the finding's author was guarding against, having
watched the same evidence shape cost F-10 nine live runs.

**Consequence for planning: D-29-02 is a standalone, non-blocking endpoint-correctness item.** It
does not gate D-29-01 (already fixed) and D-29-01's closure does not close it.

## D-29-05 — the suggested fix collides with an existing security decision

D-29-05 proposes: *"create the window with a provisional title rather than the framework default."*

`src-tauri/src/main.rs:3635` says the omission is deliberate:

> "Deliberately NO `.title()` call (WR-07 anti-phishing: the loaded document's own title must show,
> never a hard-coded 'GameLib')."

**These are reconcilable, but only in one direction, and the plan must say which.** WR-07 prohibits
a *hard-coded application* title. It does not prohibit an *origin-derived* provisional title — and
the repo already does exactly that at `main.rs:2145`, `:4018` and `:4236` via
`login_window_title(&origin, None)`.

**So the fix is `login_window_title(&origin, None)` at build time, NOT a static string.** A plan
that reaches for `.title("GameLib")` or `.title("Signing in…")` re-opens WR-07, which two live-gate
operators were already misdirected on once (the `WR-07 CORRECTION` comment at `main.rs:3656`).

Note also the recorded asymmetry, which survives this change: *a grep gate proving `.title(` is
never hard-coded can only establish the ABSENCE of the prohibited value; it structurally cannot
establish the PRESENCE of the required one.* Any gate written here must not repeat that error.

## D-29-07 — the headline item, and why the contract is the defect

`survivingNonHumble=0` in run 3's item-3 census was **arithmetically forced, not passing**:
`before total=34` equalled `matched=34`, so no non-Humble cookie existed for the delete to spare.

Root cause is the **contract**, not the operator. Precondition 6 of `34.4.1-LIVE-GATE-RERUN-3.md`
**struck** the planted non-Humble cookie as moot, on the reasoning that plan 17's census *"supplies
the evidence without it"* — conflating the **measuring apparatus** with the **thing measured**. A
census cannot report `survivingNonHumble > 0` against a single-origin jar however correct its
counting. Item 1's from-scratch reset then guaranteed that single-origin condition. **The contract
instructed the operator not to plant a cookie, then required an outcome only a planted cookie could
produce.** No action available during the run could have satisfied it.

**Plan 35 MUST unstrike precondition 6** and re-run item 3(b) against a jar holding at least one
non-Humble cookie of known name and domain. Planted cookie and census are **complements, not
alternatives**.
