<objective>
Build the sidecar-side core every probe and the pass plug into: pure runner-output classifiers
(the only place an `expired` verdict can come from), a per-store session-epoch fence, the in-memory
outcome map with its push and pull channel (D-08), and the single verdict writer
`applySignInVerdict`. No probe runs yet and nothing spawns.

Purpose: 49-05 and 49-06 return outcomes through these classifiers; 49-08's pass applies them
through `applySignInVerdict`; 49-07 reads the map. Getting "only an auth failure latches" and
"a stale verdict cannot override a fresh sign-in" right once, here, is what makes P1 and the R2
concurrency edge structural rather than per-store discipline.

Output: `src/backend/signInProbe/{classify,sessionEpoch,outcomes,verdict}.ts`, the IPC
declarations and preload slots, and their tests.
</objective>
