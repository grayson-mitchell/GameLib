| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|

| T-49-06 | Tampering | renderer writes `expired=false` to hide a verdict | low | accept | Matches the shipped precedent for `humbleConfigStore.expired` and `credentialsMissing`; impact is UI-only (the next probe re-latches); no credential is reachable. |
