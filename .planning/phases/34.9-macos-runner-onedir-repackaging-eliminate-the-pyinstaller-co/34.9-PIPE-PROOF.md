## 1. What this proves and why it exists

**The claim under test, in one sentence:** after plan 34.9-25's conversion, a `package.json` script
whose entry file fails to COMPILE exits non-zero and executes nothing -- and `pnpm dist:mac` aborts
before `electron-builder` when that script is `verify:runner-bundle`.

`34.9-VERIFICATION.md` truth 8's exact wording: *"Automated regression protection exists so a
future dereferencing regression of the F-34.9-01 shape cannot silently ship again."*

This project's hardest-won standing rule is that **a guard must be proven to FAIL against a
known-bad input** -- an assertion never shown to go red is vacuous. **Observing that a healthy
script exits 0 proves nothing** -- that is precisely the mistake gap cycle 3's own re-verification
(C2-01) describes, one layer up: a prior verification pass accepted a green re-run of the SAME
already-passing input as evidence the swallow was fixed, without ever exercising the failure shape
the swallow existed to hide.

`34.9-GUARD-PROOF.md` section 7, "What this does NOT prove", states plainly that its own live proof
of CR-01 (the guard gates `electron-builder` when the guard COMPILES) does not cover the case where
the guard **cannot compile at all**. That is exactly C2-01's shape, and it is what this contract
closes: not "does the guard fire when healthy" (already proven), but "does a COMPILE failure in the
guard, or in any of the twelve other censused entry files, still get swallowed by the pipe idiom the
`&&` conversion replaced."

An unfired proof is an unproven claim. Plan 34.9-25 rewrote the idiom; this contract makes its
behaviour under a known-bad input OBSERVABLE, in both directions, per script, and at chain level --
rather than asserted.
