## 1. What this proves and why it exists

`34.9-VERIFICATION.md` truth 8 scored **FAILED**: `meta/verifyRunnerBundle.ts` exists, is
unit-tested, and works when invoked by hand -- but was invoked by nothing in the build pipeline.
`34.9-REVIEW.md`'s sole Critical finding, **CR-01**, names this exact gap: `closeBundle` does not
fail the build on a skipped symlink restore, and nothing in the pipeline runs
`verify:runner-bundle` to catch a regression of that shape. Plan 34.9-20 wired the guard into
`dist:mac` and `release:mac` (Task 1 of that plan) -- but wiring a guard into a script and PROVING
it fires are two different claims, and this project's own narrative already made the mistake of
asserting the second without evidence once (`34.9-REVIEW.md` flags the prior claim that the guard
was already automated and self-enforcing, with no invocation anywhere, as CR-01's actual defect).

**The claim under test, stated in one sentence:** the guard wired by plan 34.9-20 aborts
`pnpm dist:mac` before `electron-builder` when a runner framework is dereferenced, and does not
obstruct a normal build when it is not.

An unfired guard is an unproven guard. This contract exists because CR-01 was a claim nobody had
tested -- it makes the guard's firing OBSERVABLE, in both directions, rather than asserted.

## 6. Verdict rule

**Both directions must pass.** A failing Direction A is the more serious outcome -- it means the
guard does not fire against a real defect -- and routes to another gap cycle. A failing Direction B
means the wiring broke a normal build and must be reverted before anything ships. Direction A alone
passing is not sufficient: a guard that fails every build (including a well-formed one) is not a
functioning guard either -- Direction B exists specifically to rule that out.

## 7. What this does NOT prove

State each explicitly as UNPROVEN. None of the following is asserted by this contract:

- **The packaged `.app`'s own shape.** The guard inspects the STAGED `build/` tree only, never the
  packaged artifact electron-builder produces from it.
- **The x64 leg.** Coverage is arm64-only; `release:mac` also builds x64, and that leg is
  unverified by this step (`build/bin/x64/darwin` holds onefile binaries and would fail the
  file-count floor).

  `pnpm download-helper-binaries`, which throws on the six `PENDING-CI-PUBLISH` sentinels in
  `meta/runnersOnedirDigests.json` (descoped REQ-34.9-02/03/04). This wiring is
  never-exercised-in-CI.
- **`release:mac` itself.** Only `dist:mac` is exercised by this contract; `dist:mac` and
  `release:mac` share the identical inserted step by construction (Task 1's edit applied the same
  literal segment to both), which is an ARGUMENT for `release:mac` behaving identically, not a
  measurement of it.
- **Windows and Linux.** Unchanged by this phase, not exercised by any direction here.
- **Real-certificate signing and notarization.** D-03/D-04 deliberately defer Apple Developer
  Program enrollment; this repo has never exercised real signing.
- **The `closeBundle` throw from plan 34.9-18.** Its failing direction is proven at UNIT level
  only (`meta/__tests__/preserveRunnerSymlinks.test.ts`) and is not reachable from a real build
  today -- this contract's Direction A exercises `verifyRunnerBundle`'s guard, not
