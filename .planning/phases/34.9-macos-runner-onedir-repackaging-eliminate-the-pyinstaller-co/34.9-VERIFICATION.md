`34.9-GUARD-PROOF.md` exercises this scenario — both directions ran a currently-correct, compiling
guard script. The live proof therefore validates a narrower claim ("the guard fires when invoked
against a well-formed input, on arm64, locally") than the one truth 8 asserts. Critically, this

a real capability sitting unrecorded, having fallen through the phase's own reconciliation step
