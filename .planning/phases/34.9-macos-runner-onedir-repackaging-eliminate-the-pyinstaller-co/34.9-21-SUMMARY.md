## Decisions Made

- **Halted on a live process rather than auto-killing it.** `pgrep -f 'target/debug/gamelib-shell'` found a running dev instance (PID 32883) during Task 1 preflight — a precondition this plan added beyond the contract's own numbered list. Per the plan's own instruction ("If ANY precondition fails, STOP and record why"), Task 1 stopped, recorded why, and returned a checkpoint rather than killing an unrelated process without consent. Resolved once the operator quit it; re-verified via `pgrep` and an independent digest match before any mutation.
- **Discarded an unscorable measurement rather than treating a blank field as a result.** Direction A's first attempt used the contract's authored `${PIPESTATUS[0]}` idiom, which silently produced a 1-byte (bare newline) exit file under the operator's actual zsh shell. This was caught before being read as evidence and the direction was cleanly re-run with a shell-portable redirect (`echo $? > file`), rather than inferring non-zero (or anything) from the empty file.
- **Demoted a vacuous assertion instead of scoring it as a pass.** The contract's `find -newer dist/` check for "no new dmg/zip" in Direction A cannot fail by construction, because `clean:dist-mac` empties `dist/` at step 1 of the `&&` chain regardless of outcome. Substituted a measured, provably-discriminating check (terminal pnpm lifecycle step + electron-builder banner absence) and recorded the vacuous check as a finding rather than silently accepting its pass.
- **Recorded a scope-narrowing supersession honestly rather than silently reconciling it.** This plan's own Task 2 spec ("Run the identical `pnpm dist:mac`") ran Direction B without the contract's originally-authored `--arm64 --publish=never` args. The core claim (guard gates electron-builder) is proven regardless; the narrower CLI-arg-passthrough claim is explicitly recorded as unproven, not quietly treated as equivalent.
