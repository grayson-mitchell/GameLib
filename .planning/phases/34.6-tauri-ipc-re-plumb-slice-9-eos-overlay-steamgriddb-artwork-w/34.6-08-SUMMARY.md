## Decisions Made

- **A-02 held as binding**: the dialog the D-05 live-gate round-trip actually hits is `remove()`'s unconditional `dialog.showMessageBox` at `eos_overlay.ts:162`, not `enable()`'s gated one at `:197`. Recorded verbatim in the module docstring so no later reader re-derives the wrong citation.
- **No idempotence guard**: unlike `wineToolsFlowRegistration.ts` (which needed `let registered = false` for its one send-kind channel), this module's 8 channels are all `ipcMain.handle` — `Map.set`'s natural idempotence is sufficient, documented explicitly in the function's own docstring.
- **`handlers.ts` call-site comment corrected to state no count**: the plan's own suggested wording ("the 8 EOS overlay channels") would have failed `flowRegistrationCensus.test.ts`'s Gate 3, which forbids `handlers.ts` from carrying a second copy of a channel count. Changed to "the EOS overlay channel cluster" — Rule 2 (CLAUDE.md/established-invariant correctness), not a deviation from plan intent.
- **`runnerSliceRegistration.test.ts` widened from "four modules" to "five modules"** throughout (top docstring, 4 describe titles, 1 canonical-snapshot comment), per the file's own in-code MODULES-table comment explicitly warning against a stale title silently absorbing a fifth module.
- **Pin-maintenance commit folded two files not in the plan's declared `files_modified`** (`flowRegistrationCensus.test.ts`, `testContainment.test.ts`): both are anti-rot census gates this same phase has already broken and fixed twice before (34.6-06, 34.6-07); their own Gate 1 / Block C set-equality checks fail the moment a new `*FlowRegistration.ts` module or `*.test.ts` file lands unregistered. Leaving them stale here would repeat a defect this phase's own precedent already established as a Rule-1/Rule-2 fix.
