## Scope note — why this UAT is 5 tests and not 24

This phase ported 24 IPC channels and has ALREADY been driven live. `34.6-PORTED-CHANNELS.md`
records a per-channel proof level, so this UAT deliberately targets only the surface that has
never been user-observed. Re-asking about channels already carrying a `LIVE` proof level would
manufacture a second, weaker record of something already measured.

**Already discharged — NOT re-asked here** (14 channels at `unit + LIVE`, PASS): `installEosOverlay`,
`removeEosOverlay`, all five `steamgriddb.*`, `frontendReady`, `getAchievements`,
`getDefaultSavePath`, `getGogDiscounts`, `getPlaytimeFromRunner`, and `importGame` / `moveInstall`
— the last two upgraded 2026-08-26 by gap-cycle-2 items G2-1/G2-2, with the REJECT direction
covered by G2-3.

**Unreachable by construction — cannot be UAT'd on this hardware, and that is the finding:**
`enableEosOverlay` / `disableEosOverlay` sit behind `isWindows &&` at
`AdvancedSettings/index.tsx:401`; `isEosOverlayEnabled` is likewise `isWindows`-gated and, per that
file's own comment, "genuinely never fires on macOS"; `runWineCommandForGame` ships with zero
renderer call sites. These need Windows hardware — Phase 38's territory, not this UAT's.

**Standing live failures NOT re-litigated:** gate Step 4 (`winetricksInstall`, cause unknown,
parked) and Step 8 (Epic logout, wry-layer). Test 5 below re-checks Step 4's symptom only so the
UAT records its CURRENT state; it does not attempt to re-diagnose it.
