## Deviations and findings

**G2-1 run 1 was VOID, and the reason is the most useful thing this plan learned.** The first
attempt produced *no log lines at all* and no UI feedback. Cause: `openDialog` is absent from
`LONG_RUNNING_CHANNELS` (`src-tauri/src/main.rs:184`, 12 entries, verified at drive time), so the
picker is bounded at `INVOKE_TIMEOUT` = 60s; past that the renderer's un-caught
`await window.api.openDialog(...)` rejects and the `if (path)` guard at `GameSubMenu/index.tsx:107`
is never reached — `moveInstall` is never invoked. This is the already-filed
`2026-08-24-opendialog-is-missing-from-long-running-channels-...` todo, **not** a CR-01 regression.
Run 2 used that todo's documented under-60s workaround and measured normally.

The generalisable consequence, recorded in `34.6-VERIFICATION.md`'s addendum: **live-gate evidence
for any picker-driven channel is only trustworthy when the pick completed inside 60s.** A slow pick
produces a silent void run that is easy to misread as a channel defect — which is exactly how it
was nearly misread here.

**G2-2 required a non-obvious fixture choice.** The Import button needs an owned, NOT-installed,
**non-Steam** title (`MainButton.tsx:301` and `:434` — `gameInfo.runner !== 'steam'`, commented
"Import makes no sense for Steam games"). Every Steam title is ineligible by construction. Balrum
(GOG `1769415595`) was used. Worth knowing before anyone re-drives this.

**Two staging traps hit and corrected mid-drive**, both worth recording because each would have
produced a wrong verdict rather than an obvious failure:
1. The `.app` bundle first staged for import is **invisible in the picker's directory mode** — the
   same thing `ImportDialog/index.tsx:101` records as having made an import fixture unreachable
   during the original 34.6 gate. A plain-directory fixture was staged alongside it.
2. G2-2's path was committed by **typing + Enter** rather than the browse button, deliberately:
   `importGame` sits in the same `openDialog` blast radius, and typing never opens the picker.

**Library mutation caused and reverted.** G2-2's import re-identified the bundle by its
`goggame-*.info` as Endless Sky rather than Balrum and rewrote *that* record's `install_path` to the
fixture volume. Restored from a pre-drive backup, shasum-verified byte-identical. The real Steam
`shortcuts.vdf` was confirmed untouched (mtime unchanged across the drive). The fixture image is
unmounted and deleted; `Endless Sky.app` is intact at 419 MB and `--remove-source-files` never ran.
