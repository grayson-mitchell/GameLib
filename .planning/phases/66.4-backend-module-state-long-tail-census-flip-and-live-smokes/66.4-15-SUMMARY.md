# Phase 66.4 Plan 15: Dispatch core Summary

The outbound HTTP tracker, the inbound IPC validator and the stdio RPC transport are per-boot instances. `outboundHttpRegistry.ts`, `ipcValidation.ts` and `sidecarRpc.ts` no longer appear in the moduleState census, and every former importer of the free `requestRustInvoke` / `pushFrontendMessage` / `sendFrontendMessage` now receives them through deps. No new census site, no sanctioned exception, no module-level slot.

## Boot steps and exit contract
- `'outbound-http-tracking'` (first): builds and installs one tracker per boot; `dispose()` unsubscribes.
- `'sidecar-rpc'` (after 'logger', before 'host-services'): `createSidecarRpc({ validator: createIpcValidator({ now: ctx.clock.now, sink: logWarning }) })`. Construction opens no stream and arms nothing.
- `'host-services'`: `createHostServices` gets the rpc instance's three bound functions and now also exposes `requestRustInvoke` and the typed `sendFrontendMessage`.
- `'rpc-server'`: `rpc.start(input, output, host.ipc)`, then `releaseOnStdinEof` receives the tracker. The release order is unchanged: pause download queue, `aborts.abortAll()`, `outboundHttp.markShuttingDown()` then `destroyAll()`. The `rustPending` timers are still `unref()`'d and the unbounded channels still hold no timer. `pendingLines` buffering-before-start is preserved (outputStreamBinding test, same assertions).
- Validator behaviour is unchanged: same rejection log format, same throttle window and per-channel burst, driven by the injected clock; `formatRejectionLog` and `validateInbound` stay pure free functions.
