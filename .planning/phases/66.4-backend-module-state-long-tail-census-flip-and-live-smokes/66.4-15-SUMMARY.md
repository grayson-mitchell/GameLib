# Phase 66.4 Plan 15: Dispatch core (partial) Summary

Only the outbound HTTP tracker (3 of the plan's 9 census sites) is converted to a per-boot instance. The validator and the RPC transport (6 sites) are blocked on an architectural decision and were left at HEAD.

## Done (commit 83835b6d7)
- `createOutboundHttpTracker()` returns `{ install, markShuttingDown, destroyAll, liveCount, dispose }`; `dispose()` unsubscribes the diagnostics_channel handler. The free functions and `__resetOutboundHttpForTests` are gone.
- 'outbound-http-tracking' (still the first step) builds and installs one tracker per boot; 'rpc-server' passes it to `releaseOnStdinEof`, which calls `markShuttingDown()` then `destroyAll()` at the same point and in the same order as before. No process.exit, no new handles beyond the subscription.
- Tests: tracker cases built per instance, plus new dispose and markShuttingDown cases; stdinEofRelease test injects a fake tracker instead of `jest.mock` (jestMock ceiling 2 -> 1 in `sidecar-tests-stdinEof.json`).
- Census moduleState 47 -> 44; `outboundHttpRegistry.ts` line deleted from `sidecar.json`; diPhaseGates pin 47 -> 44.

## Blocked: Rule 4 (architectural) for ipcValidation + sidecarRpc
The plan assumes only `bootstrap.ts` and "pushFrontendMessage importers" consume `sidecarRpc`'s free functions. Measured fan-out:
- Non-test importers of free `requestRustInvoke` / `pushFrontendMessage` / `startRpcServer`: `backend/ipc.ts` (`sendFrontendMessage`, itself imported by ~52 backend modules), `scanners/rustScan.ts`, `steam/depotRust/rustDepot.ts`, `keyringTokenStore`, `humbleSecretStore`, `steamgridSecretStore`, and the humbleLogin / clipboard / appShell / storeEmbed / gameDetails / shellFiles flow registrations, `storeWriteHandlers` (namespace import), `host/index.ts` (type).
- About 35 test files `jest.mock('.../sidecarRpc')` to stub `requestRustInvoke`/`pushFrontendMessage`, and ~10 more drive `startRpcServer`/`bindOutputStream` directly.
- There is no honest way to keep those free-function imports without a module-level slot holding the instance, which re-creates the census sites being removed. Doing it for real means threading `requestRustInvoke` and `pushFrontendMessage` through deps into every module above, including replacing `sendFrontendMessage` in all its callers.
- The validator cannot be converted independently: `dispatchInvoke`/`dispatchSend` inside sidecarRpc call `validateInbound`/`logRejection` free functions and the instance needs the transport.

Needed decision: (a) split this into its own multi-plan cascade (sendFrontendMessage and requestRustInvoke become dep-injected), then convert the three files; (b) accept `sidecarRpc`/`ipcValidation` as sanctioned census exceptions; or (c) approve a narrowly-scoped boot-installed transport slot. Plan 66.4-16 onward are unaffected.
