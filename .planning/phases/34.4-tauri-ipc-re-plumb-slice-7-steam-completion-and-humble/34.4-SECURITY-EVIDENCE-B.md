## 34.4-06-PLAN.md — Harden electronStub.net.request (D-06)

### T-34.4-27 — DoS: synchronous throw / unguarded rejection crashing the sidecar
- **Category:** Denial of Service | **Component:** `electronStub.ts` `net.request()`
- **Disposition:** mitigate
- **Status: CLOSED**
- **Evidence:** `src/backend/sidecar/electronStub.ts:735-753` — `net.request()` builds a local
  `handlers` map, defers the `'error'` emission via `setImmediate(...)`, and invokes it only via
  optional chaining (`handlers['error']?.(...)`), never a throw or a rejecting promise.
  `src/backend/sidecar/__tests__/netStub.test.ts` — "does NOT invoke the 'error' handler
  synchronously" test (no-flush assertion) and "registering no error handler at all produces
  neither a throw nor an unhandled rejection" test (spies on `process.on('unhandledRejection')`).
  Ran `npx jest src/backend/sidecar/__tests__/sidecarRejectionGuard.test.ts
  src/backend/sidecar/__tests__/netStub.test.ts` — 26/26 passing, both suites green.
