## Decisions Made
- `logoutSteam`'s guard shape mirrors `humble/ipc_handler.ts:121-125`'s `humbleDisconnect` convention: `.catch((error) => console.warn('[steamAuthFlowRegistration] logoutSteam failed:', error))`, never rethrowing (`sidecar-dialog-reject-crashes` discipline).
- Argument shape for the new handlers follows `settingsFlowRegistration.ts`'s established convention (`async (_event: unknown, ...args: unknown[]) => { ... }`), narrowing `unknown` at the boundary rather than trusting a renderer-supplied type.
- Test 5's replacement target is `humbleRevealKey` (not `humbleStartLogin`/`humbleReconnect`/`humbleStopLogin`/`humbleLoginNavigated`/`humbleGetLoginUserAgent`) — it is the clearest deferred marker per the plan's own reasoning and no other 34.4 plan registers it.
