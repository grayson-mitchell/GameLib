Migrate the SteamStub, CEG and containment callers in `shimGenerate.ts` and `drm/drmScanExe.ts` to
the async, path-based scanner boundary, and keep their tests honest.

Purpose: covers the D-09 call sites `shimGenerate.ts:525` and `:606`, plus their `+` siblings

**Recorded decision (veto-able, open decision 1): Option A.** `detectLaunchDrmWrapper` stays a TS
orchestrator of about 40 lines with no byte parsing. It calls two Rust leaves,
`scan_contained_real` and `scan_steamstub_section` (through `detectDrmWrapper`), and keeps
`findUnrealLayout` (`bridgeScan.ts`, outside the port list) in TS. In D-13, "TS scanners deleted"
therefore means **all byte-parsing and path-containment code** is deleted; this orchestration
module stays.

**Test policy for caller suites (applies to 63-07/08/09):**
- Caller tests test caller logic.
- They pin the Rust path by setting the scanner's `GAMELIB_SCANNER_*` env to `rust` in `beforeEach`
  (restoring it in `afterEach`).
- They mock `backend/sidecar/sidecarRpc`'s `requestRustInvoke` with canned results per channel and
  path. That is the real leaf boundary: `runScanner` calls `invokeScan` inside its own module, so
  mocking the `rustScan` exports would not intercept it.
- Scanner semantics (containment, `.bind`, CEG) are covered by the Rust tests, the goldens, and
  the legacyTsScanners tests.

This keeps the suites green after 63-11 flips the defaults and after 63-13 and 63-14 remove the TS path.
