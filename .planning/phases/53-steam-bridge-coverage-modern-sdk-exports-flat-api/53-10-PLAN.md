<objective>
Close the CR-01 blocker and its siblings WR-01, WR-07 and WR-08 in the flat-API generator. The
SteamInput006 and SteamGameServer014/015 manifests describe a struct return as
`returnKind: 'pointer'` plus a leading `_ret` parameter (Proton's `__thiscall` shape). The flat
generator reads `_ret` as an ordinary argument and gets past its own `> 8 bytes` guard, so it
emits `void * SteamAPI_ISteamInput_GetAnalogActionData(void *self, void * a1, ...)`. On Win64,
self is then compared against the caller's hidden return pointer, the comparison never matches,
the struct is never written, and RAX is NULL.

Fix this at the source. Model these returns as `returnKind: 'class'` with an exact `structSize`,
teach the flat emitter the Win64 hidden-pointer form for struct sizes other than 1/2/4/8, make
the structSize guard fail closed, refuse `_ret` in x64 manifests, and correct SteamFriends018's
shifted parameters.

Purpose: D-53-01/D-53-05 promise flat exports that are ABI-identical to the vtable slot. This must
land before plan 53-12 adds and regenerates new manifests, because Proton extracts more `_ret`
shapes and the generator has to refuse or handle them correctly first.
Output: a corrected generator, four corrected manifests, regenerated committed outputs, and pinned tests.
</objective>
