<objective>
Build the backend module that captures Steam platform data in bulk from the CM PICS
`appinfo.common.oslist` field (D-01), writes it as a first-class MERGE capture (D-02), scopes
itself to apps whose depot signal was never captured (D-04), and fails soft (D-03).

Purpose: today the Steam sync captures NO platform data — flags are populated lazily, one game at
a time, via `appdetails`. Across a ~378-game library `is_windows_native` is `undefined` for most
titles, which makes 34.14's D-04 fail-open load-bearing in ORDINARY use. This module reduces how
often the signal is missing.

Output: `platformCapture.ts` + its test file. This plan does NOT wire the module into `refresh()`
— that is Plan 05, so the module lands and is proven in isolation first.
</objective>
