<objective>
Close VERIFICATION gaps 2 and 4 on the shimGenerate side, plus the producer half of WR-03/WR-04,
per orchestrator decisions 1 (gap 2 half) and 3.

- Gap 2: `.bind` detection runs no matter what the shim verdict is. `withheld` results (and their
  verdicts) carry `drmWrapper: 'steamstub'`, so a future withhold is never silent. 52-16 adds the
  notice.
- Gap 4, with one concrete choice: **refresh, not refuse**. When the guard would withhold but a
  GameLib shim in scope has no restorable original (no `.gamelib-orig`, or a backup that is refused
  as a symlink, non-file or shim bytes), withholding is impossible: there is no game-original
  steam_api to fall back to, and leaving the stale shim means a helper-less shim, which is what the
  gate measured. So placeShimForGame refreshes the shim to the current build and returns
  `placed-miss` with the gaps named. The title then launches through the bridge with its helper,
  and a truthful warning advises Steam's file verification. The line "launching with the game's own
  steam_api" is logged only when it is true.
- WR-04/WR-03 producer side: the fresh drmWrapper is reported explicitly as `'steamstub' | 'none'`,
  and the exe's PE machine arch is reported as `exeArch`.

Known-bad and withheld titles never reroute to bottled Steam (orchestrator decision 6). This plan
adds no route.
Output: shimGenerate.ts changes plus tests.
</objective>
