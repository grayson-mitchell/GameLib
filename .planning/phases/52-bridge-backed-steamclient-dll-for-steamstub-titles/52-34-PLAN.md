<objective>
Build the honest notice the amended done-when requires (52-UAT item 29, ROADMAP Phase 52 "Amended
2026-10-24"): when a SteamStub title's forwarder loads and reaches the bridge, and the stub's
file/forwarder check then rejects the launch, GameLib says so instead of failing silently.

**What the code does today (read at planning time, games.ts).** `maybeNotifySteamStubUnforwarded(cause)`
(grep `private maybeNotifySteamStubUnforwarded`, ~L2722; 52-09, extended by 52-16) is called only for the causes `forwarder-not-built`,
`x86-deferred` and `setup-error` from the forwarder setup (the `else if (forwarder.status === 'unavailable')` / `else` arms after the placed branch), and `withheld` /
`no-steam-api` from the direct-launch branch (grep `shimResult.status === 'withheld' ? 'withheld' : 'no-steam-api'`). On `forwarder.status === 'placed'`, the path
UAT 24, 27 and 28 exercise, launchBridgeGame only logs `[bridge-steamstub] appId=<id> forwarder placed at
<winPath>` and launches. No notice fires. The existing test "places the forwarder after
writeBridgeConfig and before wine" even asserts `expect(notify).not.toHaveBeenCalled()` there. The 52-06
strings also do not fit this path: they say "could not connect" (52-33 adds the forwarded pair).

**Trigger: deterministic, on every forwarded SteamStub launch. Rejected: detecting the rejection.**
The product has no channel that observes the rejection.
- The MessageBox (`"Steam Error"`, `Application load error 3:0000065432`) appears only in a harness
  `--cx-log` (52-27 interfaces, "Sinks").
- The helper logs nothing after the two `FWD CreateInterface` lines (UAT 24, 27, 28).
- Process lifetime does not separate the outcomes. With the dialog up, Grim Dawn stayed alive 91 s in
  arm A and 90 s in arm B (52-30-DIAGNOSIS `## Discriminating result`, L). Re-gate 3 showed it not alive
  at 90 s and 37 s in the product (items 23, 24). A healthy game is also alive at 90 s.

So any early-exit or lifetime heuristic is wrong both ways. The trigger is the state GameLib knows for
certain before launch: drmWrapper=steamstub AND `setupSteamClientForwarder` returned `placed`. O1 shows
the stub rejects the forwarder file itself, and that file is the same for every SteamStub title, so the
notice states the check "cannot be satisfied through the native Steam bridge" and that the game will
"probably" show a Steam error. It does not claim that the game has already failed.

**Boundary (locked, unchanged).**
- The notice is informational only. The launch proceeds exactly as before.
- No bottled-Steam reroute or offer (no `steamBridgeSetupRequired`, no `markBridgeFailedThisSession`).
  Known-bad does not mean bottled Steam.
- Nothing answers the stub.
- The forwarder, the shim, the helper, the registry writes and the generated sources are not touched.

Output: games.ts notice method and call site, plus trigger tests.
</objective>
