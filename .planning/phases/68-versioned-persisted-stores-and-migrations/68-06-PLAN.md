<objective>
Deliver the read-only state to the renderer and the log: area mapping, the aggregated once-per-launch
push, the IPC channel with its zod schema and preload slot, and the IPC gate updates (RESEARCH
Finding 10). Renderer display is plan 07.

Purpose: stores are checked at module scope, long before `preload/tauriTransport.ts`'s `listen()`
(which has no buffer) has a listener, so a push at detection time would be dropped. The existing
`if (!frontendReadyBootWorkDone)` block in `appShellFlowRegistration.ts` runs once per process when
the renderer reports ready: emitting from there gives "one notice per launch" (D-05) and reaches a
mounted listener.

Output: shared closed sets, backend aggregate wiring, the `readOnlyStoresNotice` push in the
`appShell` domain, `handleReadOnlyStoresNotice` in preload, and green IPC gates.
</objective>
