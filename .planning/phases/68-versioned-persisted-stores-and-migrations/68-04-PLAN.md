<objective>
Carry the read-only state from `GameLibStore` (plan 02's backstop) up to every writer, so a refused
write is also an unannounced write, and hide the in-file version key from every consumer
(RESEARCH Findings 4 and 9, Pitfall 4, Pitfall 5).

Purpose: the `GameLibStore` setter already refuses the disk write, but the store classes and
`applyStoreWrite` notify the renderer after calling it; without early returns the renderer snapshot
would claim values that never reached disk, the exact divergence `storeChangeNotifier`'s header
warns about.

Output: early returns and `readOnly` getters in `typed_store.ts` and `cache.ts`, `__internal__`
filtering in `storePolicy.ts`, `cache.ts` and `typed_store.ts`, two new guards in
`applyStoreWrite`, and tests.
</objective>
