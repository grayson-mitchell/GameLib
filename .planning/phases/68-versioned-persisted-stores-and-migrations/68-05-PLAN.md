<objective>
Move the two settings writers onto the store engine (D-10), fold the two ad hoc fix-ups into an
ordered migration (D-11), and retire Heroic's per-class `reload()` switch and `upgrade()` step,
including the crash it causes on an unknown version (RESEARCH Finding 6, Pitfall 7).

D-11 decision recorded here: the read-side calls to `migrateTrayIconVariant` (in
`GlobalConfig.getSettings()` and `appShellFlowRegistration.ts:250` `syncTrayIcon`) and to
`migrateFocusRowSelection` (in `getSettings()`) STAY as read-side normalisers. The fold-in is the
persisted migration; the read-side calls are the degradation path when `settings/global` is
read-only after a failed migration (D-06) or a newer file (D-03), where the old `darkTrayIcon`
would otherwise silently flip the tray to `'auto'`.

Output: settings codec and migrator, two migration chains with fixture tests, rewired
`config.ts` / `game_config.ts`, version constants and types bumped to `'v1'`.
</objective>
