## Decisions Made

- **F-34.10-04 fix location:** `NavTabs/index.scss`'s `.MuiTab-root` gets a `min-height` override,
  not a `--navbar-height` bump in `NavShell/index.scss` — the latter would grow the entire navbar

  erasure-border gap identified alongside F-34.10-03.

  relationally (matching its tier-2 host), not a hardcoded hex — but whether the change lands in
  `Dropdown/index.scss` directly or in a `.NavShell__tier2`-scoped override is left open, pending
  plan 34.10-20 checking whether `Dropdown` is reused outside the tier-2 context.
- **H5/F-34.10-03 interaction:** reported as a shared root cause (both trace to the tab's 72px
  height inside its 80px container), not folded into a single fix or used to rewrite plan
  34.10-18's separate, already-confirmed missing-seam-border defect.

**2. [Rule 1 — bug in a claim, not code] Corrected the operator-relayed F-34.10-03 border-bottom
reading before writing it into the diagnosis**
- **Found during:** Task 3, source verification pass (`NavTabs/index.scss`, `NavShell/index.scss`)
- **Issue:** The coordinator's relayed analysis attributed F-34.10-03 to `.NavShell__navbar`
  having `border-bottom-width: 0px` while `border-bottom-color` was set — reading this as "the
  erasure half of the card/folder recipe was never shipped." A full read of `NavShell/index.scss`
  shows `.NavShell__navbar` declares **no** `border-bottom` property at all; the browser's
  computed `border-bottom-color` on an unset border is simply the initial `currentColor` value,
  not evidence of an unshipped erasure rule. The real erasure rule lives on
  `.Mui-selected { border-bottom: 1px solid var(--body-background) }` in `NavTabs/index.scss:71`,
  which IS present, but is measured landing 8px above the real navbar/content boundary because of
  the same H1 tab-height geometry.
- **Fix:** Wrote H5's call-out to cite the correct mechanism (the `.Mui-selected` erasure border
  sitting 8px short of the boundary, due to H1's geometry) instead of a nonexistent missing
  `border-bottom` declaration on the navbar itself.
- **Files modified:** `34.10-F04-DIAGNOSIS.md` only (no source touched)
- **Verification:** Confirmed via `grep -n "border-bottom" NavShell/index.scss` (zero matches) and
  reading `NavTabs/index.scss:65-73` directly.
- **Committed in:** `8baf1e41e`
