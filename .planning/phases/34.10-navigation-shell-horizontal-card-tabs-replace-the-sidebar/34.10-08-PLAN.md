<objective>
Mount the shell, restructure the app grid around it, and move the window drag region off `.Sidebar`
in the same commit as the three tests that pin it.

Purpose: this is the commit where `.Sidebar` stops existing in the DOM. `tauriWindowChrome.ts:333`'s
`closest('.Sidebar')` fallback is the **active** drag path under WKWebView — macOS's webview does
not implement `-webkit-app-region` — so shipping the mount without the selector change silently
kills window dragging, and shipping the selector change without the fixture change leaves three
tests passing while proving nothing.
Output: mounted shell, new grid, migrated drag region, updated fixtures.
</objective>
