<objective>
Turn the sidebar's `CurrentDownload` row into the navbar's ambient Downloads ring: always present,
dimmed when idle, filled with the head download's percent, badged with the queue count, and linked
to `/download-manager`.

Purpose: Downloads has live progress and an active count, so it is glanceable state rather than a
destination — the settled design gives it a top-right ring instead of a tab or a tier-2 row.
Output: one component, one stylesheet, one structural test suite.
</objective>
