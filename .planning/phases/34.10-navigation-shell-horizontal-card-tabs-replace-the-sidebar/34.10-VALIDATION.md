> ⚠ **HARD CONSTRAINT — no DOM.** The `Frontend` jest project is `testEnvironment: 'node'`.
> There is **no jsdom and no component rendering**. Every frontend assertion in this phase
> must use the repo's existing *structural* idiom: direct function invocation, element-tree
> inspection of returned React elements, or source-text gates over SCSS/TSX. Any plan task
> whose acceptance criteria assume `render()`, `screen.getBy*`, or `userEvent` is
> **unexecutable as written** and must be rewritten or reclassified as a manual checkpoint.

## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** `npx jest --config src/frontend/jest.config.js <touched-test-file>`
  (targeted — the full suite is too slow for per-task on a phase that touches many small files)
- **After every plan wave:** `pnpm test:ci` — must stay at 204+N suites / 4069+N tests, zero regressions
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** full suite green **AND** both live-app checkpoints below executed and recorded
- **Max feedback latency:** ~15s targeted · ~77s full suite

> theme-merging works on a real build. Both rows below are blocking phase-gate items.

|----------|-------------|------------|-------------------|

Cycle all four tier-1 tabs in **at least** `midnightMirage`, `dracula`, and `dracula-classic` (the last two invert the navbar/body relationship). | Real window dragging under WKWebView after `.Sidebar` retirement | D-02 / REQ TBD | WKWebView does not support `-webkit-app-region`; the `closest('.Sidebar')` fallback at `tauriWindowChrome.ts:333` is the ACTIVE runtime path.
