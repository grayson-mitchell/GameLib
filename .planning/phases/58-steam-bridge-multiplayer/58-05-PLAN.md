**NET line grammar (Claude's discretion per CONTEXT; this text is the contract 58-07 implements):**
The helper writes with `logts("NET", msg)`, i.e. `[<YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ>] NET    <msg>` (category
padded to 6 by `%-6s`), and the sidecar prefixes `bridge helper [<appId>]: `. `msg` is exactly
`role=<game|helper> kind=<join|send|recv|path|oversize> iface=<Matchmaking|Networking|Sockets|Messages> cb=<0-99999> n=<0-4294967295> path=<direct|relayed|unknown>`
with single spaces, every key always present, in that order. Payload regex after attribution:
`^\[[^\]]*\]\s+NET\s+role=(game|helper) kind=(join|send|recv|path|oversize) iface=(Matchmaking|Networking|Sockets|Messages) cb=(0|[1-9]\d{0,4}) n=(0|[1-9]\d{0,9}) path=(direct|relayed|unknown)$`.
A cap line `[<ts>] NET    cap of 32 reached; later lines are not logged` may follow; it never parses.
Semantics: `kind=join role=game` = a join callback (504 success / 1221 Connected) delivered to at
least one game handler; `kind=send|recv role=game` = a game wire call on a networking interface
whose real Steam call succeeded (recv only when data was returned); `n` = message count or bytes.
