## OnlineMonitor contract

`createOnlineMonitor({ clock, ipc: Pick<IpcRegistry,'handle'|'on'>, push, head, newEpoch })` returns `{ start, onConnectivityChange, runOnceWhenOnline, isOnline, getConnectivityStatus, recheckConnectivity }`.

- Construction does no I/O. `start()` registers the `connectivity-changed` listener, runs the first probe, then registers the `get-connectivity-status` handler and the `set-connectivity-online` listener (same order as before).
- The retry countdown runs on `deps.clock.setTimeout` with no `keepAlive` (unref'd by the Clock contract); a fakeClock unit test asserts exactly one pending, non-keepAlive timer and that leaving `check-online` clears it. In-flight probes keep their 10 s axios timeout and are aborted on every status change.
- `push` is `host.pushFrontendMessage` (same transport as the old `sendFrontendMessage`), `head` is `axios.head`, `newEpoch` is `randomUUID`.
- Payload `{ status, retryIn, epoch, seq }`, backoff (5 s steps), probe URLs and the `CI=e2e` shortcut are unchanged.
