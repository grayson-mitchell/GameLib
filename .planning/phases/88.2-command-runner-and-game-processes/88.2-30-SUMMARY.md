# Phase 88.2 Plan 30: WR-05, WR-08 and WR-09 of G-88.2-2 Summary

**A Stop whose Rust kill rejects still stops wine, a quit whose game kill fails says so in the log, and an early abort is asked again until the call settles instead of being dropped after about 1 s.**

## Accomplishments

- **WR-05 (Task 1, tracer, `c2b535d79`).** `bestEffortTrackedKill(kill, prefix)` awaits the kill, logs a rejection as a
  warning (fixed text plus the rejection, no filter or key) and resolves. The four `trackedKill(...)` calls (legendary,
  nile, sideload's `sideload` and `wine` kills) are wrapped; the managers still import `trackedKill` from
  `backend/launchrust/runner`, so the shim schedule and RUNNER_SHIM_IMPORTERS are untouched
  (`engineWineShimSchedule.test.ts` is not modified and passes).

  `bootstrap.ts` binds it to the backend logger with `LogPrefix.Backend`. `quitHandler.ts` still has only `import type`.
- **WR-09 (Task 3, `29cb25d70`).** The fixed 40-retry count is replaced by a doubling backoff (25 ms to 500 ms). A `false`

  in the `finally`. The doc comment drops the "within milliseconds" claim.
- **Disposition (`37ffc693a`).** WR-05, WR-08 and WR-09 are `fixed` with sources `88.2-30: c2b535d79`, `1c80bc9c2`,
