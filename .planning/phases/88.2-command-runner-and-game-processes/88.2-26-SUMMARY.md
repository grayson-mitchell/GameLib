# Phase 88.2 Plan 26: WR-07 and WR-02 Summary

**Windows liveness by a zero-timeout WaitForSingleObject (exit code 259 no longer reads as running, shared `handle_exited` helper for 88.2-27) and a `wait_end` that polls the game handle with `try_lock` so a shutdown is answered within one poll period while a registry kill holds the lock.**

## Accomplishments

- WR-07: `probe.rs` Windows `imp` gained `handle_exited(handle) -> Option<bool>` (`WaitForSingleObject(handle, 0)`: signalled, still running, or `None` when the wait cannot be made). `is_alive` opens `PROCESS_QUERY_LIMITED_INFORMATION | PROCESS_SYNCHRONIZE`, trusts the wait, and falls back to the exit-code read only when the wait fails or the process grants query but not synchronize rights. `kill_tree.rs` is untouched and none of its handles gained SYNCHRONIZE (the CR-01 ordering constraint).
- WR-02: `end.rs` `wait_end` no longer calls a blocking `lock` anywhere. A private `try_lock_game` answers the guard (poisoned lock yields its inner guard) or `None` on WouldBlock. The Close arm treats `None` as still alive and sleeps `GROUP_POLL` through `cancelled_during`; the reap retries every `LEADER_POLL` and keeps the order reap_status then `entry.ended` under the lock. `registry.rs` is untouched: the kill still holds the lock for its whole kill (D-27).
- Three arms: `probe::tests::an_exit_code_of_259_is_not_alive` (Windows), `an_exit_code_of_259_ends_the_run` (three OS) and `a_cancel_is_answered_while_a_kill_holds_the_handle` (three OS).
- `88.2-REVIEW-DISPOSITION.md`: WR-02 (`88.2-26: e98bb3d6a`) and WR-07 (`88.2-26: 1f8ac97a4`) set to `fixed` in the frontmatter and the table; `open:` 10 to 8.
