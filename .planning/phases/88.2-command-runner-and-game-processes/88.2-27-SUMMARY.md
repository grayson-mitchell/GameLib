# Phase 88.2 Plan 27: CR-01, WR-04 and IN-05 Summary

**The Windows `kill_game_tree` now opens the recorded leader once, proves it (running, creation stamp equal) through that handle and holds it through the Job arm and the whole descendant walk, takes children only through `select_tree` (age, running, and the child's own `NtQueryInformationProcess` parent pid), and falls back to the walk when the Game Job did not end the leader.**

## Accomplishments

- CR-01 (T-88.2-52, T-88.2-53): `Leader` owns an `OwnedHandle` opened with `PROCESS_TERMINATE | PROCESS_QUERY_LIMITED_INFORMATION | PROCESS_SYNCHRONIZE`; only `open_verified_leader` builds one, so the root pid cannot be reassigned while anything is collected or terminated and `collect_descendants(leader: &Leader)` has no bare-pid root. `select_tree` (generic over the handle type, outside `mod win`) accepts a child only when it is no older than its accepted parent, still running, and its own handle reports the accepted parent's pid; a failed parent query rejects (fail closed). `terminate_stamped_leader` and the per-child liveness open are gone.
- IN-05: identity is proven before anything is collected. `Pin` is Pinned, Gone (open failed with ERROR_INVALID_PARAMETER, or the held handle already signalled) or NotOurs (stamp differs, other open failure, or the wait cannot be made). `Leader::terminate` answers Terminated, ExitedOnItsOwn (TerminateProcess failed and the handle reads exited) or Refused (still running: NotOurs, nothing signalled). The tree answers Killed only when the leader or some descendant was actually terminated, else Gone. `KillOutcome` kept its three variants.
- One deadline, `grace.min(TERMINATE_SETTLE)`, bounds every wait of a call; the handles now carry SYNCHRONIZE so the waits are real.
- WR-04 (T-88.2-54, T-88.2-55): the Job arm uses `TerminateJobObject`'s result, polls `active_processes`, then waits on the held leader; only a signalled leader answers Killed, otherwise it logs `game job did not end the stamped leader; walking the descendants of the stamped leader` and walks. `GameJob::open_with_access` backs the `harness`-only `harness_kill_game_tree_query_only_job` seam (a no-harness `cargo check --lib` for the msvc target passes).
- Cargo.toml: windows-sys features `Wdk_System_Threading` and `Win32_System_Kernel`; Cargo.lock is unchanged (`git diff --quiet bac11b689 -- Cargo.lock` exits 0).
- Arms: `kill_tree::selection_tests` (5, three OS), `win::tests::kill_game_tree_on_a_reaped_pid_is_gone_and_signals_nothing`, and in orphans.rs the bystander on the shared A5 body, `stamped_kill_with_an_older_stamp_signals_nothing` (unix), `windows_stamped_kill_with_an_older_stamp_signals_nothing`, `windows_stamped_kill_over_a_live_game_job_removes_its_tree`, `windows_a_game_job_that_cannot_terminate_falls_back_to_the_walk`, `windows_a_squatted_game_job_name_falls_back_to_the_walk`.
