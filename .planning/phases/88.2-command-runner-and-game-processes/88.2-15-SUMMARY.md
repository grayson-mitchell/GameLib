# Phase 88.2 Plan 15: The runner streaming transport Summary

**`launch_runner_call`, `launch_runner_kill` and `launch_runner_abort` run end to end (shim, zod schema, generated contract, shell arm over the runner host), with `runnerOutput` and `runnerSpawned` frames validated by `registerRunnerOutputListener` and delivered in seq order by `createRunnerStreams`; nothing is switched over**

## Accomplishments

- Shell: `rpc_launch_runner_call` decodes the generated `LaunchRunnerCallArgs` with an arity check first (the fixed `launch_runner_call:bad-args`, before and after the install), answers `launch_runner_call: not-ready` before install, appends `runner_host()` (this process's environment as a map, a variable that is not UTF-8 left out, plus `PATH`) and runs the op on the dispatcher's per-request worker thread (every `rustInvoke` is its own spawned thread, so a run that lasts hours holds neither the reader nor another call). A call that observes the stream writes its frames from that thread before the arm returns, so they precede the reply on the one ordered stdin pipe. `rpc_launch_runner_kill` and `rpc_launch_runner_abort` answer booleans; an id no run holds is `false`.
- Transport: `RUST_LAUNCH_RUNNER_CALL`, `_KILL` and `_ABORT` in `sidecarTransport.ts` and `RUST_INVOKE_CHANNELS`; zod args in `ipc/rustInvoke.ts` (`env` as pairs of exactly two items, `logTargets` as the typed hand-off shape 88.2-16's `LogWriter.handOff()` answers); `pnpm gen:ipc-contract` and `pnpm check:ipc-contract` green (`ipcSchemaSnapshot.json` is unchanged: it holds the renderer-reachable channels, and the new frames are shell-only). `RUST_LAUNCH_RUNNER_CALL` is the 47th member of `UNBOUNDED_RUST_CHANNELS` (numbered paragraph twenty-third), `dialogOptionForwarding.test.ts` pins it; kill and abort stay bounded.
- Frames: `runnerOutput` and `runnerSpawned` in `sidecarInternalSendArgs` and `sidecarInternalCallers` (shell), covered by the fixture table and by the renderer allow-list test (not renderer-reachable, not on the preload surface).
- `runnerStream.ts`: `createRunnerStreams({ kill })` holds the table in a closure (no module state, no timer): `open` answers `runner-stream-N`, `output` delivers in `seq` order and ignores a duplicate or an unknown stream, `spawned` builds the `{ pid, exitCode: null, signalCode: null, kill() }` handle once, `close` delivers anything still held and forgets the stream.
- `launchrust/runner.ts`: `runnerCall`, `runnerKill`, `runnerAbort` (each one returned typed call) and `registerRunnerOutputListener(ipc, streams)` (two `ipc.on` handlers that `safeParse` and drop a malformed frame). Schedule rows `runnerCall` (92, `wiring: ['registerRunnerOutputListener']`), `runnerKill` (98) and `runnerAbort` (93); each passes `checkShimShape`; `callers` is empty because nothing imports the file until 88.2-16 and 88.2-21.
