Build `drm/runnerClone.ts`, the two DRM-mode setup steps that sit between the component and the
Valve client in 56-11's fixed order:

1. `detectAllowedCrossover()` is the **CrossOver allowlist** step (D-09). It finds the installed
   CrossOver, reads its `CFBundleVersion` and clean ntdll sha256, and fails closed unless 56-06's
   table has the exact row.
2. `ensureRunnerClone()` is the **runner** step (D-08, D-10). It does these things:
   - APFS-clones the installed CrossOver into GameLib's app-support dir, falling back to a full
     copy;
   - keeps `wine.inf`'s mtime and scrubs the download xattrs;
   - patches the clone's ntdll with 56-06's pure applier (56-08 does the disk write);
   - installs `lsteamclient.{dll,so}` as Wine builtins with the `.so` unsigned;
   - re-signs the clone's loader with the dyld entitlement;
   - verifies all of it cheaply on every launch, and prunes stale clones only when no process
     runs from them.

Purpose: SteamStub titles need a CrossOver whose ntdll carries NotProton's loader detour and
whose `lib/wine` holds GameLib's `lsteamclient` builtins. The user's CrossOver must stay stock,
and an unknown CrossOver must never be patched.

is not edited and not imported.
