Gap 2: the install route notice showed the stale scan-miss warning for Grim Dawn, a DRM-mode title.
The persisted verdict for 219990 (measured 2026-10-28 in `store_cache/steam_metadata.json`) has
`scan.status: 'miss'`, `scan.drmWrapper: 'steamstub'`, `drm: null`. Row 7 of `resolveSteamBridgeRoute`
fires the notice from the scan miss alone.

Gap 11 groundwork: Ranch Simulator 1119730 was never routed to DRM mode. Measured read-only with
`x86_64-w64-mingw32-objdump -h`: the launch exe `Ranch Simulator/Ranch_Simulator.exe` has no `.bind`
section (`.text .rdata .data .pdata .rsrc .reloc`), while
`Ranch Simulator/Ranch_Simulator/Binaries/Win64/Ranch_Simulator-Win64-Shipping.exe` has `.bind`.
games.ts routes on `detectDrmWrapper(exePath)` of the launch exe only, so the UE bootstrap hides the
stub. This plan adds the resolver and the todo; 56-21 wires it.

D-12 reading (recorded): D-12 routes "every title whose launch exe scans as steamstub". For a UE
title the bootstrap only re-launches the Shipping exe, which is the binary SteamStub wraps and which
bridgeScan already treats as the runtime exe (`findUnrealLayout`). Treating the Shipping exe as part
of the launch exe's scan is the reading that honours D-17's listing of Ranch as a target; it adds no
allowlist and no per-title override (both declined in D-12/Deferred).

Output: the route fix with tests, the resolver with tests, and the Gap 11 todo.
