<objective>
Close verification gap 2 (review IN-01): the stale DRM-setup card status after a DRM-setup failure.

Root cause, established while planning (cite it in the commit message): the Steam launch does NOT go
through `launcher.ts`'s `launchEventCallback`, so the "launcher's own 'done' after launch() returns
false" that `games.ts` relies on never arrives. `src/backend/sidecar/steamFlowRegistration.ts:345-363`
routes `runner === 'steam'` to `dispatchSteamLaunch(appName)` and returns; `launchDispatch.ts` sends
no game status at all. So any `drm-setup` progress frame `drmLaunchSetup` sent stays as the card's
status until a dev restart. The intermittency in 56-26 matches `progressSent`: Grim Dawn launch 1
sent progress (fresh fetch/clone), Ranch launch 7 used cached downloads and sent none.

Output: terminal 'done' on every DRM failure exit that follows progress, logged, with tests.
</objective>
