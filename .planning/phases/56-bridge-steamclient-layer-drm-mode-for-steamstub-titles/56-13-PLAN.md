dormant code, and to rewrite the tests that pin the forwarder for DRM mode rather than keep them.

   manifests. The presence baseline is regenerated (56-12 contract).

   `meta/buildSteamBridgeShims.ts`, its tests, `native/steam-bridge/steamclient/`, and the stale
   built `steamclient64.dll` in the working tree.

mode (D-12/D-13). There is no forwarder fallback, no notice that predicts a forwarder failure,
and no forwarder DLL in the shipped bundle.

Output: the deletions, the rewritten `games.test.ts` and `buildSteamBridgeShims.test.ts` cases,
the edited catalogs, and the regenerated baseline.
