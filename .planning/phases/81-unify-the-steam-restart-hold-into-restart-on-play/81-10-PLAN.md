<objective>
Delete what is left of the hold (D-11, D-13): its state machine, its persisted-store writer and the
HoldSink seam in the crate and shell, and the `holding` status the TS refresh filtered by. Keep the
Steam process probes (now `steam_process.rs`) and the migration's record parser (`hold_migration.rs`).

Purpose: one mechanism, not two, in code as well as behaviour; the dormant code 81-08 left is removed
so nothing can quietly re-enable a hold.
Output: a crate and shell with no hold, `hold.rs` removed, and the TS status/refresh cleanup.
</objective>
