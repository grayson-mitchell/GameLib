<objective>
Wire the adoption watch into the op contract and into every place the sidecar writes or clears a
pending-adoption record, and re-register stored records at boot (D-07, D-10).

Purpose: after this plan a GameLib depot install that Steam has not adopted is watched in Rust from
the moment its record is written, and the record clears itself when Steam's ACF rewrite or (macOS) a
Steam restart shows adoption — the first user-visible half of the 112100 fix.
Output: three steampoll ops on all four layers (crate, shell, TS transport, TS mirror), the
`adoptionWatch.ts` module, updated call sites and one boot step.
</objective>
