<objective>
Delete the boot re-arm (D-11, D-13): nothing re-reads `steam_restart_hold.json` at boot to put a game
back into "Restart Steam to finish".

Purpose: with 81-10 the hold never arms for new installs; this removes the only path that re-armed
holds persisted by older builds. 81-12/81-13 migrate those records into pending-adoption records.

Release coupling (operator decision 2026-11-17, recorded against the pre-split numbers 81-08 and
81-09): plans 81-10, 81-11, 81-12 and 81-13 ship in the same release. From this plan until 81-13's
boot drain, a hold persisted by an older build is neither re-armed nor migrated (T-81-48). Do not
tag or publish a build between them.
</objective>
