<objective>
Turn the D-05 refusal into an in-app notice that keeps today's copy and, for eligible games, offers
"Launch without Steam" (RESEARCH Open Question 2, settled here).

Open Question 2 decision: launch-without-Steam landed as a per-game setting (q1t) and the old
one-shot channel was removed, so CONTEXT D-05's "if it has landed, wire the action in, only for games
that feature marks eligible" applies. The refusal ships as an in-app notice first (Task 1, the hook),
then the notice button launches once through a new channel guarded by the backend verdict
(Task 2). The one-shot does not flip the setting; D-06 stays a single decision point.
The OS notification for these three refusals is replaced by the in-app notice, because an OS
notification cannot carry the action; the copy is unchanged.

Output: three new closed request-notice kinds, an optional action button in the shared notice
stack, the `steamLaunchWithoutSteamOnce` channel and its single-flight dispatch.
</objective>
