## SETTLED VERDICT

**SETTLED VERDICT:** PIN RESOLVED

In full, per the plan's required exact form: the tag `0.20.43` existed (and still exists, per probe
3, resolving to a real commit) in the repository that was pinned at the time —
`Heroic-Games-Launcher/legendary`. The 34.9-era provenance comment in `meta/buildRunnersOnedir.ts`
("found live against the real, cloned nile/legendary/heroic-gogdl workflows during this plan's
execution") is **TRUE**: a real clone, of a real tag, in the repository that code actually pointed
at, produced the binaries `34.9-LIVE-GATE-RERUN.md` and `34.9-03-SUMMARY.md` describe.

What the finding actually surfaced is larger than it stated: commit `0034ad265` changed the legendary
**REPOSITORY**, not merely its version (its own commit message says so: "legendary moved to
legendary-gl/legendary"). The extraction contract validated in 34.9 was validated against
`Heroic-Games-Launcher/legendary@0.20.43` — a different **project** (different tag namespace, and per
34.16-07's fixture-capture work, a different build-invocation shape: `run: pyinstaller` vs. `uv run
--module PyInstaller`) than the one now pinned, `legendary-gl/legendary@0.21.0`. That repository
change, not "a version bump" in the ordinary sense, is the root cause of `F-34.16-D`: the 34.9-era
validation simply does not carry over to a different upstream project, regardless of what its tag
happened to be.

`0.20.34`". **Neither is supported by the evidence.** The tag was never deleted (probe 3: it resolves

history of the `0.20.43` pin — was the incorrect assumption. The correct explanation, which the
finding itself flagged as a possibility but did not pursue ("Timeline" table, `34.16-LIVE-GATE.md:938`:
"legendary moved to legendary-gl/legendary and renamed its x64 release assets"), is the repo move
recorded in `0034ad265`'s own commit message. The finding's own timeline table names the repo move;
its "deleted tag or digit transposition" reasoning simply did not connect that fact to the tag-lookup
404. This is recorded plainly, not softened: **the finding's stated conclusion was wrong**, and its
own evidence (the timeline table) contained the correct answer without being applied to the
sub-question.
