# Phase 66.1 Plan 25: the stores contract

Every `*/stores.ts` module (and `constants/key_value_stores.ts`) is now a factory against a per-boot registry; the boot's 'stores' step is the only construction path, and the last facade and `storeRegistration.ts` are gone.
