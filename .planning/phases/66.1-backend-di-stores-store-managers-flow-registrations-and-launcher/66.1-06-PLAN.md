them as slices from the start (RESEARCH: the `spawnAsync` factory must exist before plans 08-20 so no manager file is
touched twice). The legacy `utils.spawnAsync`/`handleExit` stay until plan 27 deletes them once no importer remains.

keeps its `child_process` import per A2; the 21 test files that mock `spawnAsync` lower their shards as their subjects
convert in plans 08-20).
Output: two factories, two mock-free suites, boot wiring.

Transitional quit contract (checker warning 1): this plan lands in wave 4, but the legacy registries keep holding live
children until much later (legacy launcher commands in `trackedChildren` until plans 23/24, comet and the Steam bridge
helper in `longLivedChildren` until plan 26). Switching quit to the runner alone would orphan those children on Cmd+Q
for every commit from here to plan 26. So the handler calls the runner AND an explicit `legacyChildren` dep
(`{ killAllTracked, shutdownLongLivedChildren }`, wired by the boot from the two legacy modules) at the same two points.
Plan 27 deletes the legacy registries and removes this dep, its boot wiring and its test arm.

Decision A2 (recorded): `utils.ts` keeps `execAsync`, the VC-redist probe and `wineserver -k` for 66.2, so its
`child_process` ceiling stays 1 after 66.1 (matches the roadmap's "about 27 after 66.1").
</objective>
