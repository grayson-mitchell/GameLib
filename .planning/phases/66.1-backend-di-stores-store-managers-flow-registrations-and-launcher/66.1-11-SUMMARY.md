# Phase 66.1 Plan 11: download-queue boot service Summary

The download queue is now built per boot by `createDownloadQueue(deps)`; importing downloadqueue.ts reads no store and subscribes to nothing (MH-1 removed). The managers reach it through a call-time `getDownloadQueue` accessor, and dispatch/protocol/save_sync take managers, launcher and commandRunner by deps.

**3. launchSingleFlight.ts unchanged.** It holds no manager/launcher/commandRunner references (only a doc mention), so there was nothing to inject.
