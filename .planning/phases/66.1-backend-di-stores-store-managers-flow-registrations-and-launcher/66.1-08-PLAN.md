then convert the GOG manager tree onto them.

deps. This ordering is why runner-dependent moves wait: `killTracked` moves in plan 23 (together with the switch to the runner-based commandRunner, so stop and spawn share one registry); comet and the bridge helper move in plan 26.

(jestMock for removed mocks; the comet `child_process` import goes in plan 26).
