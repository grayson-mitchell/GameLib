<objective>
Expand step of the cutover (RESEARCH Pattern A, assumption A3): introduce the boot-built services and their narrow
types, filled initially by identity facades over the existing singletons (each facade is deleted by plan 05, 06, 23 or 25). Every later consumer plan converts its files
once onto these slices; every later owner plan replaces one facade with the real factory.

Purpose: D-02 and C-01 need managers/stores built at boot and handed down. A big-bang flip of 39 consumers cannot stay
green, and a transitional module-level `let` would be a new ratchet violation, so the facade carries the transition.
Output: services types, facades, three boot steps, test fakes, harness option, README section. Must-haves MH-1/MH-2 (expand half).

Decisions recorded here (Claude's discretion):
- **Where services live (PATTERNS "decide once"):** in a per-boot `BootServices` object held on `BootState`, NOT on
  `AppContext`. `src/backend/process/` is a leaf (import budget "./ files only", depcruise `runner-is-a-leaf`) and cannot
  type-import stores or managers. `AppContext` keeps platform/paths/clock/runner/log.
- **A3:** expand/contract accepted. Owner files are touched at most twice (`storeManagers/index.ts`, `launcher.ts`,
  `utils.ts`: once by a consumer-side change if any, once by their contract plan); each consumer file is converted once,
  with call-site-only edits allowed when a provider's signature changes later (recorded in that plan's SUMMARY).
- **Composition root:** `registerHandlers` (like a boot step) sees the whole `BootServices`; D-01 narrowness applies to
  each registration it calls, which declares its own `XFlowDeps` slice.
- **Step order:** 'stores' after 'logger' (no store logs through the logger at construction; logger uses no store) and
  before 'migrations' (`migration/index.ts` reads `migrationsStore`); today every store exists before step 1, so this keeps
  them available to every step that used them. 'command-runner' then 'store-managers' right after 'migrations'.
</objective>
