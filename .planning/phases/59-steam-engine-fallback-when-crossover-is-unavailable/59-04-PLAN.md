<objective>
Provide the two building blocks of the D-10/D-11 launch timeout: a "game process seen" signal
from the existing bottle process monitor, and a standalone per-appId deadline that 59-10 arms at
launch and cancels on that signal.

Purpose: D-11 says the timeout stops when the game process is seen in the bottle, not at bridge
HELLO. bottleProcessMonitor already detects that moment (`recordFirstMatch` sets `everMatched`);
it needs a callback. Keeping the deadline outside the monitor preserves LD-4 (the monitor never
times out a running game).

Output: an `onFirstMatch` option on startBottleProcessMonitor, and `engine/launchDeadline.ts`.
</objective>
