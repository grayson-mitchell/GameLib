<objective>
Add the per-game engine override as an advanced, explicitly unsupported option (D-14): a
collapsed section in the game's settings, defaulting to Automatic, with a warning for any other
choice. It is also the only way a user can reach the previous pinned build (D-08).

Purpose: power users can force CrossOver, a pinned built-in build or a Wine Manager Staging
build for one title, on the Proton model, without that ever being the default or the supported
path.

Output: SteamEngineOverride.tsx, its test, and its placement in the Advanced tab.
</objective>
