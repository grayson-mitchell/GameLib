transient notice at launch and as text on the badge, so it no longer depends on a system
notification that `tauri dev` never delivers and a packaged build may have turned off.

Purpose: D-06 says every launch that drops to the fallback shows a short notice giving the
reason; today only `notify()` carries it.

Output: an app-level SteamEngineLaunchNotice component, a badge with a visible reason, tests.
</objective>
