<objective>
Close verification gap 2 (CR-03, WR-01, WR-09) and the engine-switch half of gap 6 (G10,
WR-06). After this plan the `.gamelib-engine.json` marker says `crossover` only when DXVK is
really gone, a failed restore is visible and retried, the restore never runs through an
expired CrossOver, and a shared bottle is never switched under another title.

Purpose: CrossOver must not keep running a bottle with the built-in engine's DXVK DLLs while
the marker says the bottle is clean (the G7 end state reached by another route).

Output: strict `installRemove` restore, a rebuilt `prepareBottleForEngine` with two new
optional args (`crossoverHealthy`, `onSwitching`) and a new failure reason (`shared-bottle`),
and regression tests.
</objective>
