<objective>
G25 causes (a) to (c), wired into Play. UAT 34 showed three things in sequence: `isOnline()` read
online at Play, then `resolveBridgeLaunchExe` spent ~9 s on a `reason=launch-target` anonymous CM
logon waiting for DNS to fail, and only then did `selectLaunchEngine` run, whose refusal
predicate also missed the CrossOver-missing case. 59-40 supplies a fresh connectivity check and
59-41 the corrected predicate; this plan threads one fresh result through the bridge launch so the
refusal fires without the CM wait.

Choice (Claude's discretion, from the gap's "bound or skip"): skip, not reorder. Moving the refusal
ahead of `resolveBridgeLaunchExe` would also move it ahead of the DRM-mode routing, which needs the
resolved exe. Skipping the CM read when offline and a persisted `bridge.launchTarget` exists keeps
the routing order, and also removes the 9 s from UAT 34 run 2's legitimate offline restore. With no
stored target the PICS path is kept, so a wrong offline reading cannot make a never-resolved title
unlaunchable.

Output: offline short-circuit in launchTarget.ts, wiring in games.ts, tests.
</objective>
