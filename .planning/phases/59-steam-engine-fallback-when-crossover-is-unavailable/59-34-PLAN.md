  "The launch may fail". Wording only (operator scope).

  and 59-37 (Downloads tab) both have one owner for `gamelib.json`.

`public/locales/en/gamelib.json` (59-24 precedent). translation.json carries the operator's
uncommitted edits and must not be touched.
