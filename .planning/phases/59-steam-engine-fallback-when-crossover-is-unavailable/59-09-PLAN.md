<objective>
Let a Windows Steam title install on the bridge without CrossOver: relax the install-time
CrossOver gate for the bridge route only, and make bridge-bottle creation choose its engine,
downloading the pinned built-in engine on first need.

Purpose: D-01 and D-04 say a user who never had CrossOver can still install and play. D-05 says
the engine downloads during that first install, with progress, and licensed CrossOver users never
download it. D-12 says a CrossOver that hangs creating a bottle must not hang the install.

Output: games.ts install() and installBridgeGame() changes with games.test.ts coverage.
</objective>
