PREMISE CORRECTION -- `apt-cache policy` on this host is NOT a jammy-availability oracle.
Verbatim output of `apt-cache policy libwebkit2gtk-4.1-dev` on this host:

  libwebkit2gtk-4.1-dev:
    Installed: 2.50.4-0ubuntu0.22.04.1
    Candidate: 2.50.4-0ubuntu0.22.04.1
    Version table:
   *** 2.50.4-0ubuntu0.22.04.1 100
          100 /var/lib/dpkg/status

The only version-table source is /var/lib/dpkg/status. /var/lib/apt/lists/ holds a
stale cache of `noble` indexes (apt.pop-os.org_release_dists_noble_*), while
/etc/apt/sources.list.d/ points at `jammy` suites. So "Candidate" here simply equals
the installed version and says nothing about what the jammy archive carries. That is
why this census reads the real archive indexes and lists the installed version only as
a secondary column.

Note: the GitHub-hosted ubuntu-22.04 runner installs from Ubuntu's own archive mirror,
not from apt.pop-os.org; the indexes above are therefore the correct reference.
