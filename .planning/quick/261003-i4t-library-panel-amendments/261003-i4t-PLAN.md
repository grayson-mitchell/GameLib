**D-07 — the sort-by-status glyph is a composed badge, not a single glyph.** MEASURED: no
x-marked or slashed drive glyph exists in the installed sets. Enumerated every export of
`@fortawesome/free-solid-svg-icons` (1967 `fa*` names) and
`@fortawesome/free-regular-svg-icons` (258): the drive/disk family is
`faHardDrive`, `faHdd`, `faFloppyDisk`, `faDatabase`, `faServer`, the `faBox*` group,
`faDriversLicense`; **none** carries an `xmark`, `slash`, `ban`, `times` or `circle`
variant in either set. There is also no `fa-layers`, `mask=` or `transform=` precedent
anywhere under `src/frontend`. So: a `position: relative` wrapper span holding `faHardDrive`
solid, plus a smaller `faCircleXmark` absolutely positioned at its bottom-right corner,
rendered only while `sortInstalled` is true. No new dependency, no new FontAwesome
subsystem, and it reproduces the alphabet filter's *visual* convention (glyph plus small x)
rather than its glyph family — which is the strongest available match given the family does
not exist.

**D-08 — the badge takes `color: inherit` and punches out with `var(--navbar-background)`.**
MEASURED: `--navbar-background` is declared in all 11 theme blocks of
`src/frontend/themes.scss` (`:169, :193, :273, :323, :365, :437, :564, :602, :637, :676,
:727`) and is the literal background `.NavShell__tier2` paints on itself
(`NavShell/index.scss:244`, no fallback). A halo in that token therefore matches the panel
exactly in every theme *by construction*, and carries precisely the same resolution risk the
panel's own background already carries — not a new one. The x itself inherits the glyph
colour, which is what the alphabet filter's `faFilterCircleXmark` already does, so no new
foreground/background contrast pair is introduced. FORBIDDEN here and not used:
`--text-hover` (4 theme blocks only, absent from base `body`), the four `--navitem-*`
(scoped to `.NavShell__tier2 .NavItem`), `--border-color` (1.08:1 in 10 of 13 selectors,
open todo), and any `--action-icon-active` fill — `--action-icon-active` resolves to
`var(--accent)` in 7 of 11 blocks, and an accent fill is the exact thing D-02 removes.

**D-09 — the sort-by-status title stays STATELESS, and `aria-pressed` stays.** The brief
offered a stateful title as "nearly free if item 5's catalogue work happens anyway". It is
not: a stateful pair is two more new keys, and D-05 already establishes that one new gamelib
key costs 48 hand-derived translations, so a stateful title costs **96 more strings** — it
roughly doubles D-05. The in-code argument already at `Header/index.tsx:219-227` is correct
and stands: an accessible name should name the control, not narrate its state, and
`aria-pressed` carries the state to assistive tech properly. Keep
`title={t('library.sortByStatus', 'Sort by Status')}` and keep `aria-pressed={sortInstalled}`.

**D-10 — revert the `FormControl/index.css` half of 9ddee2792 rather than comment around it.**
MEASURED: `FormControl__button` appears at exactly three sites in `src/frontend`, all three
inside `Header/index.tsx` (`:196`, `:211`, `:239`), and the only reference to
`FormControl--segmented` outside its own stylesheet is the test assertion at
`headerTourAnchors.test.tsx:376`. Once the segmented wrapper dissolves under D-01 and the
fill goes away under D-02, `.FormControl--segmented > button:not(.non-important).active`
(currently `index.css:119`, with a 23-line justifying comment above it) has **no consumer
anywhere**. Restore the single combined `:active, .active` rule exactly as
`git show 9ddee2792^:src/frontend/components/UI/FormControl/index.css` has it at its
lines 83-85. `.FormControl--small .FormControl__segmentedFaIcon.brands.active` (currently
`:230`) is a DIFFERENT, icon-level selector and is untouched.

**D-11 — swap the tour's refresh and add-game steps.** `navTourAnchorCensus.test.ts` is
uniqueness-only (exactly one owning element per id, `:207/:216/:226`) and pins no order, so
anchors may move freely — CONFIRMED by reading it, and the three target suites are green at
HEAD (61 tests, 0.42s). But D-06 moves refresh to the right of the count while D-04 puts the
plus button at the left, so the existing array order
(`library-refresh` at `LibraryTour.tsx:126`, then `library-add-game` at `:134`) would walk
the footer right-to-left. Swap those two step objects so the footer is walked left-to-right.
Two lines, the anchor manifest is byte-identical, and it removes the only new backwards hop
the move introduces. The rest of the step order is left alone: it was never spatially
ordered (it opens on `library-search`, mid-panel), and reordering it wholesale is outside
the six items.

**D-12 — D-05 is delivered non-pluralised, with `{{total}}`/`{{shown}}`, and all 48
translations are rewritten rather than left stale.** The cost measurements that force this:

- `meta/__tests__/gamelibCatalogParity.test.ts:100-133` requires each plural group be
  **fully present or fully absent per that locale's own CLDR categories**. Censused the one
  existing gamelib plural key, `library.filterPanel.groupSelectedCount`: across 49
  catalogues its suffix sets are 27x `_one,_other`, 7x four-form, 6x `_many,_one,_other`,
  4x `_few,_one,_other`, 2x five-form, 1x six-form (`ar`), 1x `_few,_one,_other,_two`,
  1x `_one,_other,_two` — **135 strings for one pluralised key**. Two pluralised keys is
  ~270 strings. Non-pluralised is 48 per key.
- `count` is i18next-reserved and would itself trigger plural resolution. The existing
  comment at `Header/index.tsx:281-285` already records this and already avoids it. Keep
  `{{shown}}`/`{{total}}`.
- An empty string is NOT an escape hatch. `meta/lintTranslations.ts:404` (and `:333`) treat
  `value === ''` identically to `undefined` — both are missing — scored against
  `meta/i18nCatalogPresenceBaseline.json`, which sits at `totalPairs: 0` with its own
  `reason` field calling itself a record of a known gap and not permission to grow it.
  Independently confirmed: **0 empty-string values exist in any of the 48 non-en gamelib
  catalogues today**, so there is no precedent to lean on either.
- Appending " games" to `filteredOfTotal`'s English alone leaves 48 present-but-stale
  translations and the gate stays GREEN, because the placeholder set
  (`{{shown}}`, `{{total}}`) is unchanged. That is the "worse than a hard failure" case the
  brief forbids shipping, and it is not hypothetical: `az` already renders
  `{{total}} arasından {{shown}}` — total FIRST — so the noun's position is not even
  mechanically mirrorable from English. All 48 get rewritten.

**D-13 — correction to the brief: the derivation source is `library.filterPanel.viewAll`,
not `console.games`.** MEASURED: `console.games` is present in **0 of 48** non-en
