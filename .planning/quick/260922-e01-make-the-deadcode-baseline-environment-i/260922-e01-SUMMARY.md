# Quick Task 260922-e01: Make the dead-code baseline environment-independent Summary

Excluded six Node-version-dependent ts-prune parse-artifact "findings"
(`satisfies`/`Record`/`readonly`/`DescriptorKind`/`Parameters` mis-parses) from
`meta/findDeadcode.cjs`'s measured population via an identity-keyed
`KNOWN_PARSE_ARTIFACTS` set, shrinking both committed ledgers to match
(52->47 unreachable, 204->203 used-in-module) so macOS and CI now measure
the same population instead of macOS's superset going red in CI.

## What Shipped

**Task 1 (commit `c4c200569`):** Added `KNOWN_PARSE_ARTIFACTS` (a `Set<string>`
of six full `path - name` identities) to `meta/findDeadcode.cjs`, plus
`excludeKnownParseArtifacts(findings)` applying it before
`partitionFindings`. Both are exported for the test. Deleted the
corresponding five lines from `meta/deadcode-baseline-unreachable.txt` and
one line from `meta/deadcode-baseline-used-in-module.txt`, updated both
ledger header counts, and rewrote the three now-stale `#` annotation blocks
that had described those lines (the `releaseTags.ts` "MIXED GROUP" note, the
`selectionCount.ts` "PARSE ARTIFACTS" note, and the `InstallProgress.ts`
"PARSE ARTIFACT" note) to point at the new exclusion mechanism instead of
describing lines no longer present.

**Task 2 (commit `67888f54d`):** Added three new assertions to
`meta/__tests__/findDeadcode.test.ts`: (1) the exclusion drops a known
artifact identity, (2) a same-named finding (`Record`/`Parameters`) at a
*different* path survives the filter — the assertion that specifically
protects against a future regression to a bare-name filter, and (3) an
anti-vacuity check that no `KNOWN_PARSE_ARTIFACTS` entry is also present in
either committed ledger. The pre-existing CLI-half test (`exits 0 against
the real repo`) was left in place and still passes.
