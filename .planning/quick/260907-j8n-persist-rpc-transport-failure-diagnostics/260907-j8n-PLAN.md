<objective>
Route the shell's RPC transport-failure diagnostics through `shell_diag()` so they persist to
`gamelib-shell.log`, and add the two that are currently missing entirely.

**Purpose.** `shell_diag()` (`main.rs:8521`) writes to stderr AND appends to a file; a bare
`eprintln!` writes to stderr only, and under LaunchServices a bundled app's stderr is discarded.
Four of the shell's transport-failure diagnostics are on the wrong side of that line: two are bare
`eprintln!`, and **two do not exist at all**.

The second half is the substantive one. In `SidecarState::invoke`, the 60s timeout arm and the
disconnect arm both remove the pending entry, record the abandonment into the ring, and return an
`Err` — and **print nothing**. The `response for unknown/timed-out id=… channel=…` line at
`main.rs:8332` only ever fires **if a late response eventually arrives**. If the sidecar never
answers at all, the shell today emits NOTHING about the 60s abandonment; it is visible only as a
rejected promise in the renderer. That is a large part of why F-9 has been so hard to observe.
Adding a `shell_diag` at both arms, each naming id AND channel, is what makes the abandonment
itself observable.

**Output.** Four persisted diagnostics; one new pure helper with cargo coverage; one new CI-running
jest source gate; a dated Disposition on the F-9 todo. **The todo does not close.**
</objective>

The four lines fire only on transport FAILURE. Bounding argument, per site:
