Today GameSubMenu flips `hasShortcuts` before the shortcut write's outcome is known, because `addShortcut`/`removeShortcut` are send-kind and nothing comes back. This plan implements the LOCKED decision (option 1): both channels become invoke-kind and return a `Promise<boolean>` success outcome, and the submenu flips its label only from that resolved outcome. Purpose: a failed write (a deny-write Desktop on Windows, a missing `~/Desktop` on Linux, an unset `GAMELIB_SHELL_EXE` on macOS) no longer leaves the menu saying "Remove shortcuts", and the next click no longer runs a remove against shortcuts that were never written.
