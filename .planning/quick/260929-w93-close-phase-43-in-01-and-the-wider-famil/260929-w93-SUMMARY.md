### The two judgment calls

**`library.ts`'s Undo-affordance referent (site #1).** The citing sentence
gates on `redeemedAt !== null`. Verified directly: `redeemedAt` is a field on
the `ClaimAction` type (`HumbleKeyRow/index.tsx:80-87`, declared at line 82),
gated at `claimAction.redeemedAt !== null` (line 464). `settleAction` (line
120) carries `settledAt`, never `redeemedAt`. Anchored on `claimAction`, per
the rule resolving unambiguously from the sentence's own wording.

**The four-site `gog_keyless` note (sites #5, #8, #11, #13).** Its target is
a prose paragraph inside `classify.ts` (the QT-260908-UIC-01 note, lines
174-181), not a declaration — no single symbol names it. Anchored on the
nearest stable declaration plus a description, using identical wording at all
four sites: "the `gog_keyless` direct-redeem note above `KNOWN_GAME_KEY_TYPES`
in `classify.ts`" — so a future reader can grep one phrase and find all four.
