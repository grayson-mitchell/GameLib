<diagnosis>
This is the investigation the todo asked for. It was read at planning time (2026-10-11, HEAD
`16380bcbe`) and the executor can rely on it without repeating it. The DOM chain for the per-game
Settings dialog, from the outside in:

1. **`.MuiDialog-container`.** This is MUI 5.17.1 `DialogContainer` with `scroll="paper"`:
   `display: flex; justify-content: center; align-items: center`. It centres the paper, so a
   paper wider than the window overflows both edges by equal amounts. That matches the capture:
   row text is clipped at the left edge and `Cancel rem` is clipped at the right.

2. **The paper (`.MuiDialog-paper` plus `.InstallModal__dialog`). This is the unconstrained ancestor.**
   - MUI's `DialogPaper` sets `margin: 32px`, `overflow-y: auto`, `display: flex`,
     `flex-direction: column` and `max-width: 900px` (`maxWidth="md"`, Dialog.tsx:104).
   - `StyledPaper` (Dialog.tsx:52-62) overrides that to `maxWidth: '100%'`.
   - `SettingsModal/index.tsx:55` borrows `className={'InstallModal__dialog'}`. It has done so
     since `a40f27fa4` (2023).
   - `src/frontend/screens/Library/components/InstallModal/index.scss:1-5` gives that class
     `width: 700px; max-height: 90vh; min-width: fit-content; max-width: 80vw`.

   `min-width: fit-content` is the only declaration in the chain that can make the paper wider
   than the window, for two reasons:
   - The paper is a scroll-container flex item, so its automatic minimum is 0. Without the floor,
     `flex-shrink` would keep it inside the container.
   - `min-width` wins over every `max-width` (CSS 2.1 §10.4, css-sizing-3), so both `80vw` and
     `100%` lose.

   `fit-content` follows the intrinsic width of the tab's content. That content includes:
   - hundreds of `white-space: nowrap` row titles, meta and captions under Everything else;
   - the unwrapped max-content of the log lines;
   - the rigid German `Zwischengespeichert (Offline-Installation)` badge (Row/index.scss:179-185).

   This also explains why filtering the search to short rows restores the normal width: once the
   content's fit-content is 700px or less, `width: 700px` wins again.

3. **`.MuiDialogContent-root`** (Dialog.tsx:151; `flex: 1 1 auto; overflow-y: auto; padding: 20px 24px`)
   is stretched to the paper's width. Not a cause.

4. **`.settingsDialogContent`, then the TabPanel `div`, then its inner `div`** (DialogContent.tsx,
   TabPanel/index.tsx) are plain blocks that fill their width. Not a cause.

   One finding here is relevant later. `src/frontend/screens/Settings/sections/GamesSettings/index.scss:34-37`,
   `.settingsDialogContent section:not(:nth-of-type(0)) { min-width: 500px }` (specificity 0,2,1),
   out-specifies the Winetricks groups' own `min-width: 0` (0,2,0). The groups are `<section>`s:
   SuggestedGroup, TaskGroup and EverythingElseGroup. That floor is fixed at 500px, below the
   652px content box of a 700px paper, so it is not this defect. Task 2 files it as a
   narrow-window residual.

5. **`.WinetricksSettings` and its children.** The truncation contracts here are already right:
   - Row title, meta and caption use ellipsis with `min-width: 0` (Row/index.scss).
   - The StickyBar text is single-line ellipsis and its actions are `flex: 0 0 auto`
     (StickyBar/index.scss:28-39, E8-overflow).
   - The log region uses `white-space: pre-wrap; overflow-wrap: anywhere`
     (LogPanel/index.scss:56-66, E9-overflow).

   They only failed because the paper grew to their intrinsic width. `min-width: 0` on a flex
   child lowers only that child's own automatic minimum. It does not stop the child's content
   from feeding an ancestor's `fit-content`. That is why the root's existing `min-width: 0`
   (index.scss:24) never helped.

6. **Not on this path:**
   - `src/frontend/screens/Settings/index.css` (`.Settings`, `.settingsWrapper { max-width: 513px }`)
     styles the full-page Settings route. `Settings/index.tsx:49` hardcodes `appName = 'default'`,
     so `isDefault` is true and `shouldShowWinetricksTab` (visibility.ts) returns false. The tab
     never renders there.
   - `src/frontend/components/UI/Dialog/index.css` holds only `.log-upload-result` and `.Dialog__footer`.

**Where the fix lives, and the rejected alternatives:**
- **`max-width: 100vw` (the todo's suggestion): rejected.** `min-width` beats it. Two max-widths
  are already on the paper and both lost.
- **Editing `.InstallModal__dialog` in InstallModal/index.scss: rejected.** The Library Install
  modal and every other Settings-dialog tab rely on the fit-content floor. Changing it there is
  out of scope.
- **Dialog.tsx `StyledPaper`: rejected.** It is TSX, the brief says CSS only, and it is the
  primitive behind about 25 dialogs.
- **`contain: inline-size` on `.WinetricksSettings`: rejected.** It holds only while every
  ancestor is fill-width. Under any shrink-to-fit ancestor (for example `.settingsWrapper`'s
  `place-items: flex-start`), the tab collapses to zero width. Releasing the paper has no such hazard.
- **Chosen:** a top-level `.InstallModal__dialog:has(.WinetricksSettings) { min-width: 0; }` in the
  tab's own `index.scss`.
  - It ships with the tab.
  - It matches only while the tab is mounted, because TabPanel renders only the selected tab's children.
  - Its specificity is 0,2,0 against 0,1,0, so it wins whatever the stylesheet order.
  - `:has()` already has precedent in `src/frontend/App.css` (lines 98, 116, 139, 157),
    `src/frontend/index.scss:109` and Dialog.tsx:56.

  At the reported 1470x923 viewport, the paper becomes 700px wide with a 652px content box,
  centred with 385px on each side.

**Edits ruled out by this diagnosis:** `StickyBar/index.scss`, `LogPanel/index.scss` and
`Row/index.scss` are already correct. Do not touch them, and do not add `contain`, `max-width` or
any overflow/scroll declaration anywhere. D-02 allows one scroll container only.
</diagnosis>
