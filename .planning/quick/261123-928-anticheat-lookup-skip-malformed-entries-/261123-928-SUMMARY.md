# Quick 261123-928: anticheat lookup skips malformed entries and lower-cases the argument

`anticheat::find` now lower-cases the argument (Unicode `to_lowercase`) and `matches` answers a plain `bool`, so an upper-case namespace finds its entry and a malformed entry ahead of the match is skipped rather than ending the lookup with `null`.

## What changed

- `matches` reads through `Value::get` / `Value::as_str` chains (no panic path); non-string namespaces are never stringified; empty namespace never matches.
