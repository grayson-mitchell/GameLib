## Why the baseline is not re-run

and 060 (`z:` -> `/`, `y:` -> `$HOME`, spike 060's `d:`-`h:` -> `/Volumes`, plus `::` -> `/dev/rdisk`).

recorded numbers are the evidence. The ignore entry, not the deletion, is the fix: Wine/CrossOver
recreates these links whenever those prefixes run again.
