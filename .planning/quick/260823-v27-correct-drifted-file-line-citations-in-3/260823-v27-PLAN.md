## The constraint that shapes the fix

**Do not silently overwrite the old numbers.** Three reasons, all of them recorded defects this
project has already paid for:

1. **The numbers were right when written.** Overwriting them destroys the ability to read the
   2026-08-12 record against the tree as it stood — the same reason `34.9-GUARD-PROOF.md`'s
   AMENDMENT v2 was placed *before* §3 with §3-5 left unrewritten.
2. **For C2-01/C2-02/C2-03 the drift is by BEHAVIOUR, not just position.** Those findings quote
   `"build-steam-bridge": "esbuild ... | node"` and `... | node -` inline. That idiom no longer
   exists anywhere — its removal *is* the fix, and all three read FIXED. Repointing them at
   `package.json:72`/`:73` without saying so would assert a defect at a line that does not have it.
3. **`status:`/finding bodies are the historical record.** This document's own frontmatter comment
   says `status:` records what the review FOUND and is never rewritten when fixes land. Citations
   inside finding bodies follow the same rule.

So: **annotate in place, do not replace.** Each drifted citation keeps its original number and
gains a dated `[now …]` marker, and one amendment section carries the full mapping and the
behaviour warning.
