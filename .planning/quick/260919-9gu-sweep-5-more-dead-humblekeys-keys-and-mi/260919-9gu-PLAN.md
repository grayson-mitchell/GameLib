# Quick Task 260919-9gu — migrate `humbleKeys` to the `gamelib` namespace

Executes the operator's decision on **Direction step 3**: option **(b)**, migrate the
live keys into the fork-owned namespace and then fill, rather than filling the upstream
catalog in place. Option (a) was cheaper to start and left the keys permanently outside
every gate; (b) costs the same number of strings and makes any future gap CI-visible.

## The atomicity constraint

Task 2 alone turns CI **red**: the presence baseline sits at `totalPairs: 0`, so 54 × 48
unfilled pairs are a hard failure. Tasks 2 and 3 therefore ship as **one commit**. That red
is not a defect — it is the migration working, and it is the entire point of choosing (b).

**Do NOT "fix" it by regenerating the baseline.** The file's own `reason` string calls it
"a RECORD of a known gap, not a permission to grow it". After a complete fill the baseline
stays valid at `totalPairs: 0` untouched — nothing to regenerate.
