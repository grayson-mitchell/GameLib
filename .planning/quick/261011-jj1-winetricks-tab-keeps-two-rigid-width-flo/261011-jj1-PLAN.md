**Badge shrink factor: `flex: 0 1 auto`, the same as the title, NOT a lower fractional factor.** The brief
suggested a lower factor. These are the reasons for not using one:
- Flexbox distributes a deficit in proportion to flex-shrink times flex-base-size. The title's floor is
  `min-width: 0`.
- A factor below the title's cannot make the badge yield *after* the title. It only slows the badge down. The
  badge absorbs the whole remaining deficit only once the title is frozen at 0 width.
- With f = the badge's factor, A = the room for title plus badge after meta has collapsed, B = the badge's
  natural width and T = the title's, the title keeps T·(A - (1-f)·B)/(T + f·B). It reaches 0 whenever
  A < (1-f)·B.
- Desk estimate for the target regime, unmeasured: a ~600px window in `de` gives A ≈ 144px, the German badge
  B ≈ 230px at 0.6875rem, and a typical title T ≈ 175px.
  - f = 1: the title keeps ≈ 62px and the badge ≈ 82px. Each keeps the same ~36% fraction of its natural width.
  - f = 0.5: the title keeps ≈ 17px.
  - f near 0: the title keeps 0, leaving a row that shows "Zwischengespeichert (Offl…" and not which verb it is.
- The title is the row's identity and the badge is a modifier, so f = 1 is chosen. The resulting order is:
  meta gives way first (3x weight, existing behaviour), then the title and the badge together in proportion.
  Neither ever paints over the status slot, and both carry a native tooltip.
- Cost: at moderate widths, such as hh5's ~700px paper, the German badge may now ellipsize slightly where it
  previously fitted. Task 3 adds this to the owed live capture.
