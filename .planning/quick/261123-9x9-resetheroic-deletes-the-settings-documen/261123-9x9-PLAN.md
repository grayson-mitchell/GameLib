`resetHeroic` deletes `config.json` and `GamesConfig/` through the Rust guard, and the shell evicts the open stores, but TS never drops the matching mirror cells. Until the 1 s relaunch fires, two things go wrong. First, `readSettingsDoc` and `settingsDocExists` keep serving the deleted documents. The fix is the TS fix the todo chose, not the cross-layer shell reset-event option:
1. Add a `MirrorRegistry` method that drops cells by path predicate.
2. Add a settingsDocs helper that drops every cell whose path `docKeyOf` recognises.
3. Give `resetHeroic` and `registerShellFilesFlows` a `storeRoot` dep, and call the helper after `removed.outcome === 'removed'`.
4. Add the jest test the todo specifies: run a reset with a populated settings cell; `readSettingsDoc` must miss afterwards, and a later write must be visible.

Purpose: a reset stops serving deleted settings and stops swallowing the next settings write during the relaunch window.
