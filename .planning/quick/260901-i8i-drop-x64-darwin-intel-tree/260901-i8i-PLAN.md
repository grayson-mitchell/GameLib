<w1_decision>
## RC-3 resolved: Option A — remove the entry, update both assertions

**Decision: remove `'x86_64-apple-darwin'` from `NATIVE_LZMA_REQUIRED_TRIPLES`, reword the doc
comment from "four" to "three", and update BOTH assertions in
`buildSidecarSea.test.ts:640-651`.**

Reasoning, and why this does not violate the plan's own scope-out of `buildSidecarSea.ts`:

1. **`buildSidecarSea.ts` holds two different kinds of thing, and only one is scoped out.**
   The triple-parameterised *capability* (`sidecarOutputPath`, `nodeDistUrls`, `triplePlatform`,
   `expectedMachoArch`, …) must keep `x86_64-apple-darwin` support — the linux/windows legs need
   multi-triple handling and those tests pass triples as explicit arguments. But
   `NATIVE_LZMA_REQUIRED_TRIPLES` is not capability; it is a **declaration of what ships**. It is
   the release matrix, restated in a second file.
2. **Consistency with RC-1 forces this.** This plan already insists that
   `releaseWorkflow.test.ts:181`'s `toHaveLength(4)` must become `toHaveLength(3)`. Fixing one
   restatement of the shipping-leg count while leaving the other at four would be the same
   split-record defect the plan exists to prevent — and would be a fresh instance of
   `status-doc-can-lag-two-gate-runs-undetected`.
3. **The functional consequence is correct, not merely tolerable.** `resolveNativeLzmaAsset()`
   (`:577-594`) consults the array only for the triple currently being built. Dropping the entry
   means an ad-hoc `x86_64-apple-darwin` SEA build no longer hard-fails on a missing
   lzma-native prebuild. That is right: you do not hard-gate the prebuild inventory of a target
   you never publish.
4. **The array's own convention does not cover retention.** Its comment says a triple whose
   prebuild went missing "would be named here as excluded rather than added" — that is about
   *exclusion on prebuild grounds*, a different axis from *retirement of the triple*. It does
   not license keeping a retired triple.

**Option B (annotate and retain) was considered and rejected**, despite the `260823-v27`
annotate-don't-rewrite precedent. That precedent governs **recorded measurements** — historical
observations that were true when taken. `NATIVE_LZMA_REQUIRED_TRIPLES` is not a measurement; it
is a live declaration consulted at build time. Annotating a live declaration to say it is wrong,
rather than correcting it, is how a false record survives a sweep.

**Scope guard:** this licenses editing exactly ONE array entry, its doc comment, and the two
assertions that bind it. It does NOT license touching any other part of `buildSidecarSea.ts` or
`buildSidecarSea.test.ts` — in particular the `x86_64-apple-darwin` cases at `:435`, `:492`,
`:503`, `:533` and their tests stay exactly as they are.
</w1_decision>
