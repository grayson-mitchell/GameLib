## The mechanism, stated narrowly (this is what makes the scope decision)

GNU tar's `host:path` remote-spec parsing applies to **the archive name only** — the `-f`
operand. That is precisely what `--force-local` overrides ("archive file is local even if it has
a colon"). `-C <dir>` is not remote-parsed; it is a chdir target tar passes through verbatim.

So the invariant to establish is narrow, and it is NOT "no absolute paths in the argv":

> **The `-f` operand must carry no drive letter. Every other path operand may stay absolute.**

This is why the transform is not uniform across the five sites, and why three of them need no
change at all.
