# Quick Task 260905-jg4

## Measured ground truth (re-verified at HEAD, 2026-09-05)

- The inertness pin `loggerFlows.test.ts` → "this file's own jest.mock('os') is
  INERT -- setupContainment's wins" **passes at HEAD**. The finding still holds.
- Census by comment-stripped source (`backend/testUtils/stripSourceComments`),
  not raw grep: **26** files under `src/` carry a code-level
  `jest.mock('os', ...)`. Two are not suite-level shadowed mocks —
  `jest.setupContainment.ts` (the EFFECTIVE one) and
  `stripSourceComments.test.ts` (string-literal fixtures). **24** suite-level
  declarations are shadowed. A further 9 files match only in prose.
  The todo's "31 files" was a raw grep and over-counted.
- `testContainment.test.ts` **Block A does not depend on the `os` mock.** Its two
  assertions are RED-PROOFed (34.2-18-SUMMARY.md, quoted in-file) against the
  `../pathShim` and `backend/logger/paths` mocks only, and its module docstring
  already states the proven property is one "a bare `os.homedir()` mock does NOT
  have". No re-pointing of assertions is required — only its docstring's
  numbered kit list (item 1) misstates that the file's own mock is what
  redirects `homedir()`.
- Block B's source gate matches `../pathShim`, `backend/logger/paths` and
  `getLogFilePath({})` — **not** `jest.mock('os'`. Deleting the `os` mocks would
  not break Block B.

## Decision — KEEP the mocks, correct the documentation

Rejected: deleting the 24 shadowed declarations. They are genuine, currently
shadowed defence-in-depth — if the `setupFiles` registration were ever removed
they become effective again, which is exactly the failure this repo already
suffered once (a hand-maintained per-suite list that rotted). Deleting them
trades a documentation defect for a containment regression risk across 24 files.

Adopted rule: **`jest.setupContainment.ts` shadows every per-suite `os` mock in
the Backend project. Suite-level `os` mocks are inert-but-retained
defence-in-depth. No suite may describe its own `os` mock as load-bearing.**
