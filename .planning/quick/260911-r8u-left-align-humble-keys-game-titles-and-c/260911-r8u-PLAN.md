<decision_wrapped_titles>
**Resolved in favour of BOTH declarations. `align-items: flex-start` alone is not enough.**

`align-items: flex-start` on a column flex container shrink-wraps the title span to its
content width. For a SINGLE-line title that makes the inherited `text-align: center`
invisible — the box is exactly as wide as the text, so centring inside it is a no-op, and the
box's left edge lands on the track start. That satisfies the live gate's metric, which
measures the left edge of the title block.

But for a title that WRAPS to two lines the shrink-wrapped box is as wide as the LONGEST
line, and the inherited `center` still centres the SHORTER line inside that box. The gate
would read a stable left edge while the rendered text was visibly ragged. Long wrapping
titles are known to exist in this list (`.humbleKeyTypeCell`'s own comment at `:250-255`
documents the two-line wrap as the intended behaviour of this row family).

So: `align-items: flex-start` on `.humbleKeyGameCell` (places the box) **and**
`text-align: start` on `.humbleKeyRowTitle` (aligns the lines inside it). The first satisfies
the metric; the second satisfies the actual requirement. Shipping only the first would be
shipping to the gate rather than to the decision.

Note the asymmetry with the header: `.humbleKeysColumnHeader` is a GRID container, not a flex
one, so the flex-only `align-items: flex-start` instrument does not apply there. Its three
labels are grid items that stretch to their tracks, and the correct instrument is the
inherited `text-align: start` on the container. One rule, two mechanisms, because the two
surfaces use two different layout models.
</decision_wrapped_titles>
