# Phase quick-261128-22q Plan 01: Humble classifier resolves zone-less and date-only expiries at their own date's host offset Summary

`LocalZone` is now `Fixed(secs)` or `Host(&dyn HostZone)`, and a zone-less or date-only Humble expiry is converted to UTC with the host offset in force at that local time, following ECMA-262 `UTC(t)` the way V8 does it. A winter expiry synced in summer no longer reads a day early.
