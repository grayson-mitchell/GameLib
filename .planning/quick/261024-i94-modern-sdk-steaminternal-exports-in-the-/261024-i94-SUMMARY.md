The x64 bridge shim now exports the five SDK 1.58+ `SteamInternal_*` entry points, and placement now sees steam_api imports made by PEs other than the launch exe.

## What changed

**Part 1.** `SteamInternal_SteamAPI_Init` (HELLO via `SteamAPI_Init`, 0 on OK, 2 on failure), `_ContextInit` (callback and counter protocol), `_FindOrCreateUserInterface` and `_FindOrCreateGameServerInterface` (same lookup and null-object fallback as `SteamInternal_CreateInterface`), and `_GameServer_Init_V2` (always 1) are added to `MODERN_LOOKUP_FLAT_EXPORTS`. `steam_api_shim.c` and `steam_api64.def` are regenerated. `SHIM64_EXPORTED_SYMBOLS` is re-pinned to 49 symbols. The two FindOrCreate* names join `RUNTIME_LOOKUP_SYMBOLS`. `SHIM_EXPORT_SET_VERSION` changes automatically, which re-flags persisted verdicts (intended).

**Part 2.** `findSteamApiImporterPes` (bounded to depth 4 and 512 files, symlink-safe, sequential, bottle-contained through the new `resolveWalkRoot`) runs in two places:
- Exe-imports branch: same-arch importer symbols are merged into the required set, and their version strings are read when the lookup family is used. It runs only after the byte-identical no-op return.
- Runtime-loaded branch: the launch exe's arch (`readPeMachineArch`, a new zero-import COFF read, plus `readExeMachineArch`) selects which shipped dll to evaluate. Each considered dll gets coverage from its same-arch importers, or keeps the `@runtime-loaded` marker when none exist. Every shipped dll is still replaced. A fully covered tree returns `placed` with `shimPaths` and a clean verdict. games.ts needed no change.

## Scope decisions

- **x64 only. x86 is not free.** 51-15 (D-51-13) made the modern lookup family Win64-only. x86 has no `SteamInternal_CreateInterface`, no `SteamAPI_GetHSteamUser`/`GetHSteamPipe` and no null-object fallback, and a modern-SDK x86 context init also calls `SteamAPI_GetHSteamUser`. Exporting the five on x86 means widening the whole family to x86. `steam_api.def` and `SHIM_EXPORTED_SYMBOLS` are unchanged (verified: regenerated x86 .def has no diff, built `steam_api.dll` has 0 `SteamInternal_` exports).
- **Godot 322 MB "could not parse the exe as a PE" is left out.** `detectExeSteamDrm` already does fd-ranged reads with no size cap and no whole-file read, so this is not a read-limit fix. The failure likely comes from the PE's own layout (for example the resource-RVA container lookup on Godot's appended-pck section layout), and finding it needs the actual binary. Suggested follow-up todo: reproduce with the Overlooting (3410180) exe and trace which `readExact` in `detectSteamDrmInPe` fails.
- **meta/bridgeCensus.ts is unchanged.** It already classifies runtime-loaded titles as `missing-symbols` (counted not covered). Teaching the census the importer walk is a follow-up.
- **The todo was not moved or closed.**
