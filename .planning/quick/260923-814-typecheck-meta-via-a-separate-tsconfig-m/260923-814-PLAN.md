Three design questions are DECIDED here. Do not reopen them; implement them.

second `tsc` invocation in `codecheck`. Option 1 (widening `tsconfig.json`'s `include` to

measured that as 73 newly-exposed used-in-module identities against a ledger that closed at 0
yesterday. `tsconfig.json` is not edited at all -- not `include`, not `exclude`, not
`compilerOptions`. Typecheck coverage and ledger reduction are two different goods.

**D2 -- the include set is `["meta", "src/common/typedefs/*.d.ts"]`, and it is MEASURED, not
guessed.** All four candidates were run against the live tree at c43d6bdaf:

and `extra-mock-function.ts` augments `backend/config`, which is not in this program.

uses `entry.split(/:(.*)/s)`; the `s` flag needs `target: es2018+` and the inherited target is

structural weakness of Option 2 is "a second source of compiler settings that can drift from the
first" (the todo's own words), and a zero-`compilerOptions` config is the only version of Option 2
with no drift surface at all. If `meta/` later genuinely needs es2018+ SYNTAX, that is a deliberate
change with its own todo -- not a side effect of this one.
