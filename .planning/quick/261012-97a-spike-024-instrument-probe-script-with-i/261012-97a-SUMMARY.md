## What it means

- **The todo's question has a measured answer: no head resource.** `inflight` is empty by
  construction (Resource Timing lists only finished fetches) and the set-difference of head
  scripts/stylesheets against timing entries is empty on every blank arm. The parser is done;
  the page is simply not being rendered.
- **Mechanism, reproduced both ways:** host window occluded ⇒ WebKit stops rendering updates
  (no paint, no rAF); Epic's app boot waits on one, so the title stays the server's. Un-occlude
  ⇒ boots and paints within the next sample. Steam shows the same unpainted state under
  pre-load occlusion, so it is WebKit, not Epic.
- **Not a shipping blocker** for the raw-subview design: the user is not looking at an occluded
  window, and it recovers when they are. The sign-in-button design todo no longer waits on it.
- **Open and narrower:** the mid-parse stall (`readyState=loading`, `isLoading=true`, 938 k,
  runs 15–19) did not recur in 8 arms and two occluded arms parsed to completion, so occlusion
  is not its cause. Its offset is fixed to ±1 character within a day; what sits there is the
  next measurement. `curl` cannot inspect the document (`403 cf-mitigated: challenge`).
