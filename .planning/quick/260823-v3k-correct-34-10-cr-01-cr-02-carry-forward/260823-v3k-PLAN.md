# Quick task 260823-v3k — 34.10's CR-01/CR-02 carry-forward is stale, and stale in two different directions

## Why this exists

Asked "what is blocking 34.10?", the honest answer is **nothing** — it closed 2026-08-09 on live
gate run 4's 5/5, `34.10-VERIFICATION.md` is `status: passed` with `human_verification: []`.

But `ROADMAP.md`'s 34.10 block still reads:

> **two CONFIRMED Critical findings, deliberately NOT fixed in this phase** [...] They are deferred
> on the operator's explicit decision

That sentence is 14 days out of date. Phase 34.11 took both under decisions **D-31** (CR-01) and
**D-32** (CR-02), in a CONTEXT.md section headed "Carried forward from Phase 34.10", and plan
`34.11-03` implemented them. The block's *sibling* paragraph for WR-01 **was** updated when WR-01
closed on 2026-08-23 (quick task `260823-tct`) — so the CR paragraphs are now inconsistent with
their own neighbours, which is exactly what makes a reader take them at face value.

## The trap this task exists to avoid

The obvious correction — "mark both CLOSED" — is **wrong**, and re-grepping caught it.

`34.10-REVIEW.md`'s CR-01 heading reads *"undefined [...] in **8 of 11 themes**, including a
live-gate-scored theme"*. Plan `34.11-03` declared `--navbar-active` in **`gruvbox_dark` only** —
the scored theme, which is what D-31 undertook. It did not touch the other 7, and it did not add a
fallback chain at the consuming site. Recording CR-01 as flatly "closed" would retire a finding
whose majority is still live, and would do it in the phase's own permanent record.

This is the `threat-register-ranges-hide-uncovered-ids` shape: an audit unit coarser than the
defect's unit. The defect's unit is **the theme block**, not the finding ID.
