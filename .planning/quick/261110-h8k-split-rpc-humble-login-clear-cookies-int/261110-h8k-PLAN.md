Pin mechanics that constrain the split (main.rs tests):
- F-34.4.2-12: `current_arm` is set only on a column-0 `fn`/`pub(crate) fn` line whose
  `rpc_fn_name` is in `target_arms`. Any other column-0 fn resets it to None, which means non-rpc
  step fns are currently INVISIBLE to the pin. For each `.cookies()` site it walks backward to the
  nearest `#[cfg(` line and STOPS at a trimmed line that starts with `fn ` or `pub(crate) fn `. So a
  site inside a step fn whose cfg sits on the fn's own attribute line would get guard None and
  fail. That is why both `.cookies()` sites stay inline in the handler (Task 2), and the
  predicate, walk-back and `expected` vec are not touched.
- Window-branch pin: `start` = line containing `concat!("fn rpc_humble_login", "_clear_cookies(")`,
  `end` = next line exactly `}`. It requires `WKWebsiteDataTypeCookies`/`DiskCache`/`MemoryCache`,

  lines, and that the window does NOT contain `fn clear_default_data_store_cookies_for_domain`.

  `matching.len()` anywhere, at least 2 `verified_delete_count(` occurrences, and
