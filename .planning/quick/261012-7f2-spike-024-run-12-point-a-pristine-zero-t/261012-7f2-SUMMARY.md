## Method notes

- macOS has no `timeout(1)`; the first launch silently ran nothing and `: > run.log` truncated a
  TRACKED file. Pattern used after: record `wc -l` of `run.log`, run, `tail -n +N` into
  `run-<n>.log`, `git checkout -- run.log`.
- The harness exports `events-export.json` on exit with the raw IP; restore it after each run.
- Rust-side `WKWebView.title`/`URL` at 20 s and 40 s before the probe is what separates
  "rendered" from "stalled" without touching the page: the stalled state carries the server's
  initial short `<title>`, the rendered state the long one the app sets after boot.
