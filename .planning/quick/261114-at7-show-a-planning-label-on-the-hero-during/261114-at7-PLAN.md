## Root cause as traced from source by the planner (Task 1 re-confirms it, or corrects it)

The status chain:
1. `src/backend/downloadmanager/utils.ts` ~L486: `updateGame` sends `{ status: 'updating' }` with
   no context, then calls `library[runner].getGame(appName).update(...)`.
2. `src/backend/storeManagers/steam/games.ts` `update()` (L455) routes bridge installs to
   `updateBridgeGame` -> `runBridgeUpdate` in `src/backend/storeManagers/steam/updateBridge.ts`.
3. `runBridgeUpdate` does `resolveBridgeUpdateContext`, then `estimateBridgeUpdate` (L145-147,
   can be skipped by `freshCachedEstimate`), then `getDiskInfo`, then `downloadSteamDepotsRust`.
   No status is sent anywhere in this stretch.
4. `downloadSteamDepotsRust` (`depotRust/install.ts`) runs `buildDepotRefs`, content-server
   hosts, `readAcfInstalledDepots` and one `fetchDepotKey` per retired depot, then
   `createProgressBridge(opts.installRuntime.depotJobs.transport, appId)` (~L264) and
   `runDepotJob`. Again no status.
5. Only the Rust job emits `stage: delta_planning` (`gamelib-depot/src/delta.rs:997`), then later
   `planned` (`delta.rs:1428`), progress, and `stage: delta_committing` (`delta.rs:1585`).
6. `progressBridge.ts` L45-58: `stage` sends `status 'updating'` + planning/committing context;
   `planned` only records `totalBytes`. Progress goes on the separate `progressUpdate` channel
   (`sendProgressUpdate`, `backend/utils.ts` ~L1568), which does not touch `libraryStatus`.
7. Frontend: `libraryState.ts` `handleGameStatus` stores `{status, context}` per game;
   `DownloadHero/index.tsx` L81 calls `heroPhase(libraryStatus entry)`; `heroModel.ts` L124-130
   maps `updating` + `steam-update-planning` to `update-planning` ("Planning update…") and makes
   the bar indeterminate with no transfer stats.

Findings:
- UAT 6b (items 5/6, 2026-11-13 ~05:xx) ran BEFORE `378aa80b1` (19:28 the same day, "admit the
  delta stage event in the depotJobEvent schema", UAT 18). Until then every `stage` event was
  rejected as `IPC_INVALID_ARGS ... channel=depotJobEvent ... invalid_union`, so neither stage
  label could ever show. That was the primary cause of 6b and is already fixed: UAT item 21
  (after the fix) showed "Applying update…".
- WR-07 remains real on current source: (a) the pre-job TS work in steps 3-4 runs under the
  context-less `updating` from step 1, so the hero shows "0%" and nothing else; (b) nothing
  clears `steam-update-planning` on `planned`, so after the schema fix the hero would show
  "Planning update…" with an indeterminate bar and no stats for the whole transfer, until
  `delta_committing`.
- A Rust delta can fall back to a full reconcile (`DeltaRoute::Fallback`, `delta.rs:955`); the
  stale-journal fallback at `delta.rs:992` returns before `delta_planning` is emitted. So the
  clear on `planned` must key off "this bridge belongs to an update job", not off "a planning
  stage was seen", or a TS-emitted planning label would stick through the fallback transfer.
