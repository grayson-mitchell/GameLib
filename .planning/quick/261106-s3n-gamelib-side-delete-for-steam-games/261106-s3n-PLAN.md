Discretion choices made in this plan (document them in the SUMMARY):
- `UninstallApp` is used whenever the delete setting is ON; it is NOT additionally gated on
  `steamExperimentalInstallApp` (D-01: separate key; the Decided section names UninstallApp the preferred
  path).
- Any EAppUpdateError 18 falls back to the file delete (the Decided section lists 18 as a fallback
  trigger). `installedApps` (GetNumInstalledApps) only changes the log line ("Steam signed out" vs
  "Steam reports not installed").
- A non-zero EAppUpdateError OTHER than 18 deletes nothing: Steam made a definite refusal, so GameLib
  reports it ("Steam couldn't uninstall {{game}} (error {{code}})") and clears Uninstalling.
- The helper is macOS arm64 only; on Windows/Linux (and Intel Mac) the wrapper reports `unavailable`
  without spawning, so ON goes straight to the platform-generic file-delete fallback.
- ON only changes the NATIVE root. Bridge-bottle and CrossOver-bottle roots keep their existing direct
  deletion paths untouched, and the frontend only switches to GameLib's dialog for native-root installs.
