<objective>
Fix the macOS Steam bridge helper after the Nov 10 2026 Mac Steam client update. The update broke every bridge launch.

Root cause, verified by the orchestrator. The shipped `Steam Helper.app/.../libsteam_api.dylib` now exports `SteamAPI_SteamUser_v024` and no longer exports `SteamAPI_SteamUser_v023`. The generated `bridge_dispatch.h` dlsyms only the fixed name `"SteamAPI_SteamUser_v023"` (the steamuser023/016/018/019/021 slots all go through that one name). So `g_bd_self_steamuser023` is NULL. CONTROL WHOAMI then answers STATUS_ERR even though `READY init=ok`. `ensureBridgeHelperReady` reads that as not-inited, for both the real AppID and the 480 fallback, and the user sees "Steam bridge unavailable".

Approved scope:
1. In the generator (`meta/gen_vtables.ts`), resolve every versioned accessor by probing at runtime from newest to oldest. Log the resolved version once per accessor.
2. Make the failure diagnosable:
   - The helper logs a MISSING line.
   - WHOAMI returns a distinct reason byte.
   - The TS side maps it to a distinct outcome and UI reason, with no 480 fallback.

Out of scope, recorded by the orchestrator as a separate todo: pinning a fixed libsteam_api.dylib.

Output:
- the generator change and regenerated header
- the helper C changes
- the TS reason mapping, UI and en i18n key
- tests
- the rebuilt helper, verified live
</objective>
