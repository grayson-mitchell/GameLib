<objective>
Remove the logged-out sidebar anti-pattern. Today, when no store account is
logged in, `SidebarLinks` renders a `Login` item promoted to the very top of the
menu and suppresses the `Manage Accounts` item entirely. When any account is
logged in, the reverse happens. Both the item's label and its menu position
change based on login state, which yanks focus and makes the menu unstable.

Fix: always render a single item labelled "Manage Accounts", in its normal
(non-promoted) position, pointing at `/login`, regardless of login state.

Purpose: a stable, predictable navigation menu. Menu identity and ordering
should not be a function of session state.
Output: `SidebarLinks` renders one unconditional Manage Accounts item;
`SidebarTour` targets it unconditionally; regression tests pin both label and
position across logged-out and logged-in contexts.
</objective>
