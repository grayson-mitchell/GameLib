anywhere — not in meta/\_\_tests\_\_, not in src/\*\*/\_\_tests\_\_, nowhere"*. That search was over
**test/mock files only**. It never looked at non-test `meta/` files, and three entries have a live
non-test importer or a deliberate re-export:

| `meta/buildRunnersOnedir.ts - InvocationForm` | `meta/runnerBuildInvocations.ts:62` does `import type { InvocationForm, OnedirRunnerName } from './buildRunnersOnedir'`. Invisible to ts-prune only because `tsconfig.json`'s `include: ["src"]` never loads that importer. |

Two bucket-A entries pointed the other way — the name-grep *inflated* them and they ARE safe:

**This is the finding worth keeping from this task**, and it is why the ledger's annotation must be
corrected in Task 3 rather than left to mislead the next reader.
