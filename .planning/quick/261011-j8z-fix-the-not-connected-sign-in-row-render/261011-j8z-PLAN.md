<objective>
Make the Library sign-in row (`LibrarySignInNotice`) readable on its banner in every theme the app ships, and pin that with a per-theme WCAG gate that sees F-49-R1-6.

**The defect (traced and measured at planning).** `src/frontend/screens/Library/components/LibrarySignInNotice/index.scss`, `&__row`, paints `background: var(--navbar-background)` and `color: var(--text-default)`. `--text-default` is the BODY surface's text token. It has no relationship to the navbar surface. nord-light is the one theme whose navbar is dark against a light page (banner vs page 6.40:1; every other theme 1.07 to 1.56:1). So its dark body text (`#20242c`) sits on a dark band (`#4c566a`): 2.11:1. The other nine themes pass only because their navbar and body surfaces are both dark. G-48-7 recorded the same structural shape for the FOCUS ROW divider (`FilterFocusRow/index.scss`).

**Three more foregrounds on the same banner carry the same defect:**
- The not-connected store logo: `&__row--notConnected .LibrarySignInNotice__icon`, `color: var(--text-default)`.
- The dismiss glyph: `&__dismiss`, `color: var(--text-default)`.
- The footer button label ("Sign in" / "Reconnect"). Its colour depends on which stylesheet has loaded:
  - Fresh launch: the base `.button` rule paints `var(--primary)` fill with `var(--text-tertiary)` text.
  - Once the Settings chunk loads, `Settings/index.css` `.button.is-footer` paints a transparent fill with `var(--text-default)`, which puts the label directly on the banner.
  - nord-light fails in both states (2.59 fresh, 2.11 Settings-loaded). zombie declares no `--primary`, so on a fresh launch the fill drops out and `#070a0b` sits on the banner at 1.55:1.

How the other states map onto this table:
- The Settings-loaded button label BEFORE equals the row BEFORE column.
- The store logo and the dismiss glyph BEFORE equal the row BEFORE column. They are non-text, so the bar is 3:1, and nord-light fails it at 2.11.
- AFTER, all four foregrounds equal the row AFTER column, because they inherit it.

The resolver's BEFORE column agrees with all four of the operator's live verdicts: nord_light fail, and midnightMirage, gruvbox_dark and dracula pass.

**The fix: one token pairing that holds by construction (no per-theme override).**
- The row's `color` moves from `var(--text-default)` to `var(--navbar-inactive, var(--navbar-accent))`. That is the navbar surface's own text chain: `NavItem/index.scss` paints it as `--navitem-inactive-color` on the tier-2 panel, whose background is `var(--navbar-background)` (pinned by G-48-7's non-vacuity test).
  - `--navbar-accent` is declared in all 10 theme blocks.
  - `--navbar-inactive` is declared in midnightMirage and nord-light (`#ffffff`).
  - It clears 4.5:1 in all 10 themes. The minimum is marine at 4.87.
- The logo, the dismiss glyph and the in-row footer button all become `color: inherit`, so the row has one pairing and no second token can drift.
- The in-row footer button also gets `background-color: transparent`. It goes in hj6's existing three-class rule, `.LibrarySignInNotice__row .button.is-footer`, which hj6 wrote so that load order cannot decide the outcome. With it, the label sits on the banner with the row's pair in both load states.

Per the orchestrator's preference, a per-theme override is not used, because a pairing that holds by construction exists and passes 10/10.

**Rejected alternatives, measured:**
- A nord-light-only `body.nord-light &` override. It would leave a body-surface token on the navbar surface in the other nine themes, correct there only because both surfaces happen to be dark.
- `--navbar-accent` alone: nord-light 2.74.
- `--text-title`: nord-light 2.11.
- `--accent`: nord-light 1.39.
- A nord-light `--text-default` change in `themes.scss`. It would break nord-light's dark body text on its light page, which is the same reason G-48-7 rejected it.
- Repainting the banner surface. The `--navbar-background` band is Phase 49 D-09's design, and changing it would be a separate decision.

**Trade-offs this accepts (record them; do not reverse them):**
- In the nine themes that already passed, the row text colour changes. The three themes the operator passed barely move: midnightMirage `#caf3fd` to `#d1e4e9`, gruvbox_dark `#eae8e5` to `#f9f5d7`, dracula `white` to `#eae8e5`.
- cyberSpaceOasis, marine and zombie take their navbar accent hue (amber `#e0ab40`, gold `#d39f37`, green `#59c627`). Those themes' tier-2 nav rows already paint that hue on the same surface.
- On a fresh launch, the in-row footer button stops being a `--primary`-filled button and becomes the transparent footer button that `is-footer` is designed to be. That is already how it renders once Settings loads, and already how zombie renders it.

**Out of scope, recorded as a residual todo in Task 3 (all nord-light, all pre-existing):**
- (a) Dismiss hover `var(--text-secondary, var(--text-default))` measures 1.52 on the banner, under the 3:1 non-text floor. dracula is 4.27, which passes 3:1.
- (b) The expired-row triangle `var(--status-danger, orange)` (`#f97881`) measures 2.82, under 3:1.
- (c) The in-row button's hover/focus-visible state comes from Settings' `.button.is-footer:hover` (0,3,0), which ties hj6's (0,3,0) rule on source order. When it wins, nord-light paints `--text-hover` `#5e81ac` on `--background-darker` `#eceff4`: 3.50, under 4.5.

None of these is the "text on the banner" defect. Each needs a hue or state decision, so none is fixed here. The banner border (`--neutral-04`, 1.03 to 2.64) is decorative, and WCAG 1.4.11 does not require it on a non-interactive container. It is not recorded.

**Threshold.** The gate uses 4.5:1, WCAG 1.4.3 AA for normal text. That is both the orchestrator's default and the convention the gpn gate (`Winetricks enabled Apply ...
