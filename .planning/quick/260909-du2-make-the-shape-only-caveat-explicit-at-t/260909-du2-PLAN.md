# Quick 260909-du2 — name the shape-only caveat at the exemption site

## Origin

`.planning/todos/pending/2026-09-07-t-exemption-fires-on-shape-alone-never-a-real-call-site.md`
(41-REVIEW.md WR-02). Two acceptable dispositions were offered:

## The measurement (2026-09-09) — option 1 is REFUTED

A ts-morph census replaying both exemption predicates over all 174 files of

| `src/frontend/screens/Library/facetLabels.ts`                           | 8  | 0 | **no** |

The footprint grew from the 4 files the review measured to 6. Decisively: **three of the six are
pure cross-module label tables with no `t()` call anywhere in the file** — `consoleSteamTarget.ts`,
`stateLabels.ts`, `facetLabels.ts` export their tables and the consumer calls `t(key, default)`.
A file-local dataflow check convicts 22 literals across three genuinely-compliant files. That is
exactly the failure [[a-gate-can-convict-correct-code]] warns about, and exactly the outcome the
todo said would be "worse than the hole it closes".

So option 1 is not viable at file scope, and cross-module tracing is out of proportion to a hole
with zero live instances. Disposition: **option 2**, plus recording the census so nobody rebuilds
the refuted fix.
