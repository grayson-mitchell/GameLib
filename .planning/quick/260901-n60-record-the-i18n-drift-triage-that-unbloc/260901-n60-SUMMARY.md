## Outcome

**The blocker was based on a false premise and is lifted.** The drift is 78 added keys, all
under `public/locales/en/`; the two upstream refreshes touch 73 files across 45 locales
with **zero** under `en/`. Disjoint file sets — a pull cannot mask the drift.

**All three complications the todo listed were falsified:**

1. Masking risk — impossible; disjoint files.
2. `en/translation.json` "+290 lines needs hand-resolution" — upstream touches no `en/`
   file, so nothing is contested.
3. `i18nCatalogChurnGuard` "may not distinguish a replay from a fork edit" — it reads
   `git diff --name-only` (unstaged only), so a committed refresh is invisible to it.

**One real complication was found that the todo missed:** nine further upstream commits
between fork base `b5b5cad3f` and `c39d40174` also touch `public/locales/`, so a two-commit
cherry-pick will not apply cleanly. 57 of the 73 target files differ from the upstream
commits' parents — all of it intermediate *upstream* drift, none of it fork drift. Two pull
options are now documented in the todo, with Option A (wholesale non-English content at
`c39d40174`) recommended and its safety argued from `printExtraTransations = false`.

**Drift shape (measured, HEAD `d730934ad`, clean tree):** 78 added, **0 removed, 0 English
values changed**. Four buckets: 4 test-only sentinels leaking via the parser's input glob,
6 `{{count}}` plural expansions (possibly a live bug), 1 lexer-unresolvable default, 67 real
fork-authored strings that never got a catalog entry.

**Structural finding:** the pre-push `pnpm i18n --fail-on-update` gate and D-05's churn
guard are in direct contradiction on this drift. The only resolution that satisfies both is
to re-namespace the fork-authored keys through `gamelib:` — recorded as the new todo's job.

**Incidental correction:** `pnpm i18n --fail-on-update` **writes nothing** (verified twice);
only bare `pnpm i18n` writes.
