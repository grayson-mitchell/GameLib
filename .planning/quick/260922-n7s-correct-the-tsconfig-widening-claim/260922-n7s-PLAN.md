# Plan — correct the tsconfig-widening claim in all four places

## The claim, and why it is wrong

Both dead-code ledgers, the pending todo, and `STATE.md` all say some form of:

> Widening `tsconfig.json` to cover `meta/` would dissolve the false-positive part of this
> cluster, but that is a tsconfig decision with its own blast radius.

`260922-lh4`'s handoff went further and called it *"the single highest-leverage move left on this
todo."* **Measured 2026-09-22, that is false — widening makes the ledger bigger.**

The claim only does half the arithmetic. `tsconfig.json` has `include: ["src"]`, so adding `meta`
does dissolve findings whose importer was previously invisible — but it also makes **~60 `meta/`
build scripts analysed for the first time**, and their own exports immediately become findings.

## Measured

Method: a throwaway `tsconfig.measure.json` (`{"extends":"./tsconfig.json","include":["src","meta"]}`)
at repo root, `ts-prune/lib/runner` invoked against it with `.ts-prunerc`'s own `ignore`, findings
passed through `meta/findDeadcode.cjs`'s own `parseFinding` / `excludeKnownParseArtifacts` /
`partitionFindings`, diffed against both committed baselines. Config deleted afterwards; tree
verified clean and the gate still green at `47 OK | 67 OK`.

| population | today | with `meta/` included | dissolved | newly exposed |
| ---------- | ----- | --------------------- | --------- | -------------- |
| used-in-module | 67 | **108** | 32 | **73** |
| unreachable | 47 | 45 | 3 | 1 |

Net **+41** on the ledger the todo exists to shrink.

The 73 newcomers are ordinary exports in scripts nothing has ever analysed —
`buildCrossoverIndex.ts` (5), `captureShellScrollback.ts` (7), `gen_vtables.ts` (5),
`genI18nGateScope.ts` (5), `graphifyCodeViz.ts` (5), `hardcodedStringGate.ts` (5+),
`buildDecompressWorkerDev.ts` (3), `downloadZig.ts` (2), and more.

**Widening is also not free to switch on.** `npx tsc -p` with `meta` included exits 2 with **12
type errors across 6 files** — `meta/hardcodedStringGate.ts`, `meta/machineFillGamelib.ts`,
`meta/pruneStaleHelperBinaries.ts`, `meta/rebrandUpstreamCatalogs.ts`,
`meta/__tests__/lintTranslations.test.ts`, `meta/__tests__/machineFillGamelib.test.ts`
(TS2339 ×4, TS2345 ×3, TS2322 ×2, TS2365, TS2352, TS1501). These are real: nothing typechecks
those scripts today.

Of the 4 parked traps, **3 dissolve** (`InvocationForm`, `DownloadedBinary`, `FakeHomeEnvKey`);
`DECOMPRESS_WORKER_ENTRY_PATH` correctly survives — it is a genuinely live re-export, not a
scope artifact.

**The conclusion to record: widening conflates two different goods.** Typecheck coverage over ~60
unchecked build scripts is worth having on its own merits. Ledger reduction is not what it
delivers — it delivers the opposite. They must be decided separately, and the dead-code todo is
the wrong home for the first one.
