**DD-2, what "half the height" means (request 2), derived from source.**
- *Current row (measured from source):* the row's font-size is `var(--text-sm)` = 16px / 1.2 = 13.33px, so its `padding-block` of `var(--space-sm)` (0.75em) is 10px per side. The tallest child is the Sign in / Reconnect `button is-footer`. `.button` (`_buttons.scss:11-25`) gives it font-size 16px, padding 0.75em (12px), line-height 17px and max-height 40px. Under App.css's global `box-sizing: border-box` that makes it 40px tall.
- *Two baselines:* `Settings/index.css:154-161` adds `margin-block: 8px` to `.button.is-footer`. That stylesheet belongs to the lazy Settings route chunk (`App.tsx:278`), so it applies only once Settings has been opened.
  - Fresh launch: one row is 1 + 10 + 40 + 10 + 1 = **62px**.
  - Once Settings has loaded: 1 + 10 + 8 + 40 + 8 + 10 + 1 = **78px**. This is most likely what the operator saw, because live-gate item 10 cycled themes through Settings.
- *Why halving only the row padding is not enough:* it gives 52px / 68px (84% / 87%), not half, because the button dominates the row.
- *Decision:* halve every vertical padding inside the banner and zero the leaked button margin.
  - Row `padding-block`: `var(--space-sm)` becomes `var(--space-2xs)` (0.375em, exactly half: 10px to 5px).
  - Footer button `padding-block`: becomes `var(--space-2xs)` (12px to 6px), so the button is 6 + 17 + 6 = 29px.
  - Footer button `margin-block`: `0`.
- *Result:* one row is 1 + 5 + 29 + 5 + 1 = **41px in both states**, which is 53% of 78 and 66% of 62. The icon (`--text-lg`, 19.2px) and the dismiss control (about 20px) stay shorter than the 29px button. As a side effect, the banner height stops jumping when Settings is first opened.
- *Unchanged:* inline padding (`var(--space-md-fixed)`), `margin-block-end`, gaps, colours and borders.

`filterChipRowPlacement.test.ts`, `librarySyncNoticeSource.test.ts`, `librarySignInRows.test.ts`
